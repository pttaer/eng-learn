# Passage
Today is Saturday. I go to the market with my mother. It is big and busy. There are many people. The market opens at six. We go there early.

I see fruit and vegetables. The apples are red. The bananas are yellow. Apples are two dollars. Bananas are one dollar. My mother buys rice, fish and eggs. I carry the bag. It is a good day. I like the market.

A woman sells flowers. They are pink and white. I want one flower. I ask, "How much is it?" She says, "It is one dollar." I say thank you. We go home at ten.

## Cold Read
- **Thesis**: A girl and her mother go to the market. They buy food, and the girl buys a flower.
- **Targets**: market, fruit, apples, rice, flower, dollar

### Comprehension Checks
- Q: Who goes to the market?
  A: The girl and her mother go to the market.
- Q: What colour are the bananas?
  A: The bananas are yellow.
- Q: How much is the flower?
  A: It is one dollar.

## Syntax Dissection
### Sentence 1
- **Sentence**: The apples are red.
- **Subject**: The apples
- **Verb**: are
- **Object**: none
- **Analysis**: The verb "to be" with a colour word. "Apples" is plural, so we use "are". (Danh từ số nhiều đi với "are".)

### Sentence 2
- **Sentence**: I go to the market with my mother.
- **Subject**: I
- **Verb**: go
- **Object**: none
- **Analysis**: "To the market" shows the place. "With my mother" shows who goes too. (Giới từ "to" chỉ nơi đến, "with" nghĩa là cùng với.)

### Sentence 3
- **Sentence**: How much is it?
- **Subject**: it
- **Verb**: is
- **Object**: none
- **Analysis**: A question about price. "How much" comes first, then "is", then "it". (Câu hỏi giá tiền: "How much" + is + danh từ/it.)

## Sentence Mining
### market
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈmɑː.kɪt/
- **Definition**: A place where people buy and sell food and other things.
- **Vietnamese**: chợ
- **Context**: I go to the market with my mother.
- **Collocations**: go to the market, a busy market, market day
- **Etymology**: From Latin mercatus (trade).

### vegetables
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈvedʒ.tə.bəlz/
- **Definition**: Plants that we eat, like carrots and beans.
- **Vietnamese**: rau củ, rau
- **Context**: I see fruit and vegetables.
- **Collocations**: fresh vegetables, eat vegetables, fruit and vegetables
- **Etymology**: From Latin vegetabilis (alive, growing).

### buy
- **Part of Speech**: verb
- **IPA**: /baɪ/
- **Definition**: To get something and give money for it.
- **Vietnamese**: mua
- **Context**: My mother buys rice, fish and eggs.
- **Collocations**: buy food, buy a bag, buy and sell
- **Etymology**: From Old English bycgan (to buy).

### flower
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈflaʊ.ər/
- **Definition**: The coloured part of a plant, often beautiful.
- **Vietnamese**: hoa, bông hoa
- **Context**: I want one flower.
- **Collocations**: a red flower, buy flowers, flower shop
- **Etymology**: From Latin flos (flower).

### dollar
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈdɒl.ər/

- **Vietnamese**: đô la

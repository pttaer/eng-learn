# Passage
Tom arrived in a new city last Monday. He wanted to find the museum, but he had no map. His phone was dead, so he could not use it. He asked a woman near the station for help. She was kind and friendly.

"Go straight, then turn left at the bank," she said. "The museum is next to the park." Tom thanked her and walked for ten minutes. But he turned right by mistake. He walked for a long time. Soon he was in a busy street with many shops and noisy cars. He did not know where he was.

He felt lost and a little worried. He sat down for a moment. Then he saw a policeman and asked again. The policeman smiled and showed him the way on a map. "It is behind you," he said. At last, Tom found the museum. The door was locked and a sign said closed. It was closed, so he had a coffee and went back to the hotel.

## Cold Read
- **Thesis**: A visitor with no map and a dead phone gets lost after a wrong turn, but a helpful policeman shows him the way.
- **Targets**: go straight, turn left, next to, by mistake, a little worried, at last

### Comprehension Checks
- Q: Why could Tom not use his phone?
  A: It was dead.
- Q: What mistake did Tom make?
  A: He turned right instead of left.
- Q: Was the museum open when Tom found it?
  A: No, it was closed.

## Syntax Dissection
### Sentence 1
- **Sentence**: He wanted to find the museum, but he had no map.
- **Subject**: He
- **Verb**: wanted to find
- **Object**: the museum
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Contrast Clause with "but"]: but he had no map | Shows the problem.
- **Analysis**: Two past simple clauses joined by "but". Ghi chú: "want to" + động từ nguyên mẫu = muốn làm gì.

### Sentence 2
- **Sentence**: His phone was dead, so he could not use it.
- **Subject**: His phone
- **Verb**: was
- **Object**: (none; "dead" is a complement)
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Result Clause with "so"]: so he could not use it | Shows the result of the dead phone.
- **Analysis**: Cause then result with "so". Ghi chú: "could not" là phủ định quá khứ của "can"; "dead" ở đây nghĩa là hết pin.

### Sentence 3
- **Sentence**: "Go straight, then turn left at the bank," she said.
- **Subject**: she
- **Verb**: said
- **Object**: "Go straight, then turn left at the bank"
- **Analysis**: The woman speaks in imperatives to give directions. Ghi chú: "Go straight" = đi thẳng, "turn left" = rẽ trái.

### Sentence 4
- **Sentence**: The door was locked and a sign said closed. It was closed, so he had a coffee and went back to the hotel.
- **Subject**: he
- **Verb**: had, went
- **Object**: a coffee
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Result Clause with "so"]: so he had a coffee and went back to the hotel | Tom's choice after finding the museum closed.
- **Analysis**: One cause ("It was closed") and two actions in a row. Ghi chú: "had" và "went" là quá khứ bất quy tắc của "have" và "go".

## Sentence Mining
### map
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /mæp/
- **Definition**: A drawing that shows roads, cities and places.
- **Vietnamese**: bản đồ
- **Context**: He wanted to find the museum, but he had no map.
- **Collocations**: city map, read a map, on a map
- **Etymology**: Latin mappa (cloth, napkin).

### station
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈsteɪ.ʃən/
- **Definition**: A place where trains or buses stop.
- **Vietnamese**: nhà ga, trạm
- **Context**: He asked a woman near the station for help. She was kind and friendly.
- **Collocations**: train station, bus station, near the station
- **Etymology**: Latin statio (a standing still).

### straight
- **Part of Speech**: adverb
- **IPA**: /streɪt/
- **Definition**: In one direction without turning.
- **Vietnamese**: thẳng
- **Context**: "Go straight, then turn left at the bank," she said.
- **Collocations**: go straight, straight on, straight ahead
- **Etymology**: Old English streht (stretched).

### mistake
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /mɪˈsteɪk/
- **Definition**: Something that is wrong; an error.
- **Vietnamese**: lỗi, sai lầm
- **Context**: But he turned right by mistake.
- **Collocations**: by mistake, make a mistake, big mistake
- **Etymology**: Old Norse mistaka (to take wrongly).

### worried
- **Part of Speech**: adjective
- **IPA**: /ˈwʌr.id/
- **Definition**: Unhappy because you think something bad may happen.
- **Vietnamese**: lo lắng
- **Context**: He felt lost and a little worried.
- **Collocations**: a little worried, worried about, look worried
- **Etymology**: From the verb worry, Old English wyrgan (to strangle).

### policeman
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /pəˈliːs.mən/
- **Definition**: A man who works for the police and helps keep people safe.
- **Vietnamese**: cảnh sát (nam), chú công an
- **Context**: He sat down for a moment. Then he saw a policeman and asked again.
- **Collocations**: ask a policeman, a friendly policeman, call the police
- **Etymology**: police (from Greek polis, city) + man.

# Passage
Working from home has become much more common in recent years. Many people say that it has changed their lives for the better, and I can understand why. If you work from home, you save time and money because you do not have to travel to an office every day. You can also organise your day in the way that suits you best.

However, I think working from home has some problems too. When your home is also your office, it is difficult to stop working in the evening. Some people have told me that they feel lonely because they rarely see their colleagues. If I worked at home every day, I would probably miss the chats at lunchtime and the small jokes during the day.

In my opinion, the best solution is a mix of both. If companies allowed their staff to work from home two or three days a week, people could enjoy the freedom of home and still meet their team. This is better than choosing only one way, because every person is different and every job has different needs.

I have tried both ways, and I have learned one important thing. Whatever you choose, you should make a clear plan for your day. If you decide when to start and when to finish, you will feel less stressed and get more done. It sounds simple, but it really helps.

## Cold Read
- **Thesis**: Working from home saves time and gives freedom, but it can cause loneliness and long hours, so a mix of home and office work is the best choice.
- **Targets**: recent years, save time and money, organise your day, rarely see, in my opinion, a mix of both, make a plan

### Comprehension Checks

- Q: What problems does the writer mention?
  A: It is hard to stop working in the evening, and some people feel lonely because they rarely see their colleagues.
- Q: What does the writer suggest as the best solution?
  A: Working from home two or three days a week and spending the other days at the office with the team.

## Syntax Dissection
### Sentence 1
- **Sentence**: Working from home has become much more common in recent years.
- **Subject**: Working from home
- **Verb**: has become
- **Object**: (none; "much more common" is the complement)
- **Analysis**: The present perfect ("has become") links a change in the past with the situation now. The gerund phrase "Working from home" is the subject, and "in recent years" shows the time period.

### Sentence 2
- **Sentence**: If you work from home, you save time and money because you do not have to travel to an office every day.
- **Subject**: you
- **Verb**: save
- **Object**: time and money
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Conditional Clause]: If you work from home | Gives the condition for the result in the main clause.
  - [Reason Clause]: because you do not have to travel to an office every day | Explains why you save time and money.
- **Analysis**: A conditional structure in the present simple, followed by a "because" clause that gives the reason. "Do not have to" means it is not necessary.

### Sentence 3
- **Sentence**: If I worked at home every day, I would probably miss the chats at lunchtime.
- **Subject**: I
- **Verb**: would miss
- **Object**: the chats at lunchtime
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Second Conditional Clause]: If I worked at home every day | Describes an unreal or unlikely situation, so the past simple is used.
- **Analysis**: The second conditional ("If + past simple, would + verb") talks about an imaginary situation. The adverb "probably" sits between "would" and the main verb.

### Sentence 4
- **Sentence**: If you decide when to start and when to finish, you will feel less stressed and get more done.
- **Subject**: you
- **Verb**: will feel, (will) get
- **Object**: (none; "less stressed" is the complement, "more" is the object of "get")
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Conditional Clause]: If you decide when to start and when to finish | The condition; it contains two "when" phrases joined by "and".
- **Analysis**: A first conditional with two results joined by "and". The repeated "when to ..." pattern keeps the advice clear and balanced.

## Sentence Mining

- **Collocations**: organise your time, organise a meeting, organise your work

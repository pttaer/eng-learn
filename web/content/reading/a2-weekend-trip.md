# Passage
Last weekend, my friend Anna and I went to the seaside. We left home early on Saturday morning because we wanted to avoid the traffic. The train was cheap, so we bought two tickets online. When we arrived, the weather was sunny and warm. We walked along the beach and took many photos. The sea looked blue and beautiful.

At lunch, we ate fish and chips in a small cafe near the harbour. Later, we swam in the sea, but the water was very cold. In the afternoon, it started to rain, so we visited a museum. It was interesting, and the tickets were free. We learned about old boats and the people who worked on them.

In the evening, we were tired but happy. We sat on a bench and watched the sun go down. The train home was late, but we did not mind. Next month, we are going to visit the mountains. I think it will be another great trip.

## Cold Read
- **Thesis**: Two friends have a cheap, enjoyable weekend at the seaside, and the good experience makes them plan another trip.
- **Targets**: avoid the traffic, bought tickets, along the beach, in the afternoon, did not mind, are going to visit

### Comprehension Checks
- Q: Why did they leave home early on Saturday?
  A: They wanted to avoid the traffic.
- Q: What did they do when it started to rain?
  A: They visited a museum, and the tickets were free.
- Q: What are they going to do next month?
  A: They are going to visit the mountains.

## Syntax Dissection
### Sentence 1
- **Sentence**: We left home early on Saturday morning because we wanted to avoid the traffic.
- **Subject**: We
- **Verb**: left
- **Object**: home
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Reason Clause]: because we wanted to avoid the traffic | Explains why they left early.
- **Analysis**: Simple past tense plus a "because" clause that gives the reason. Ghi chú: "because" + mệnh đề = vì, bởi vì.

### Sentence 2
- **Sentence**: The train was cheap, so we bought two tickets online.
- **Subject**: The train
- **Verb**: was
- **Object**: (none; "cheap" is a complement)
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Result Clause with "so"]: so we bought two tickets online | Shows the result of the cheap price.
- **Analysis**: Two short clauses joined by "so" (cause, then result). Ghi chú: "so" = nên, vì vậy; "bought" là quá khứ bất quy tắc của "buy".

### Sentence 3
- **Sentence**: We swam in the sea, but the water was very cold.
- **Subject**: We
- **Verb**: swam
- **Object**: (none)
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Contrast Clause with "but"]: but the water was very cold | Gives a negative detail.
- **Analysis**: Two clauses joined by "but" to show contrast. Ghi chú: "swam" là quá khứ bất quy tắc của "swim".

### Sentence 4
- **Sentence**: Next month, we are going to visit the mountains.
- **Subject**: we
- **Verb**: are going to visit
- **Object**: the mountains
- **Analysis**: "be going to" + động từ nguyên mẫu diễn tả kế hoạch trong tương lai. The time phrase "Next month" comes first.

## Sentence Mining

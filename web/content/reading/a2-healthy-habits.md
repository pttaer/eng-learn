# Passage
Good habits make life better. You should drink a lot of water every day. It is also important to eat fruit and vegetables. Many people eat too much sugar, and it is bad for your health. Try to cook at home because it is cheaper and healthier. Fast food is quick, but it is not always good for you.

Exercise is important too, but you do not need an expensive gym. You can walk to work or ride a bike. Last year, I started to run twice a week. At first it was difficult, and I often felt tired. Now I feel stronger and much happier. My friends say that I look better too.

Sleep is also very important. Most adults need about eight hours every night. You should not use your phone just before bed. If you are sick, you must rest and see a doctor. Do not worry about big changes. Small changes can help you live a long and healthy life. Start today, and you will feel the difference soon.

## Cold Read
- **Thesis**: Simple daily habits such as drinking water, eating well, exercising and sleeping enough can make you healthier and happier.
- **Targets**: a lot of water, too much sugar, twice a week, at first, see a doctor, healthy life

### Comprehension Checks
- Q: What should you drink every day?
  A: You should drink a lot of water.
- Q: How often did the writer start to run?
  A: Twice a week.
- Q: What must you do if you are sick?
  A: You must rest and see a doctor.

## Syntax Dissection
### Sentence 1
- **Sentence**: You should drink a lot of water every day.
- **Subject**: You
- **Verb**: should drink
- **Object**: a lot of water
- **Analysis**: "should" + động từ nguyên mẫu dùng để đưa ra lời khuyên. "Every day" is a time phrase at the end.

### Sentence 2
- **Sentence**: Many people eat too much sugar, and it is bad for your health.
- **Subject**: Many people
- **Verb**: eat
- **Object**: too much sugar
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Coordinated Clause with "and"]: and it is bad for your health | Adds an opinion about eating sugar.
- **Analysis**: Present simple for general facts. Ghi chú: "too much" + danh từ không đếm được = quá nhiều.

### Sentence 3
- **Sentence**: Try to cook at home because it is cheaper and healthier. Fast food is quick, but it is not always good for you.
- **Subject**: (you, understood)
- **Verb**: Try to cook
- **Object**: (none)
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Reason Clause]: because it is cheaper and healthier | Gives two reasons using comparatives.
- **Analysis**: An imperative sentence (advice) with a "because" clause. Ghi chú: "cheaper", "healthier" là so sánh hơn.

### Sentence 4
- **Sentence**: If you are sick, you must rest and see a doctor.
- **Subject**: you
- **Verb**: must rest and see
- **Object**: a doctor
- **Subordinate Clauses**:
  - [Condition Clause]: If you are sick | Sets the condition for the advice.
- **Analysis**: "If" + present simple, then "must" + verb. Ghi chú: "must" diễn tả điều bắt buộc hoặc rất cần thiết.

## Sentence Mining
### habit
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈhæb.ɪt/
- **Definition**: Something you do often or regularly.
- **Vietnamese**: thói quen
- **Context**: Good habits make life better.
- **Collocations**: good habit, bad habit, healthy habit
- **Etymology**: Latin habitus (condition, way of being).

### vegetable
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈvedʒ.tə.bəl/
- **Definition**: A plant that you eat, such as a carrot or an onion.
- **Vietnamese**: rau, rau củ
- **Context**: It is also important to eat fruit and vegetables.
- **Collocations**: fresh vegetables, eat vegetables, vegetable soup
- **Etymology**: Latin vegetabilis (able to grow).

### sugar
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈʃʊɡ.ər/
- **Definition**: A sweet white or brown food used in drinks and cakes.
- **Vietnamese**: đường
- **Context**: Many people eat too much sugar.
- **Collocations**: too much sugar, a spoon of sugar, sugar in tea
- **Etymology**: From Arabic sukkar, through Italian and French.

### exercise
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈek.sə.saɪz/
- **Definition**: Physical activity that keeps your body strong and healthy.
- **Vietnamese**: sự tập thể dục, bài tập thể dục
- **Context**: Exercise is important too, but you do not need an expensive gym.
- **Collocations**: do exercise, regular exercise, take exercise
- **Etymology**: Latin exercere (to train, keep busy).

### sleep
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /sliːp/
- **Definition**: The natural rest of your body and mind, usually at night.
- **Vietnamese**: giấc ngủ
- **Context**: Sleep is also very important.
- **Collocations**: get enough sleep, a good night of sleep, go to sleep
- **Etymology**: Old English slaep.

### rest
- **Part of Speech**: verb
- **IPA**: /rest/
- **Definition**: To stop working or being active so your body can feel better.
- **Vietnamese**: nghỉ ngơi
- **Context**: If you are sick, you must rest and see a doctor.
- **Collocations**: rest at home, rest for a day, need to rest
- **Etymology**: Old English rest (repose).

## Synthesis
- **Model Précis**: The writer gives simple advice for a healthy life. You should drink water, eat fruit and vegetables, and avoid too much sugar. You should also exercise, sleep about eight hours, and see a doctor when you are sick.
- **Incorporated Vocabulary**: habit, vegetable, sugar, exercise, sleep, rest
- **Syntactic Reconstruction**: You should eat more vegetables and less sugar because good habits keep you healthy.

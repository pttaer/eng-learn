# Passage
I have a small family. There are five people. My father is a driver. My mother is a nurse. I have one brother and one sister. We live in a small house.

My brother is ten. He likes football. My sister is five. She likes cats. My grandmother lives with us. She is old and happy. Her name is Hoa. She makes tea every morning.

We have dinner together every evening. My mother makes soup. My father tells funny stories. We all laugh. After dinner, we watch TV. I love my family. I am happy at home. We are a happy family.

## Cold Read
- **Thesis**: This is a small family of five people. They live together and they are happy.
- **Targets**: family, father, mother, brother, sister, grandmother

### Comprehension Checks
- Q: How many people are in the family?
  A: There are five people.
- Q: What is the father's job?
  A: He is a driver.
- Q: What does the grandmother do every morning?
  A: She makes tea.

## Syntax Dissection
### Sentence 1
- **Sentence**: There are five people.
- **Subject**: five people
- **Verb**: are
- **Object**: none
- **Analysis**: "There are" tells us what exists. We use "are" with many people. (Cấu trúc "There are" + danh từ số nhiều: có ... người/vật.)

### Sentence 2
- **Sentence**: My father is a driver.
- **Subject**: My father
- **Verb**: is
- **Object**: a driver
- **Analysis**: The verb "to be" tells us about a person. We use "a" before a job. (Dùng "a" trước tên nghề nghiệp.)

### Sentence 3
- **Sentence**: She likes cats.
- **Subject**: She
- **Verb**: likes
- **Object**: cats
- **Analysis**: Simple present tense. "She" takes -s: like becomes likes. (Với "she" động từ thêm -s.)

## Sentence Mining
### family
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈfæm.əl.i/
- **Definition**: A group of people who are related, like a mother, a father and children.
- **Vietnamese**: gia đình
- **Context**: I have a small family.
- **Collocations**: a small family, a big family, family dinner
- **Etymology**: From Latin familia (the people of a house).

### grandmother
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /ˈɡræn.mʌð.ər/
- **Definition**: The mother of your mother or your father.
- **Vietnamese**: bà (bà nội, bà ngoại)
- **Context**: My grandmother lives with us.
- **Collocations**: my grandmother, visit your grandmother, grandmother and grandfather
- **Etymology**: From grand + mother.

### nurse
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /nɜːs/
- **Definition**: A person who helps sick people in a hospital.
- **Vietnamese**: y tá
- **Context**: My mother is a nurse.
- **Collocations**: a nurse, hospital nurse, nurse and doctor
- **Etymology**: From Old French norrice (a woman who feeds a baby).

### soup
- **Part of Speech**: noun
- **IPA**: /suːp/
- **Definition**: A hot food made with water, meat or vegetables.
- **Vietnamese**: súp, canh
- **Context**: My mother makes soup.
- **Collocations**: make soup, hot soup, chicken soup
- **Etymology**: From Old French soupe (bread in broth).

### happy
- **Part of Speech**: adjective
- **IPA**: /ˈhæp.i/
- **Definition**: Feeling good and pleased.
- **Vietnamese**: vui, hạnh phúc
- **Context**: I am happy at home. We are a happy family.
- **Collocations**: very happy, happy family, happy birthday
- **Etymology**: From Old Norse happ (luck).

## Synthesis
- **Model Précis**: A girl has a small family of five people. Her parents work, her brother and sister like to play, and her grandmother makes tea. They eat dinner together and they are happy.
- **Incorporated Vocabulary**: family, grandmother, nurse, soup, happy
- **Syntactic Reconstruction**: My mother is a nurse, and she makes soup.
- **Copywork Prompt**: Write the sentence "My father is a driver." Then write one sentence about your father or mother.

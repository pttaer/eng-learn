# Mode: Particle Lab (A2 Phrasal Verbs)

## get up
- **ID**: vocab-a2-pl-1
- **Mode**: PARTICLE_LAB
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Word**: get up
- **IPA**: /ɡet ʌp/
- **Definition**: Leave your bed after you wake up.
- **Vietnamese**: thức dậy, rời khỏi giường
- **Context**: I get up at six o'clock every morning.
- **Verb**: get (become, move to a new state)
- **Particle**: UP
- **Semantic Archetype**: Upward movement & rising
- **Particle Logic**: UP shows a move from a low position, like lying down, to a higher one.
- **Remind Candidate**: false

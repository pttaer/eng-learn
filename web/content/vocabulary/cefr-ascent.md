## mitigate
- **ID**: vocab-cefr-1
- **Mode**: CEFR_ASCENT
- **Level**: 1
- **Word**: mitigate
- **IPA**: /ˈmɪt.ɪ.ɡeɪt/

- **Vietnamese**: giảm nhẹ, xoa dịu tác động xấu

- **Collocates**: mitigate risks, mitigate impact, mitigate climate change
- **Synonyms**: alleviate, attenuate, diminish
- **Register**: Formal Business / Technical
- **Remind Candidate**: false

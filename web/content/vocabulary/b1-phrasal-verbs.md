# Mode: Particle Lab (B1 Phrasal Verbs)

## carry out
- **ID**: vocab-b1-pl-1
- **Mode**: PARTICLE_LAB
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Word**: carry out
- **IPA**: /ˈkær.i aʊt/
- **Definition**: Do or complete a piece of work, a plan or a test.
- **Vietnamese**: thực hiện, tiến hành
- **Context**: The team will carry out a survey of local shops next week.
- **Verb**: carry (take from place to place)
- **Particle**: OUT
- **Semantic Archetype**: Completion & delivery
- **Particle Logic**: OUT shows the action moves from plan to a finished, real result.
- **Remind Candidate**: false

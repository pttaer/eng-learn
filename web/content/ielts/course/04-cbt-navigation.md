# Module IV: Computer-Based Exam Mastery

## Section 23: CBT Interface Navigation & Exam Logistics

### The Modern Computer-Delivered Reality
The Computer-Delivered IELTS (CBT) utilizes a standardized testing workstation environment. The cognitive and pedagogical difficulty is identical to the paper test, but tactical performance depends on digital ergonomics, keyboard fluency, and interface navigation efficiency.

### Core Ergonomics & Hotkey Shortcuts

#### 1. Text Highlighting & Annotations
- **Highlight Function**: Highlight any word or sentence in the passage pane by selecting the text, right-clicking, and choosing **Highlight**. Highlights remain visible throughout the test section.
- **Notes Function**: Select text, right-click, and choose **Notes** to open a scratchpad text box attached to the highlighted text.
- **Clearing Highlights**: Right-click highlighted text and select **Clear** (or **Clear All** to reset the passage).
- **Tactical Rule**: Highlight only 1–2 key nouns per paragraph. Over-highlighting clutters the visual field and slows down scanning.

#### 2. Keyboard & Input Shortcuts
- **Tab Key**: In form completion and tables, press `Tab` to jump immediately to the next input field without reaching for the mouse.
- **Shift + Tab**: Navigates backwards to the preceding input field.
- **Copy & Paste (`Ctrl + C` / `Ctrl + V`)**: Supported within the Writing module and Reading inputs.
  - In Reading fill-in-the-blank questions, candidates can copy the exact word from the passage and paste it into the blank to guarantee 100% spelling accuracy.
- **Arrow Keys**: Move cursor across text fields or scroll through active panes.

#### 3. Visual Customization & Display Settings
- **Font Sizing**: Candidates can toggle between Standard, Large, and Extra Large font sizes via the Settings menu in the top-right corner.
- **Contrast Themes**: The interface provides Cambridge contrast schemes:
  - *Standard*: Black text on white background.
  - *Inverted*: White text on black background (reduces glare and eye fatigue).
  - *High Contrast*: Yellow text on black background.
- **Split-Screen Resizer**: Hover over the vertical boundary separating the passage and question panes. Drag left or right to allocate more screen real estate to the passage or the questions.

### Module-Specific CBT Tactics

#### Listening in CBT
- **Headphone Volume**: Test audio via the volume slider on the pre-test sound-check screen. Adjust volume at any time during the test using the master volume slider at the top right.
- **Direct Entry**: Type answers directly into text fields as the audio plays.
- **Review Period (2 Minutes)**: Unlike the paper test's 10-minute transfer window, CBT provides only **2 minutes** at the conclusion of Section 4 to check spellings and review flagged items.
- **Bottom Navigator Bar**: Questions with entered answers appear with a solid square; unanswered questions appear outlined. Flag ambiguous questions using the **Review** button to revisit during the final 2 minutes.

#### Reading in CBT
- **Split-Screen Ergonomics**: The passage remains on the left; questions appear on the right. Both panes scroll independently.
- **Drag-and-Drop Questions**: In summary and classification tasks with a word bank, candidates drag answer tiles from the bank directly into sentence blanks.
- **Direct Highlighting Strategy**: Highlight the first sentence of each paragraph during initial skimming to construct a mental map of paragraph themes.

#### Writing in CBT
- **Automatic Word Counter**: Displayed dynamically beneath each text box. No manual word counting is required.
  - Target Task 1: 170–190 words (minimum 150 words).
  - Target Task 2: 270–300 words (minimum 250 words).
- **Editing Speed**: Restructuring paragraphs via cut-and-paste allows candidates to spend more time planning (5 minutes) and revising (5 minutes).
- **Auto-Correction Warning**: The CBT interface has **no spellchecker or grammar checker**. Misspelled words are marked as errors.

### One Skill Retake (OSR) Regulatory Framework & Strategy

#### 1. Official Conditions for OSR Eligibility
- **Modality**: Candidate must have taken the full four-skill test in a certified Computer-Delivered test center (OSR is not available for paper-based tests).
- **Time Window**: The single-skill retake must be sat within **60 days** of the original test date.
- **Frequency**: Candidates may sit only **one** skill retake per primary test sitting.
- **Documentation**: Candidates receive an updated Test Report Form (TRF) showing the improved score alongside original scores from the other three components.

#### 2. Strategic Retake Decision Matrix

| Scenario | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **L: 8.0, R: 8.5, S: 7.5, W: 6.0** (Target: 7.0 each) | **Sit OSR Writing Immediately** | Highly targeted single-skill preparation; high likelihood of boosting Writing by 0.5–1.0 within 3–4 weeks without fatiguing other skills. |
| **L: 6.0, R: 6.0, S: 6.5, W: 6.0** (Target: 7.0 each) | **Do NOT Sit OSR; Retake Full Test** | Deficits span multiple skills; general English proficiency requires holistic uplift across all four components. |
| **L: 7.0, R: 6.5, S: 7.0, W: 7.0** (Target: 7.0 each) | **Sit OSR Reading** | Reading raw score deficit is narrow (typically 2–3 questions); 4 weeks of 14-question-type tactical drills will reliably achieve Band 7.0+. |

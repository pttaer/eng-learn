# Mode: Inversion & Emphasis (C1/C2)

> [!TIP]
> **Sắc thái & Bối cảnh sử dụng (Register & Communicative Context)**: Đảo ngữ (Inversion) và câu chẻ (Cleft sentences) là vũ khí cú pháp tạo điểm nhấn trang trọng, kịch tính trong văn học thuật (IELTS Task 2, C1/C2 Academic Essay) hoặc báo chí phân tích.
> **Cảnh báo lạm dụng (Overuse Trap)**: Chỉ nên dùng 1-2 lần trong toàn bộ bài viết tại các luận điểm then chốt. Ép đảo ngữ liên tục sẽ khiến bài viết thiếu tự nhiên, gượng gạo và phản tác dụng.

## Negative Inversion with 'Seldom'
- **ID**: gram-inv-1
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The executive team seldom realized how vulnerable their supply chain had become.
- **Transformation**: Seldom did the executive team realize how vulnerable their supply chain had become.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Fronting the negative frequency adverb 'seldom' triggers subject-auxiliary inversion.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với Seldom: đưa trạng từ phủ định lên đầu câu, đảo trợ động từ 'did' lên trước chủ ngữ.
- **Formula**: `Seldom + Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb (Base Form)`
- **Analysis**: When negative frequency adverbs ('seldom', 'rarely', 'scarcely') are moved to the sentence-initial position for rhetorical emphasis, English requires standard subject-auxiliary inversion in the main clause.
- **Exemplar**: Seldom did early astronomers observe such sudden changes in planetary brightness without subsequent solar flare activity.

## Negative Inversion with 'Rarely'
- **ID**: gram-inv-2
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Central banks rarely adjust benchmark lending rates without prior forward guidance.
- **Transformation**: Rarely do central banks adjust benchmark lending rates without prior forward guidance.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Fronting 'rarely' requires present auxiliary 'do' before plural subject 'central banks'.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với Rarely: đưa 'Rarely' lên đầu câu, mượn trợ động từ 'do' đặt trước danh từ số nhiều.
- **Formula**: `Rarely + do/does + Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: In present simple declarative statements with full lexical verbs, fronting 'rarely' obligates the insertion of 'do' or 'does' immediately preceding the noun phrase subject.
- **Exemplar**: Rarely do early-stage biotech startups achieve profitability prior to Phase III clinical trials.

## Correlative Inversion with 'Hardly... when'
- **ID**: gram-inv-3
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The lead engineer had hardly deployed the hotfix when the secondary database crashed.
- **Transformation**: Hardly had the lead engineer deployed the hotfix when the secondary database crashed.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Negative time adverb 'hardly' at the front inverts the past perfect auxiliary 'had'.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với cấu trúc Vừa mới... thì (Hardly had + S + V3 when S + V2).
- **Formula**: `Hardly + had + Subject + Past Participle... + when + Subject + Simple Past Verb`
- **Analysis**: Used to describe an event immediately followed by another. The first past event requires past perfect inverted syntax ('Hardly had [subject] [participle]'), followed by the subordinate time conjunction 'when'.
- **Exemplar**: Hardly had the research vessel dropped anchor in the archipelago when unexpected gale-force winds forced a change in course.

## Conditional Inversion Type 1 ('Should...')
- **ID**: gram-inv-4
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: If you encounter any discrepancies in the audit log, notify security immediately.
- **Transformation**: Should you encounter any discrepancies in the audit log, notify security immediately.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Omit 'if' and invert modal auxiliary 'should' to the front in conditional clauses.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ câu điều kiện loại 1: Bỏ 'if', đảo 'Should' lên trước chủ ngữ để tăng tính trang trọng.
- **Formula**: `Should + Subject + Base Verb..., [Imperative or Future Main Clause]`
- **Analysis**: In formal and business English, replacing 'if + subject + present verb' with initial modal 'should' conveys high professional decorum without altering conditional truth semantics.
- **Exemplar**: Should field investigators detect anomalous seismic signals near the dormant caldera, the geological observatory alerts civil protection teams without delay.

## It-Cleft Sentence for Subject Focus
- **ID**: gram-inv-5
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: Systemic misalignment of economic incentives caused the subprime mortgage meltdown.
- **Transformation**: It was systemic misalignment of economic incentives that caused the subprime mortgage meltdown.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Wrap focal subject inside 'It was [Subject] that [Predicate]' cleft syntax.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu chẻ (It-cleft): Nhấn mạnh đích danh chủ ngữ gây ra hành động bằng cấu trúc 'It was X that...'
- **Formula**: `It + is/was + Focused Element + that/who + Remaining Proposition`
- **Analysis**: An it-cleft isolates the focal constituent (the subject) into the predicate of an introductory copula clause, explicitly contrasting it against competing alternative causes.
- **Exemplar**: It was structural masonry failure rather than foundation subsidence that precipitated the historic aqueduct's partial collapse.

## Negative Inversion with 'Not only... but also'
- **ID**: gram-inv-6
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The new framework not only halved query latencies, but it also reduced server energy consumption.
- **Transformation**: Not only did the new framework halve query latencies, but it also reduced server energy consumption.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Fronting 'Not only' forces auxiliary inversion in the first coordinate clause.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'Not only': Đảo trợ động từ ở vế đầu; vế sau giữ nguyên trật tự thông thường.
- **Formula**: `Not only + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb..., but [Subject] also + Verb`
- **Analysis**: When 'not only' begins the sentence, the first clause undergoes obligatory inversion ('did the framework halve'), while the second coordinate clause introduced by 'but' retains normal canonical declarative order.
- **Exemplar**: Not only did the court overturn the administrative injunction, but it also assessed punitive damages against the petitioner.

## Conditional Inversion Type 2 ('Were...')
- **ID**: gram-inv-7
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: If the consortium were to withdraw funding, the infrastructure project would stall indefinitely.
- **Transformation**: Were the consortium to withdraw funding, the infrastructure project would stall indefinitely.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Omit 'if' and invert subjunctive copula 'were' with infinitive complement.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ câu điều kiện loại 2: Đảo 'Were' lên đầu câu thay cho 'If', động từ chính chuyển thành 'to-V'. Cấu trúc này làm câu văn trang trọng hơn và ngụ ý rằng khả năng điều này xảy ra là rất thấp.
- **Formula**: `Were + Subject + to-Infinitive..., Subject + would/could + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: Omitting the conditional subordinator 'if' and advancing subjunctive 'were' creates an elevated hypothetical proposition indicating that the event is unlikely to happen.
- **Exemplar**: Were international donors to withhold emergency funding, regional biodiversity preservation programs would halt within months.

## Wh-Cleft / Pseudo-Cleft with 'What...'
- **ID**: gram-inv-8
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: We fundamentally need an impartial oversight committee with statutory subpoena power.
- **Transformation**: What we fundamentally need is an impartial oversight committee with statutory subpoena power.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Convert sentence to pseudo-cleft with relative free clause 'What we need is...'.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu chẻ giả (Wh-cleft / Pseudo-cleft): Đưa mệnh đề 'What...' lên đầu để dồn toàn bộ trọng tâm sang vị ngữ.
- **Formula**: `What + Subject + Verb... + is/was + Focal Noun Phrase / Infinitive Phrase`
- **Analysis**: A pseudo-cleft establishes a presupposition in the subject relative clause ('What we need'), reserving the informational climax for the complement following the copular verb 'is'.
- **Exemplar**: What documentary filmmakers seek during extended field expeditions is patience and genuine rapport with local communities.

## Prepositional Phrase Fronting with Full Inversion
- **ID**: gram-inv-9
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: A massive archive of encrypted state correspondence lay beneath the limestone citadel.
- **Transformation**: Beneath the limestone citadel lay a massive archive of encrypted state correspondence.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Front spatial prepositional phrase; invert full lexical intransitive verb before subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ toàn bộ với cụm giới từ chỉ nơi chốn: Đảo nguyên động từ chính ra trước chủ ngữ dài.
- **Formula**: `Prepositional Locative Phrase + Intransitive Verb (lay/stood/sat) + Subject`
- **Analysis**: In descriptive literary or historical prose, fronting a locative adjunct triggers full inversion (not auxiliary inversion) when the verb is an intransitive verb of posture or motion, resolving end-weight on a lengthy subject.
- **Exemplar**: Across the negotiating table sat three veteran diplomats determined to resist territorial concessions.

## Inversion with 'Under no circumstances'
- **ID**: gram-inv-10
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Employees must not disclose unreleased fiscal projections to third-party analysts under any circumstances.
- **Transformation**: Under no circumstances must employees disclose unreleased fiscal projections to third-party analysts.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Negative prepositional phrase 'Under no circumstances' fronted with modal inversion.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'Under no circumstances': Tuyệt đối trong bất kỳ hoàn cảnh nào cũng không được làm gì.
- **Formula**: `Under no circumstances + Modal Auxiliary (must/should/may) + Subject + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: Categorical negative adjuncts fronted for legal or regulatory imperative force modal inversion ('must employees disclose'), leaving no semantic ambiguity regarding strict compliance.
- **Exemplar**: Under no circumstances should production credentials be committed to public code repositories.

## Conditional Inversion Type 3 ('Had...')
- **ID**: gram-inv-11
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: If the intelligence analysts had synthesized the intercepted telemetry earlier, the attack might have been averted.
- **Transformation**: Had the intelligence analysts synthesized the intercepted telemetry earlier, the attack might have been averted.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Omit 'if' and invert past perfect auxiliary 'had' before complex subject noun phrase.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ câu điều kiện loại 3: Bỏ 'if', đảo 'Had' lên đầu câu trước chủ ngữ để diễn tả giả định quá khứ.
- **Formula**: `Had + Subject + Past Participle..., Subject + would/might/could have + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: Third conditional inversion ('Had [subject] [participle]') eliminates conversational 'if', establishing a sharp counterfactual premise characteristic of rigorous academic historiography and legal analysis.
- **Exemplar**: Had the conservation team deployed acoustic monitoring sensors across the canopy earlier, the endangered gibbon population might have been documented far sooner.

## Negative Inversion with 'Not until...'
- **ID**: gram-inv-12
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The public did not understand the systemic ecological ramifications of microplastics until peer-reviewed longitudinal studies emerged.
- **Transformation**: Not until peer-reviewed longitudinal studies emerged did the public understand the systemic ecological ramifications of microplastics.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Front 'Not until [time clause]'; invert auxiliary 'did' in the subsequent main clause.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'Not until': Mệnh đề sau 'Not until' giữ nguyên, chỉ đảo trợ động từ ở mệnh đề chính.
- **Formula**: `Not until + Subordinate Clause/Time Expression + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: A common pitfall is attempting to invert the subordinate time clause. Correct grammar leaves the 'until' clause uninverted and applies auxiliary inversion exclusively to the governing matrix clause ('did the public understand').
- **Exemplar**: Not until high-resolution satellite imagery became publicly available did glaciologists realize how quickly the ice shelf had fractured.

## Degree Inversion with 'So... that'
- **ID**: gram-inv-13
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The macroeconomic shock was so severe that sovereign bond yields plunged to historic lows.
- **Transformation**: So severe was the macroeconomic shock that sovereign bond yields plunged to historic lows.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Front intensive adjective phrase 'So severe'; invert copular verb 'was' before subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'So... that': Đưa 'So + Adj' lên đầu câu, đảo động từ to-be lên trước chủ ngữ.
- **Formula**: `So + Adjective + be + Subject + that + Result Clause`
- **Analysis**: Fronting the degree adverb 'so' alongside its predicate adjective shifts emotional intensity to the forefront of the sentence, requiring inversion of the copula before introducing the consequential 'that' clause.
- **Exemplar**: So entrenched was the bureaucratic inertia that three successive cabinet reshuffles failed to enforce reform.

## All-Cleft
- **ID**: gram-inv-14
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The founder simply requested a fair arbitration hearing before an independent magistrate.
- **Transformation**: All the founder requested was a fair arbitration hearing before an independent magistrate.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Structure sentence with restrictive universal cleft 'All [Subject] [Verb] was [Noun Phrase]'.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu chẻ với 'All': Nhấn mạnh sự khiêm tốn hoặc duy nhất của một yêu cầu/hành động.
- **Formula**: `All + [Subject + Verb] + was/is + Focal Element`
- **Analysis**: An all-cleft acts as a restrictive scalar focus device, downplaying extraneous desires and spotlighting a singular non-negotiable objective as the sole item under consideration.
- **Exemplar**: All the dissident scientists demanded was an unredacted publication of the clinical trial datasets.

## Fronted Participial Phrase with Subject-Verb Inversion
- **ID**: gram-inv-15
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The fundamental question of algorithmic sovereignty lies embedded within these dense legislative amendments.
- **Transformation**: Embedded within these dense legislative amendments lies the fundamental question of algorithmic sovereignty.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Front participle phrase 'Embedded within...'; invert unaccusative verb 'lies' before subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với phân từ quá khứ (Participle Fronting): Đưa phân từ chỉ trạng thái lên đầu, đảo động từ.
- **Formula**: `Past Participle Phrase + Verb (lies/stands/comes) + Heavy Noun Phrase Subject`
- **Analysis**: Participle inversion achieves graceful syntactic cohesion by linking backwards to prior context via the participle phrase while reserving the end-position for a heavy, conceptually novel noun phrase.
- **Exemplar**: Concealed beneath layers of corporate shell companies was the beneficial owner of the sanctioned assets.

## Negative Inversion with 'Little did...'
- **ID**: gram-inv-16
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The shareholders did not suspect that the merger talks had already collapsed behind closed doors.
- **Transformation**: Little did the shareholders suspect that the merger talks had already collapsed behind closed doors.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Fronting the negative adverb 'little' (meaning 'not at all') triggers do-support inversion: 'did + subject + base verb'.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'Little did': 'Little' mang nghĩa phủ định 'hoàn toàn không'; đưa lên đầu câu, đảo trợ động từ 'did' lên trước chủ ngữ và đưa động từ chính về nguyên mẫu.
- **Formula**: `Little + do/does/did + Subject + Verb of Knowledge or Expectation (know/suspect/imagine/dream)`
- **Analysis**: Here 'little' is not a quantifier but a negative degree adverb equivalent to 'not at all'; like other negative adverbials, it forces subject-auxiliary inversion when fronted. It combines almost exclusively with verbs of knowledge and expectation (know, suspect, imagine, dream) and typically creates dramatic irony by contrasting a character's ignorance with what the reader already knows.
- **Exemplar**: Little did the young clerk imagine that the ledger he was copying would one day convict his employer.

## Restrictive Inversion with 'Only after...'
- **ID**: gram-inv-17
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The board approved the acquisition only after the due-diligence report had been independently verified.
- **Transformation**: Only after the due-diligence report had been independently verified did the board approve the acquisition.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Front the restrictive adjunct 'Only after [clause]'; invert the auxiliary in the main clause, never inside the 'after' clause.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'Only after/Only when': Mệnh đề hoặc cụm từ đi sau 'Only' giữ nguyên trật tự; chỉ đảo trợ động từ ở mệnh đề chính.
- **Formula**: `Only after/when/once/if + Clause or Phrase + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Only' restricts the truth of the proposition to a single condition or moment and so carries an implicit negative ('not before that'); fronting the whole restrictive adjunct therefore triggers inversion in the matrix clause. When 'only' modifies the subject itself, however, no inversion occurs ('Only the auditor noticed the error'), because the focused element is the subject and there is nothing to invert around it.
- **Exemplar**: Only when the last hostage had been released did the negotiators allow themselves to sleep.

## Degree Inversion with 'Such + be + Noun Phrase'
- **ID**: gram-inv-18
- **Mode**: INVERSION_EMPHASIS
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The public backlash was so intense that the ministry withdrew the bill within a week.
- **Transformation**: Such was the intensity of the public backlash that the ministry withdrew the bill within a week.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Front predicative 'Such'; invert the copula before an abstract-noun subject ('the intensity of...'); the verb agrees with that following subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Đảo ngữ với 'Such + be + cụm danh từ + that': Đưa 'Such' lên đầu câu, đảo động từ 'be' lên trước chủ ngữ là danh từ trừu tượng (the intensity of...), theo sau là mệnh đề kết quả.
- **Formula**: `Such + be (agreeing with the following subject) + Noun Phrase Subject + that + Result Clause`
- **Analysis**: Unlike 'So + adjective + be + subject', the 'such' pattern fronts a pro-form standing for the degree itself, so the original adjective usually has to be recast as an abstract noun ('intense' becomes 'the intensity of'). Because the real subject follows the verb, agreement is easily mishandled: 'Such were the pressures on the team that...' requires plural 'were'. The construction suits formal narrative and rhetoric in which the result clause forms the climax.
- **Exemplar**: Such were the delays at the border that perishable cargo had to be destroyed before it reached the market.

# Mode: Subjunctive & Unreal Conditionals (C1/C2)

> [!TIP]
> **Sắc thái & Bối cảnh sử dụng (Register & Communicative Context)**: Thể giả định (Mandative Subjunctive) mang tính trang trọng cao trong văn bản chính sách, nghị quyết pháp lý, khuyến nghị hành pháp hoặc thư tín thương mại cấp cao.
> **Lưu ý giao tiếp**: Trong văn cảnh đời thường, người bản xứ thường dùng 'should + V' hoặc động từ nguyên mẫu có 'to' để câu văn nhẹ nhàng, tự nhiên hơn.

## Mandative Subjunctive with 'Demand that'
- **ID**: gram-subj-1
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The regulatory commission demands that the exchange operator halts trading immediately.
- **Transformation**: The regulatory commission demands that the exchange operator halt trading immediately.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Verbs of demand require base bare form of the verb in the dependent 'that' clause.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định mệnh lệnh với 'demand': Động từ trong mệnh đề 'that' luôn ở dạng nguyên mẫu không 'to'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + demand + that + Subject + Base Form of Verb (no -s, no modal)`
- **Analysis**: In formal American and international English, the mandative subjunctive strips third-person singular inflection ('halt', not 'halts') and modal auxiliaries in content clauses governed by verbs of urgency, order, or demand.
- **Exemplar**: The treaty demands that each signatory state submit annual emissions inventories to verification inspectors.

## Mandative Subjunctive with 'Recommend that'
- **ID**: gram-subj-2
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Independent auditors recommend that management revises their revenue recognition schedules.
- **Transformation**: Independent auditors recommend that management revise their revenue recognition schedules.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Verb 'recommend' triggers subjunctive bare infinitive 'revise' regardless of subject number.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định với 'recommend': Dùng động từ nguyên mẫu 'revise' thay vì thêm '-s/es'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + recommend + that + Subject + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: Verbs expressing recommendation ('recommend', 'suggest', 'advise') take subjunctive complements where the predicate expresses a proposed ideal rather than an established factual truth.
- **Exemplar**: Physicians recommend that every adult patient undergo preventive coronary artery screening after age forty.

## Unreal Past with 'Wish + Past Simple'
- **ID**: gram-subj-3
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: The lead architect regrets that the system relies on a monolithic architecture.
- **Transformation**: The lead architect wishes the system did not rely on a monolithic architecture.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Express counterfactual present desire using 'wish + past tense' to signal distance from reality.
- **Vietnamese**: Ước muốn trái ngược với thực tại ở hiện tại: Dùng 'wish + Past Simple' (did not rely).
- **Formula**: `Subject + wish(es) + (that) + Subject + Simple Past Verb (signaling counterfactuality)`
- **Analysis**: The unreal past tense functions as a modal preterite, encoding subjective distance from current reality rather than chronological past time. The basic B1 version of this structure is gram-b1-14; this item practises it in a formal register.
- **Exemplar**: Economists wish central bank governors possessed more granular tools than blunt interest rate levers.

## Unreal Past with 'Wish + Past Perfect'
- **ID**: gram-subj-4
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The founders regret that they sold fifty-one percent of equity during the seed round.
- **Transformation**: The founders wish they had not sold fifty-one percent of equity during the seed round.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Past regret requires past-on-past counterfactual tense 'had + past participle'.
- **Vietnamese**: Ước muốn trái ngược với sự việc trong quá khứ: Dùng 'wish + Past Perfect' (had not sold).
- **Formula**: `Subject + wish(es) + (that) + Subject + had (not) + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: When expressing counterfactual regret about an unchangeable historical past event, English shifts from simple past to the modal pluperfect ('had not sold').
- **Exemplar**: Diplomats wished both belligerents had ratified the ceasefire before artillery strikes resumed.

## Fixed Subjunctive Idiom 'Be that as it may'
- **ID**: gram-subj-5
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: Even if that is true, we must nevertheless enforce the statutory compliance sanctions.
- **Transformation**: Be that as it may, we must nevertheless enforce the statutory compliance sanctions.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use archaic formulaic subjunctive clause 'Be that as it may' as a concessive discourse transition.
- **Vietnamese**: Cụm giả định cố định 'Be that as it may' (Cho dù có đúng như vậy đi chăng nữa).
- **Formula**: `Be that as it may, [Main Clause]`
- **Analysis**: A relic of Old English formulaic subjunctive syntax where inversion of the subjunctive copula 'be' before demonstrative 'that' replaces a modern subordinate concessive clause ('Even if that may be so').
- **Exemplar**: Be that as it may, historical precedents cannot excuse deliberate accounting fraud.

## Impersonal Mandative 'It is imperative that...'
- **ID**: gram-subj-6
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: It is imperative that every flight controller remains vigilant during severe weather diversions.
- **Transformation**: It is imperative that every flight controller remain vigilant during severe weather diversions.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Adjectives of necessity ('imperative', 'vital') govern bare base verbs in 'that' complements.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định với tính từ bắt buộc 'It is imperative that + S + V(bare)'.
- **Formula**: `It is imperative that + Subject + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: Evaluative adjectives expressing necessity ('imperative', 'essential', 'mandatory') demand the uninflected subjunctive base verb in the subordinate clause.
- **Exemplar**: It is imperative that the server cluster maintain nine-nines uptime throughout the holiday shopping spike.

## Impersonal Mandative 'It is essential that...'
- **ID**: gram-subj-7
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: It is essential that the forensic analyst documents the chain of custody for all hard drives.
- **Transformation**: It is essential that the forensic analyst document the chain of custody for all hard drives.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Adjective 'essential' strips third-person '-s' in formal subjunctive clause.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định với tính từ 'essential': Động từ 'document' không chia theo chủ ngữ số ít.
- **Formula**: `It is essential that + Subject + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: Subjunctive 'document' emphasizes the institutional obligation as a normative rule rather than a description of an ongoing factual observation.
- **Exemplar**: It is essential that sovereign wealth funds disclose political risk exposures to international credit rating agencies.

## Unreal Comparison with 'As if / As though'
- **ID**: gram-subj-8
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The CEO acted like the antitrust lawsuit was an irrelevant nuisance.
- **Transformation**: The CEO acted as if the antitrust lawsuit were an irrelevant nuisance.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Formal counterfactual comparison replaces informal 'like' with 'as if' and subjunctive 'were'.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh không có thực với 'as if / as though': Dùng 'were' thay cho 'was' để nhấn mạnh tính giả định.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb + as if / as though + Subject + were (Subjunctive Past)`
- **Analysis**: In academic and elevated stylistic prose, counterfactual manners or states are linked using 'as if' paired with the traditional subjunctive 'were' (regardless of singular subject) to signal deliberate departure from reality.
- **Exemplar**: The authoritarian regime conducted elections as though popular consent were genuinely being solicited.

## Idiomatic Unreal Structure 'It is high time that...'
- **ID**: gram-subj-9
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The legislature should finally modernize antiquated consumer privacy legislation.
- **Transformation**: It is high time the legislature modernized antiquated consumer privacy legislation.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'It is high time' takes past simple verb to express overdue action with gentle reproach.
- **Vietnamese**: Cấu trúc đã đến lúc muộn màng: 'It is high time + S + Past Simple'.
- **Formula**: `It is (high / about) time + Subject + Simple Past Verb`
- **Analysis**: This idiomatic construction employs the modal preterite ('modernized') to indicate that the appropriate moment for action has already passed, injecting urgency and mild criticism.
- **Exemplar**: It is high time corporate governance frameworks acknowledged the existential risks of unaligned automation.

## Formulaic Subjunctive 'Come what may'
- **ID**: gram-subj-10
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: Whatever happens, the scientific expedition will press on toward the subglacial lake.
- **Transformation**: Come what may, the scientific expedition will press on toward the subglacial lake.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace concessive conditional clause with classic formulaic subjunctive 'Come what may'.
- **Vietnamese**: Thành ngữ giả định cổ 'Come what may' (Dù có xảy ra bất cứ chuyện gì chăng nữa).
- **Formula**: `Come what may, [Main Clause]`
- **Analysis**: An inverted hortatory subjunctive idiom originating from early modern English, equivalent to 'let whatever may come, come'. It conveys resolute commitment in the face of unknown adversity.
- **Exemplar**: Come what may, the central bank must defend currency parity against speculative short attacks.

## Passive Mandative Subjunctive 'Be done'
- **ID**: gram-subj-11
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The ethical charter decrees that the training dataset is purged of unconsented biometrics.
- **Transformation**: The ethical charter decrees that the training dataset be purged of unconsented biometrics.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Combine passive voice with mandative subjunctive: 'be + past participle'.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định bị động: Dùng 'be + V3' (be purged) thay vì 'is/are purged' trong mệnh đề mệnh lệnh.
- **Formula**: `Subject + decree/order/mandate + that + Subject + be + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: When a passive construction falls inside a mandative subjunctive clause, the auxiliary copula remains invariant 'be' (never inflected as 'is', 'are', or 'was').
- **Exemplar**: International law stipulates that prisoners of war be treated with humane dignity under all conditions.

## Mixed Conditional (Past Cause -> Present Result)
- **ID**: gram-subj-12
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Because the developers did not architect modular microservices in 2021, the application is unmaintainable today.
- **Transformation**: Had the developers architected modular microservices in 2021, the application would be maintainable today.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Blend inverted past perfect condition ('Had... architected') with present modal result ('would be').
- **Vietnamese**: Câu điều kiện trộn (Quá khứ tác động Hiện tại): Mệnh đề điều kiện dùng Type 3, mệnh đề kết quả dùng Type 2.
- **Formula**: `Had + Subject + Past Participle..., Subject + would (not) + Base Verb + today/now`
- **Analysis**: A mixed conditional bridges temporal domains: the antecedent is counterfactual past ('Had they architected'), while the consequent refers to current ongoing counterfactual reality ('would be').
- **Exemplar**: Had regulators capped subprime securitization ratios in 2005, the global financial system would enjoy greater stability today.

## Mixed Conditional (Permanent Trait -> Past Counterfactual)
- **ID**: gram-subj-13
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Because the lead negotiator is not fluent in Mandarin, she misunderstood the subtle contractual nuance yesterday.
- **Transformation**: Were the lead negotiator fluent in Mandarin, she would not have misunderstood the subtle contractual nuance yesterday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Invert present subjunctive 'Were [Subject] [Adj]' linked to past modal perfect result 'would have [V3]'.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu điều kiện trộn (Bản chất hiện tại tác động Quá khứ): Điều kiện dùng Type 2, kết quả dùng Type 3.
- **Formula**: `Were + Subject + Adjective/Noun..., Subject + would (not) have + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: Here, the antecedent describes an enduring state or characteristic in the present ('Were she fluent'), while the consequent evaluates an unfulfilled counterfactual outcome in the historical past ('would not have misunderstood').
- **Exemplar**: Were sovereign debt markets less liquid, yesterday's treasury auction would have triggered widespread bond market panic.

## Formulaic Subjunctive 'Suffice it to say'
- **ID**: gram-subj-14
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: It is enough to say that the subsequent investigation revealed catastrophic governance failures.
- **Transformation**: Suffice it to say that the subsequent investigation revealed catastrophic governance failures.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use classical formulaic subjunctive 'Suffice it to say' for concise rhetorical summary.
- **Vietnamese**: Thành ngữ giả định cổ 'Suffice it to say that...' (Chỉ cần nói rằng... là đủ hiểu).
- **Formula**: `Suffice it to say that + Clause`
- **Analysis**: A fossilized third-person singular present subjunctive verb ('suffice', meaning 'let it suffice') functioning as an elevated discourse marker to truncate lengthy narratives while conveying gravity.
- **Exemplar**: Suffice it to say that the trial testimony permanently dismantled the defense counsel's alibi.

## Negative Subjunctive with 'Lest... (should) be'
- **ID**: gram-subj-15
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The treasury diversified dollar reserves so that they would not be caught unprepared by sanctions.
- **Transformation**: The treasury diversified dollar reserves lest they be caught unprepared by sanctions.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace 'so that... not' with negative subordinator 'lest' governing bare subjunctive 'be caught'.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định với liên từ 'Lest' (E rằng, vì sợ rằng): Mệnh đề sau 'lest' dùng động từ nguyên mẫu.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb... + lest + Subject + (should) + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Lest' is an intrinsically negative conjunction of prevention meaning 'for fear that'. It traditionally governs the bare subjunctive ('lest they be caught') without auxiliary 'not'.
- **Exemplar**: Engineers added physical thermal breakers lest the reactor core suffer uncontained coolant runaway.

## Counterfactual Dependence with 'Were it not for...'
- **ID**: gram-subj-16
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: If the emergency credit line did not exist, the airline would already be insolvent.
- **Transformation**: Were it not for the emergency credit line, the airline would already be insolvent.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace 'If X did not exist' with the fixed inverted subjunctive 'Were it not for + noun phrase'.
- **Vietnamese**: Cấu trúc 'Were it not for + danh từ' (Nếu không nhờ có / Nếu không vì...): diễn tả điều kiện trái với hiện tại; dạng quá khứ tương ứng là 'Had it not been for'.
- **Formula**: `Were it not for + Noun Phrase, Subject + would/could + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: The clause uses dummy 'it' with subjunctive 'were' inverted in place of 'if', so the noun phrase after 'for' names the single factor on which the whole situation depends. For past counterfactuals the form becomes 'Had it not been for + noun phrase, subject + would have + past participle'. 'But for + noun phrase' is a terser, equally formal alternative that works for both time frames, while the uninverted 'If it were not for' is acceptable in neutral registers.
- **Exemplar**: Were it not for the volunteers who staff the night shifts, the shelter could not remain open through the winter.

## Negative Mandative Subjunctive ('that he not be...')
- **ID**: gram-subj-17
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The tribunal insists that the witness does not discuss her testimony with the press.
- **Transformation**: The tribunal insists that the witness not discuss her testimony with the press.
- **Grammatical Cue**: In a negative mandative clause, place 'not' directly before the bare verb: no 'do' support and no third-person '-s'.
- **Vietnamese**: Thể giả định mệnh lệnh dạng phủ định: đặt 'not' ngay trước động từ nguyên mẫu (not discuss, not be), không dùng trợ động từ 'do/does'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + insist/request/require/recommend + that + Subject + not + Base Verb (or not be + Past Participle)`
- **Analysis**: Because the subjunctive verb carries no tense, it cannot take do-support, so negation is simply 'not' plus the base form ('that she not attend', 'that the files not be released'). With 'insist', the mood also disambiguates meaning: indicative 'insisted that he was innocent' asserts a fact, whereas subjunctive 'insisted that he be present' issues a demand. American formal usage strongly prefers this subjunctive; British English accepts it in formal writing but more often uses 'should not discuss', and the indicative is also heard in casual British speech.
- **Exemplar**: The family requested that the minister's private letters not be published until after their mother's death.

## Archaic Optative 'Would that...'
- **ID**: gram-subj-18
- **Mode**: SUBJUNCTIVE_UNREAL
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: I wish the founders had foreseen how fragile their governance model would prove.
- **Transformation**: Would that the founders had foreseen how fragile their governance model would prove.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace 'I wish' with the literary optative 'Would that'; keep the backshifted tense (past perfect for past regret).
- **Vietnamese**: Cấu trúc ước nguyện cổ 'Would that...' (Giá mà...): thay cho 'I wish'; dùng quá khứ đơn hoặc 'were' cho ước muốn ở hiện tại và quá khứ hoàn thành cho sự tiếc nuối về quá khứ.
- **Formula**: `Would that + Subject + were/Past Simple (present wish) | Past Perfect (past regret)`
- **Analysis**: 'Would that' descends from an elliptical 'I would (wish) that' and now functions as a fixed optative formula with no overt subject. It follows the same tense backshift as 'wish': 'Would that it were true' for the present, 'Would that they had listened' for the past. The structure is markedly literary or elegiac; in contemporary prose it signals deliberate rhetorical heightening and would sound affected in a routine report.
- **Exemplar**: Would that every reform were as easy to implement as it is to announce.

# Mode: Clausal Condensation & Participial Architecture (C1/C2)

> [!TIP]
> **Sắc thái & Bối cảnh sử dụng (Register & Communicative Context)**: Rút gọn mệnh đề phân từ (Participle Clauses) giúp bài viết đạt mật độ thông tin cao (high lexical density) - tiêu chuẩn then chốt trong IELTS Band 8.0+ và các báo cáo học thuật.
> **Bẫy nguy hiểm (Dangling Participle)**: Luôn đảm bảo chủ ngữ của phân từ rút gọn phải trùng khớp chính xác với chủ ngữ của mệnh đề chính đứng sau.

## Present Participle Clause for Simultaneous Action
- **ID**: gram-cond-1
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The lead investigator reviewed the transaction logs and he discovered three unauthorized wire transfers.
- **Transformation**: Reviewing the transaction logs, the lead investigator discovered three unauthorized wire transfers.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Condense coordinated clause into fronted present participial adjunct ('Reviewing...').
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề đồng thời dùng hiện tại phân từ (V-ing): Chủ ngữ hai vế phải đồng nhất.
- **Formula**: `Present Participle (-ing) Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: When two actions with the identical subject occur concurrently or in immediate sequence, the first coordinated clause can be streamlined into an introductory participial adjunct, elevating syntactic economy.
- **Exemplar**: Scanning the horizon through infrared binoculars, the coast guard spotted the adrift life raft.

## Past Participle Clause for Passive Condition
- **ID**: gram-cond-2
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: When the algorithm was trained on biased historical data, it systematically penalized minority applicants.
- **Transformation**: Trained on biased historical data, the algorithm systematically penalized minority applicants.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Strip conjunction and passive copula, opening sentence directly with past participle phrase.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề bị động dùng quá khứ phân từ (V-ed/V3): Lược bỏ 'When' và to-be.
- **Formula**: `Past Participle Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Passive adverbial clauses condense cleanly into bare past participle phrases. The implied subject of the participle must strictly match the grammatical subject of the governing main clause.
- **Exemplar**: Deprived of access to foreign capital markets, the sanctioned regime turned to domestic bond monetization.

## Reduced Relative Clause with Present Participle
- **ID**: gram-cond-3
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: All candidates who are seeking admission to the doctoral fellowship must submit three peer recommendations.
- **Transformation**: All candidates seeking admission to the doctoral fellowship must submit three peer recommendations.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Eliminate relative pronoun 'who' and auxiliary 'are', leaving active participle 'seeking'.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ chủ động: Bỏ đại từ quan hệ và to-be, giữ lại V-ing làm định ngữ.
- **Formula**: `Noun + Present Participle Phrase + Main Predicate`
- **Analysis**: Active restrictive relative clauses with progressive or continuous aspects condense by deleting the relative pronoun and the copula, transforming the clause into an agile post-nominal participial modifier.
- **Exemplar**: Passengers arriving on transatlantic flights must present biometric declaration barcodes.

## Reduced Relative Clause with Past Participle
- **ID**: gram-cond-4
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The infrastructure bill which was passed by the Senate will revitalize crumbling bridge corridors.
- **Transformation**: The infrastructure bill passed by the Senate will revitalize crumbling bridge corridors.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Omit relative pronoun 'which' and auxiliary 'was' to create a tight participial post-modifier.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ bị động: Bỏ 'which was', chỉ giữ lại quá khứ phân từ (passed by...).
- **Formula**: `Noun + Past Participle Phrase + Main Predicate`
- **Analysis**: Passive relative clauses trim redundant function words ('which was'), sharpening prose density without sacrificing informational fidelity.
- **Exemplar**: The settlement agreed upon by both litigants averts a costly five-week federal jury trial.

## Prepositional Participial Phrase ('Upon arriving...')
- **ID**: gram-cond-5
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: As soon as the foreign dignitary arrived at the summit venue, she was escorted to the bilateral chambers.
- **Transformation**: Upon arriving at the summit venue, the foreign dignitary was escorted to the bilateral chambers.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Condense temporal clause with preposition 'Upon / On' followed by gerund-participle.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề thời gian với 'Upon + V-ing' (Ngay khi vừa đến nơi...).
- **Formula**: `Upon / On + -ing Verb Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Prepositional participial phrases ('Upon [verb]-ing') cleanly compress temporal clauses ('As soon as [subject] [verb]-ed') while explicitly reinforcing temporal immediacy.
- **Exemplar**: Upon reviewing the quarterly earnings reconciliation, the board voted unanimously to authorize a share buyback.

## Perfect Participle Clause for Prior Action
- **ID**: gram-cond-6
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: Because she had exhausted all administrative appeals, the claimant filed a federal constitutional lawsuit.
- **Transformation**: Having exhausted all administrative appeals, the claimant filed a federal constitutional lawsuit.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Convert prior causal clause into perfect participle 'Having + past participle'.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề nguyên nhân xảy ra trước bằng phân từ hoàn thành 'Having + V3'.
- **Formula**: `Having + Past Participle Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: A perfect participle ('Having exhausted') explicitly marks anteriority, showing that the participial event was completed prior to the onset of the main clause action.
- **Exemplar**: Having secured unanimous shareholder endorsement, the management board executed the cross-border merger.

## Passive Perfect Participle Clause
- **ID**: gram-cond-7
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: After it had been stress-tested under extreme simulated market liquidity freezes, the risk model was cleared.
- **Transformation**: Having been stress-tested under extreme simulated market liquidity freezes, the risk model was cleared.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Combine perfect and passive aspect into 'Having been + past participle'.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn phân từ hoàn thành thể bị động 'Having been + V3' (Sau khi đã được kiểm nghiệm...).
- **Formula**: `Having been + Past Participle Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Passive perfect participle clauses compress lengthy background passive histories into a potent introductory participial modifier, maintaining chronological sequence.
- **Exemplar**: Having been scrutinized by federal ethics monitors for six months, the proposed acquisition received regulatory blessing.

## Concessive Participle Clause ('Although warned...')
- **ID**: gram-cond-8
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Although they were repeatedly warned about impending credit tightening, the directors continued borrowing.
- **Transformation**: Although repeatedly warned about impending credit tightening, the directors continued borrowing.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Retain concessive subordinator 'Although' but drop subject pronoun and auxiliary verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề nhượng bộ giữ lại liên từ: 'Although + V3' (Dù đã được cảnh báo nhiều lần...).
- **Formula**: `Although / Even though + Past Participle Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Unlike general participle clauses that omit subordinating conjunctions, retaining 'Although' or 'While' clarifies the precise semantic relation (concession) while still achieving clausal condensation.
- **Exemplar**: Although severely battered by post-pandemic supply chain disruptions, the automaker preserved operating profitability.

## Absolute Construction with Noun + Participle
- **ID**: gram-cond-9
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: Because the market close was rapidly approaching, day traders liquidated their speculative derivative calls.
- **Transformation**: The market close rapidly approaching, day traders liquidated their speculative derivative calls.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Create an absolute clause: noun phrase subject + participle phrase, grammatically independent of main clause.
- **Vietnamese**: Cấu trúc tuyệt đối (Absolute Construction): Cụm danh từ đi với phân từ, không cần liên từ nối.
- **Formula**: `Noun Phrase + Participle (-ing / -ed) Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: An absolute construction possesses its own overt subject ('The market close'), distinct from the main clause subject ('day traders'). It attaches to the sentence without any subordinating conjunction.
- **Exemplar**: All quorum hurdles successfully cleared, the parliamentary assembly proceeded to ratify the constitution.

## Verbless Adjective Clause for Conciseness
- **ID**: gram-cond-10
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Because he was aware of the severe geopolitical risks, the ambassador spoke with calculated discretion.
- **Transformation**: Aware of the severe geopolitical risks, the ambassador spoke with calculated discretion.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Drop causal conjunction and copula, starting directly with adjective complement 'Aware of...'.
- **Vietnamese**: Mệnh đề tính từ lược bỏ động từ (Verbless Adjective Clause): Đặt tính từ ở đầu câu làm trạng ngữ.
- **Formula**: `Adjective Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Verbless clauses operate as condensed appositives or circumstantial adjuncts. Opening with an adjective phrase ('Aware of...') creates swift, elegant narrative pacing.
- **Exemplar**: Anxious to avoid diplomatic confrontation, the envoy offered a compromise communique.

## Absolute Construction with Preposition 'With + Noun + Participle'
- **ID**: gram-cond-11
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Because inflation rates were escalating and bond yields were cratering, the pension fund reassessed allocations.
- **Transformation**: With inflation rates escalating and bond yields cratering, the pension fund reassessed allocations.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use preposition 'With' to govern compound absolute participial complements.
- **Vietnamese**: Cấu trúc tuyệt đối mở đầu bằng 'With': 'With + Noun + V-ing/V3' để liệt kê bối cảnh phức tạp.
- **Formula**: `With + Noun Phrase 1 + Participle 1 and Noun Phrase 2 + Participle 2, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: The preposition 'with' licenses an absolute structure where multiple circumstantial forces are packed into parallel nominal-participial pairs before the main clause action.
- **Exemplar**: With venture valuations contracting and interest rates ascending, early-stage startups pivoted toward immediate cash generation.

## Negative Perfect Participle Clause
- **ID**: gram-cond-12
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Because the analysts had not anticipated the sudden export ban, they scrambled to revise earnings projections.
- **Transformation**: Not having anticipated the sudden export ban, the analysts scrambled to revise earnings projections.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Position negative particle 'Not' immediately before the perfect participle 'having anticipated'.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn phân từ hoàn thành thể phủ định: Đặt 'Not' đứng trước 'Having + V3'.
- **Formula**: `Not + having + Past Participle Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: In negative participial constructions, 'not' usually comes first, directly before the perfect participle 'having' ('Not having anticipated'). The internal order 'Having not anticipated' does occur, but it is less usual in formal prose.
- **Exemplar**: Not having received written confirmation from the ministry, the general refused to mobilize reserve brigades.

## Double Participle Sandwich for Rapid Narrative Pacing
- **ID**: gram-cond-13
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: As she was pursued by regulatory hounds and abandoned by institutional backers, the CEO stepped down.
- **Transformation**: Pursued by regulatory hounds and abandoned by institutional backers, the CEO stepped down.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Coordinate two passive participle phrases in parallel before the primary subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Kỹ thuật phân từ kép song hành (Double Participle Sandwich): Kết hợp hai cụm V3 tăng cường nhịp điệu dồn dập.
- **Formula**: `Past Participle Phrase 1 and Past Participle Phrase 2, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Stacking coordinated participial phrases in parallel rhythmically heightens causal tension before dropping the hammer on the final independent clause.
- **Exemplar**: Stunned by the unexpected verdict and threatened with immediate extradition, the fugitive collapsed in open court.

## Absolute Construction Expressing Metric Outcome
- **ID**: gram-cond-14
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The conglomerate executed the debt refinancing package, and its net debt-to-equity ratio fell to 1.2.
- **Transformation**: The conglomerate executed the debt refinancing package, its net debt-to-equity ratio falling to 1.2.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Attach post-clausal absolute construction with present participle showing mathematical consequence.
- **Vietnamese**: Cấu trúc tuyệt đối đứng sau câu chính để chỉ hệ quả định lượng hoặc kết quả số liệu.
- **Formula**: `Main Clause, + Noun Phrase Subject + Present Participle Phrase`
- **Analysis**: A trailing absolute construction provides a sophisticated journalistic mechanism to report secondary metric consequences without creating clumsy coordinate compound sentences.
- **Exemplar**: The central bank unexpectedly cut benchmark rates by fifty basis points, equity indices surging three percent within seconds.

## Reduced Concessive Clause with Ellipsis ('Though seemingly intractable...')
- **ID**: gram-cond-15
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: Although it was seemingly an intractable logistical deadlock, the dispute was resolved within forty-eight hours.
- **Transformation**: Though seemingly an intractable logistical deadlock, the dispute was resolved within forty-eight hours.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Retain concessive 'Though' while ellipting the subject pronoun 'it' and the copula 'was'.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề nhượng bộ bằng tỉnh lược (Ellipsis): Giữ 'Though', lược bỏ đại từ chủ ngữ 'it' và to-be.
- **Formula**: `Though / Although + Adverb + Noun Phrase Complement, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Syntactic ellipsis removes the subject pronoun plus copula ('it was') in introductory concessive clauses, throwing maximum semantic weight directly onto the descriptive noun phrase complement.
- **Exemplar**: Though initially a modest regional initiative, the economic corridor evolved into a trillion-dollar trade artery.

## Infinitival Relative Clause after Ordinals ('the first to...')
- **ID**: gram-cond-16
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: She was the first engineer who identified the race condition in the payment service.
- **Transformation**: She was the first engineer to identify the race condition in the payment service.
- **Grammatical Cue**: After an ordinal, a superlative or 'only', reduce the subject relative clause 'who + finite verb' to a to-infinitive.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ bằng 'to + V' sau số thứ tự (the first, the last), so sánh nhất hoặc 'the only'.
- **Formula**: `the first/last/next/only/Superlative + Noun + to-Infinitive`
- **Analysis**: When the head noun is restricted by an ordinal, a superlative or 'only', a subject relative clause can be condensed into a to-infinitive whose understood subject is the head noun itself. The infinitive is tenseless, so time reference is inferred from the main clause, and a passive relative condenses to 'to be + past participle' ('the last item to be approved').
- **Exemplar**: The ministry was the last government agency to move its records off paper ledgers.

## Causal Participle Clause with 'Being + Adjective'
- **ID**: gram-cond-17
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Because the senior partner was unfamiliar with the jurisdiction, she deferred to local counsel.
- **Transformation**: Being unfamiliar with the jurisdiction, the senior partner deferred to local counsel.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace 'Because + subject + be + adjective' with a fronted 'Being + adjective' phrase that shares the main-clause subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề chỉ nguyên nhân bằng 'Being + tính từ': hai vế phải cùng chủ ngữ; 'Being' làm rõ quan hệ nhân quả.
- **Formula**: `Being + Adjective Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: A 'being' clause attached to a stative adjective is read almost always as causal, because a state cannot easily be construed as an action happening alongside the main event. 'Being' can sometimes be dropped to yield a verbless clause ('Unfamiliar with the jurisdiction, ...'), but retaining it makes the reason relation explicit. As with all participle clauses, the implied subject must be the subject of the main clause, or the modifier dangles.
- **Exemplar**: Being acutely aware of the reputational stakes, the editor delayed publication until every source had been verified.

## Subordinator-Retained Reduced Clause ('Once ratified...')
- **ID**: gram-cond-18
- **Mode**: CLAUSAL_CONDENSATION
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Once the amendment has been ratified by both chambers, it cannot be revoked without a referendum.
- **Transformation**: Once ratified by both chambers, the amendment cannot be revoked without a referendum.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Keep the subordinator 'once' but delete the subject and the auxiliary 'has been', leaving the bare past participle.
- **Vietnamese**: Rút gọn mệnh đề trạng ngữ nhưng giữ liên từ (once, when, if, unless, while): lược bỏ chủ ngữ và 'be', chỉ còn phân từ, tính từ hoặc cụm giới từ.
- **Formula**: `Once/When/If/Unless/While/Until + Past Participle / Adjective / Prepositional Phrase, Subject + Main Verb`
- **Analysis**: Subordinators such as 'once', 'when', 'if', 'unless' and 'until' can survive the ellipsis of subject plus 'be', yielding a compact clause that keeps a precise logical relation which a bare participle would leave vague. Fixed verbless forms like 'When in doubt', 'If in doubt' and 'If necessary' follow the same pattern. The ellipted subject must be recoverable as the main-clause subject: 'Once ratified, voters cannot revoke the amendment' wrongly implies that the voters were ratified.
- **Exemplar**: When in doubt, the duty officer escalates the alert rather than closing it.

# Mode: Syntactic Repair & Parallelism Precision (C1/C2)

> [!TIP]
> **Sắc thái & Bối cảnh sử dụng (Register & Communicative Context)**: Cấu trúc song hành (Parallelism) quyết định tính đối xứng, rõ ràng và sức nặng logic của lập luận.
> **Quy tắc vàng**: Tất cả các vế liệt kê nối bằng 'and/or/but/as well as' phải giữ nguyên cùng một dạng từ (toàn bộ danh từ, toàn bộ V-ing hoặc toàn bộ mệnh đề hoàn chỉnh).

## Correction of Faulty Parallelism in Series
- **ID**: gram-prec-1
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The audit revealed that the branch was inflating invoices, concealed debts, and to bribe local officials.
- **Transformation**: The audit revealed that the branch was inflating invoices, concealing debts, and bribing local officials.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Harmonize all coordinate verbal complements into parallel gerund-participle (-ing) forms.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi cấu trúc song hành trong chuỗi liệt kê: Tất cả động từ phải ở cùng một dạng V-ing.
- **Formula**: `Verb + X(-ing), Y(-ing), and Z(-ing)`
- **Analysis**: When coordinating a series of items connected by conjunctions ('and', 'or'), all grammatical members must occupy identical syntactic categories and morphological forms.
- **Exemplar**: Effective executives excel at distilling complex data, communicating clear visions, and empowering autonomous teams.

## Correction of Correlative Parallelism ('Not only... but also')
- **ID**: gram-prec-2
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The policy change not only alienated key enterprise customers, but also it generated meager recurring revenues.
- **Transformation**: The policy change not only alienated key enterprise customers, but also generated meager recurring revenues.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Ensure constituents following 'not only' and 'but also' share identical grammatical categories (Verb Phrase).
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi song hành với liên từ tương quan 'Not only... but also': Hai vế sau liên từ phải đồng nhất cấu trúc.
- **Formula**: `Subject + not only + Verb Phrase 1 + but also + Verb Phrase 2`
- **Analysis**: In correlative structures ('not only X but also Y'), the constituent following 'not only' (past verb 'alienated') must syntactically mirror the constituent following 'but also' (past verb 'generated', without superfluous subject pronoun 'it').
- **Exemplar**: The novel antitrust decree seeks not only to penalize past anti-competitive collusion, but also to forestall future market concentration.

## Fixing Unintentional Tense Shifts in Narrative Discourse
- **ID**: gram-prec-3
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: The lead engineer detected anomalous telemetry spikes, and immediately he isolates the compromised gateway.
- **Transformation**: The lead engineer detected anomalous telemetry spikes and immediately isolated the compromised gateway.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Eliminate arbitrary shift from past tense 'detected' to present tense 'isolates'.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi chuyển thì tùy tiện: Duy trì nhất quán thì quá khứ đơn (detected... isolated).
- **Formula**: `Subject + Past Verb 1 + and + Past Verb 2`
- **Analysis**: Writers must maintain tense consistency across coordinated clauses unless a legitimate temporal or modal shift occurs. Shifting from past ('detected') to present ('isolates') creates jarring cognitive dissonance for the reader.
- **Exemplar**: The forensic investigator examined the encrypted drive, recovered the deleted private key, and compiled the final report.

## Eliminating Passive Wordiness via Direct Transitive Verbs
- **ID**: gram-prec-4
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: An analysis of the quarterly logistics telemetry was conducted by the operations optimization squad.
- **Transformation**: The operations optimization squad analyzed the quarterly logistics telemetry.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Convert clunky passive nominalization ('An analysis was conducted by...') into vigorous active transitive verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Chuyển câu bị động rườm rà thành câu chủ động sắc bén: Biến danh từ hóa 'analysis' thành động từ 'analyzed'.
- **Formula**: `Agent Subject + Active Transitive Verb + Direct Object`
- **Analysis**: Smothered verbs (nominalizations like 'conduct an analysis') and passive agent bypasses drain prose of vitality. Replacing them with direct active voice clarifies agency and trims syllable bloat.
- **Exemplar**: The appellate court vacated the lower tribunal's patent injunction.

## Coordinated Adverbial Parallelism
- **ID**: gram-prec-5
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The spokesperson addressed the accusations calmly, thoroughly, and in a professional manner.
- **Transformation**: The spokesperson addressed the accusations calmly, thoroughly, and professionally.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Harmonize prepositional phrase 'in a professional manner' into clean parallel '-ly' adverb.
- **Vietnamese**: Song hành hóa trạng từ: Thay thế cụm giới từ dài dòng bằng trạng từ đuôi '-ly' đồng bộ.
- **Formula**: `Verb + Adverb 1(-ly), Adverb 2(-ly), and Adverb 3(-ly)`
- **Analysis**: Mixing lexical adverbs with prepositional phrases in an adverbial list disrupts rhetorical cadence. Converting the final element into an '-ly' adverb restores crisp prosodic symmetry.
- **Exemplar**: The negotiation team argued their counter-proposals persuasively, tenaciously, and diplomatically.

## Dangling Modifier with Introductory Participial Phrase
- **ID**: gram-prec-6
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Approaching the international border crossing, the travelers' passports were inspected by biometric cameras.
- **Transformation**: Approaching the international border crossing, the travelers had their passports inspected by biometric cameras.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Ensure grammatical subject immediately following introductory participle is the agent performing the action.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi phân từ lơ lửng (Dangling Modifier): Hộ chiếu không thể tự 'tiến đến biên giới', chủ ngữ phải là du khách.
- **Formula**: `Participial Phrase (Agent action), Agent Subject + Verb`
- **Analysis**: A dangling participle occurs when the implied subject of an introductory verbal phrase differs from the grammatical subject of the following independent clause. Passports cannot physically approach a border; the human travelers must serve as matrix subject.
- **Exemplar**: Synthesizing fifteen longitudinal demographic studies, the researchers demonstrated a sharp correlation between urbanization and declining birth rates.

## Misplaced Modifier with Adverbial Quantifier ('Only')
- **ID**: gram-prec-7
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The compliance officer only flagged three fraudulent wire transfers during the audit.
- **Transformation**: The compliance officer flagged only three fraudulent wire transfers during the audit.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Place focus adverb 'only' directly adjacent to the numerical noun phrase it restricts.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi đặt sai vị trí từ hạn định 'Only': Đặt 'only' ngay trước đối tượng cần bổ nghĩa (only three).
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb + only + Quantifier / Object Noun Phrase`
- **Analysis**: Positioning 'only' before the verb 'flagged' technically asserts that flagging was the sole action the officer performed (e.g., she didn't approve or delete them). Moving it before 'three' correctly restricts the numerical count.
- **Exemplar**: The appellate court granted review of only one constitutional claim among the dozen raised.

## Faulty Comparison / Illogical Parallelism ('More than that of')
- **ID**: gram-prec-8
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The computational throughput of our distributed cluster is substantially higher than our primary competitor.
- **Transformation**: The computational throughput of our distributed cluster is substantially higher than that of our primary competitor.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use pro-form 'that of' to prevent comparing an abstract metric ('throughput') directly against a company.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi so sánh khập khiễng (Illogical Comparison): Thêm 'that of' để so sánh công suất với công suất.
- **Formula**: `Metric of A + is higher than + that of B`
- **Analysis**: Comparing an inanimate metric ('throughput') to an entity ('competitor') represents an illogical comparative fallacy. The pro-form 'that of' (or 'those of' for plural nouns) must be inserted to preserve ontological parity.
- **Exemplar**: The fuel efficiency of modular molten-salt reactors far exceeds that of conventional light-water facilities.

## Split Infinitive Resolution in Formal Register
- **ID**: gram-prec-9
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The statutory directive instructs administrators to strictly and without exception enforce the embargo.
- **Transformation**: The statutory directive instructs administrators to enforce the embargo strictly and without exception.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Relocate lengthy adverbial phrase outside the 'to + verb' infinitive nexus in formal legal prose.
- **Vietnamese**: Xử lý chẻ động từ nguyên mẫu (Split Infinitive): Chuyển cụm trạng từ dài ra sau tân ngữ trong văn phong pháp lý.
- **Formula**: `to + Base Verb + Object + Adverbial Modifier`
- **Analysis**: While mild split infinitives are permissible in modern English, wedging a sprawling parenthetical adverbial phrase ('strictly and without exception') between 'to' and 'enforce' fractures cognitive momentum in high-register formal documentation.
- **Exemplar**: The regulatory body resolved to prosecute rogue currency speculators vigorously and transparently.

## Restrictive vs. Non-Restrictive Precision ('Which' vs 'That')
- **ID**: gram-prec-10
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: All proprietary trade secrets, which were acquired during the joint venture, must be returned.
- **Transformation**: All proprietary trade secrets that were acquired during the joint venture must be returned.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use restrictive pronoun 'that' without commas to define an essential subset of trade secrets.
- **Vietnamese**: Phân biệt mệnh đề quan hệ xác định (that) và không xác định (, which,): Chọn 'that' để xác định nhóm cụ thể.
- **Formula**: `Noun Phrase + that + Restrictive Clause (no commas) + Predicate`
- **Analysis**: Surrounding the clause with commas and using 'which' implies that all trade secrets in existence were acquired during the venture. Omitting commas and using 'that' restricts the obligation exclusively to the subset acquired during the venture. Note that reserving 'that' for restrictive clauses and 'which' for non-restrictive ones is an American style convention; British usage often accepts 'which' in restrictive clauses too, so the comma is the more reliable signal.
- **Exemplar**: Assets that are subject to sovereign freeze orders cannot be collateralized in repo markets.

## Dangling Gerund Phrase after Prepositional Opener
- **ID**: gram-prec-11
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: After dissecting the malware payload, the zero-day vulnerability was successfully patched by our security team.
- **Transformation**: After dissecting the malware payload, our security team successfully patched the zero-day vulnerability.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Make the human actor ('our security team') the matrix subject following prepositional gerund phrase.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi danh động từ lơ lửng: Lỗ hổng bảo mật không thể 'mổ xẻ mã độc', đội ngũ an ninh phải làm chủ ngữ.
- **Formula**: `After + -ing Phrase, Human Agent Subject + Active Verb + Object`
- **Analysis**: A passive main clause following a temporal prepositional gerund phrase falsely attributes the gerund's action to the grammatical subject ('the zero-day vulnerability'). Restoring the agent ('our security team') cures the semantic absurdity.
- **Exemplar**: Upon completing the forensic autopsy of the insolvent exchange, the court-appointed liquidator filed claims against the former directors.

## Squinting Modifier Disambiguation
- **ID**: gram-prec-12
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: Board members who review compensation packages frequently express severe dissatisfaction.
- **Transformation**: Board members who frequently review compensation packages express severe dissatisfaction.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Disambiguate adverb 'frequently' so it clearly modifies 'review' (intended meaning: the reviewing is frequent, not the complaining).
- **Vietnamese**: Khắc phục bổ ngữ mập mờ (Squinting Modifier): Di chuyển 'frequently' để làm rõ nghĩa. Ở đây, nghĩa cần diễn đạt là họ xem xét thường xuyên (không phải phàn nàn thường xuyên).
- **Formula**: `Subject + who + Adverb + Verb... + Main Predicate`
- **Analysis**: A squinting modifier is sandwiched ambiguously between two syntactic verbs, making it impossible to determine whether 'frequently' modifies the preceding verb 'review' or the succeeding verb 'express'. Moving it inside the relative clause ('who frequently review') resolves the ambiguity in the intended reading; if the writer meant that the complaining is frequent, the valid repair would instead be to move it to the end ('...express severe dissatisfaction frequently').
- **Exemplar**: Executives who consistently communicate clear architectural boundaries inspire enduring organizational trust.

## Deliberate Rhetorical Ellipsis in Comparative Contrast
- **ID**: gram-prec-13
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The senior partner authored eight landmark antitrust briefs, while the junior associate authored only two landmark antitrust briefs.
- **Transformation**: The senior partner authored eight landmark antitrust briefs; the junior associate, only two.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Ellipt identical verb and noun phrase using comma of gapping and semicolon coordination.
- **Vietnamese**: Tỉnh lược tu từ (Rhetorical Ellipsis / Gapping): Dùng dấu phẩy thay thế động từ bị lặp lại trong vế đối xứng.
- **Formula**: `Clause 1; Subject 2, + Contrasting Constituent`
- **Analysis**: Gapping ellipsis removes redundant repetitive verbs ('authored') and direct objects ('landmark antitrust briefs'), using a gapping comma to signal structural omission and creating an incisive rhetorical antithesis.
- **Exemplar**: The treasury secretary advocated bold fiscal expansion; the central bank governor, immediate austerity.

## Subjunctive-Indicative Mood Collision Correction
- **ID**: gram-prec-14
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The charter requires that every delegate is registered and that each votes according to their constituency.
- **Transformation**: The charter requires that every delegate be registered and that each vote according to their constituency.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Harmonize coordinated mandative subjunctive clauses into invariant bare forms 'be' and 'vote'.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi xung đột thức giả định và chỉ định: Cả hai mệnh đề phụ thuộc 'requires that' đều phải ở thức giả định.
- **Formula**: `requires that + Subject 1 + Base Verb... and that + Subject 2 + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: When coordinating multiple dependent content clauses under a single governing mandative verb ('requires that'), writers frequently lapse into the indicative mood in the second clause. Both clauses must maintain strict subjunctive morphology.
- **Exemplar**: The treaty mandates that verification inspectors be granted unimpeded access and that host governments abstain from interference.

## Asymmetrical Nominalization vs. Verbal Coordination
- **ID**: gram-prec-15
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The transformation plan entails the restructuring of internal debt and to divest non-core semiconductor assets.
- **Transformation**: The transformation plan entails restructuring internal debt and divesting non-core semiconductor assets.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Eliminate clash between prepositional noun phrase 'the restructuring of' and infinitive 'to divest' by rewriting both coordinate objects as bare gerund (-ing) phrases.
- **Vietnamese**: Đồng bộ hóa danh từ hóa và động từ: Chuyển toàn bộ thành cụm danh động từ (restructuring... divesting...).
- **Formula**: `Subject + entails + Gerund Phrase 1 and Gerund Phrase 2`
- **Analysis**: Pairing a Latinate nominalization with an infinitive phrase produces clumsy, asymmetric syntax. Harmonizing both coordinate objects into direct gerund phrases creates aerodynamic structural balance.
- **Exemplar**: The turnaround strategy necessitates renegotiating credit terms and consolidating redundant manufacturing facilities.

## Subject-Verb Agreement with 'The number of' vs 'A number of'
- **ID**: gram-prec-16
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The number of complaints lodged against the contractor have tripled, and a number of residents is now considering legal action.
- **Transformation**: The number of complaints lodged against the contractor has tripled, and a number of residents are now considering legal action.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'The number' is a singular head noun; in 'a number of', the plural noun after 'of' is the head and governs the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Hòa hợp chủ ngữ và động từ: 'The number of + danh từ số nhiều' (con số) đi với động từ số ít; 'A number of + danh từ số nhiều' (một số, nhiều) đi với động từ số nhiều.
- **Formula**: `The number of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb | A number of + Plural Noun + Plural Verb`
- **Analysis**: In 'the number of complaints', the head is 'number' (a single quantity), so the verb is singular despite the plural noun and the intervening modifier 'lodged against the contractor'. 'A number of' has become a fixed quantifier meaning 'several', so the following plural noun is the notional head and takes a plural verb. Similar head-identification problems arise with 'the majority of' and 'none of', where usage varies: 'none of the reports is' is the traditional formal choice, but 'none of the reports are' is also standard.
- **Exemplar**: The number of applicants has fallen sharply, yet a number of universities are still expanding their intake.

## Ambiguous Pronoun Reference Resolution
- **ID**: gram-prec-17
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The chief executive had written the quarterly report. When the chief executive met the auditor, she insisted that her figures were accurate.
- **Transformation**: The chief executive insisted to the auditor that her own figures were accurate.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Two singular female antecedents precede 'she' and 'her'; the intended referent is the chief executive (she wrote the report), so restructure so that she is the only salient candidate.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi đại từ quy chiếu mơ hồ: khi có hai danh từ cùng giống, cùng số đứng trước, cần lặp lại danh từ hoặc tái cấu trúc câu để đại từ chỉ có một tiền ngữ duy nhất.
- **Formula**: `Repeat the antecedent noun | Recast so the antecedent is the clause subject | Bind the possessive with 'own' or use a reflexive`
- **Analysis**: A pronoun is ambiguous whenever more than one preceding noun phrase matches it in person, number and gender; readers then default to the subject or to the most recent noun, and either guess may be wrong. Repair strategies include repeating the noun, recasting the sentence so that the intended antecedent is the subject of the same clause, or adding 'own' to tie the possessive to that subject. Singular 'they' can create the same ambiguity when both antecedents are unspecified for gender.
- **Exemplar**: When the minister briefed the ambassador, the ambassador asked for the proposal in writing.

## Faulty Predication ('The reason is because')
- **ID**: gram-prec-18
- **Mode**: SYNTACTIC_REPAIR
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The reason the pilot project stalled is because the regional offices were never consulted.
- **Transformation**: The reason the pilot project stalled is that the regional offices were never consulted.
- **Grammatical Cue**: After 'The reason... is', use a 'that' clause; 'because' duplicates the causal meaning already carried by 'reason'.
- **Vietnamese**: Sửa lỗi vị ngữ không tương hợp: sau 'The reason... is' dùng mệnh đề 'that', không dùng 'because' vì bản thân 'reason' đã mang nghĩa nguyên nhân.
- **Formula**: `The reason (why/that) + Clause + is + that + Clause | Subject + Verb + because + Clause`
- **Analysis**: The copula 'is' equates its subject with its complement, so the complement should be a noun-like element: a 'that' clause qualifies, whereas a 'because' clause is an adverbial of cause, producing both a predication mismatch and a redundancy. 'The reason is because' is common in speech and even appears in respected writers, but it is widely censured in edited formal prose. The cleaner alternatives are 'The reason is that...' or simply 'The project stalled because...'.
- **Exemplar**: The reason the merger failed was that the two firms could never agree on who would make the final decisions.

# Mode: A1 Foundations

> [!NOTE]
> **Mẹo phản xạ nhanh cho người Việt (Rule of Thumb)**: Tiếng Việt không biến đổi động từ và không cần trợ từ, nhưng trong tiếng Anh:
> 1. Tính từ **bắt buộc** đi kèm 'be' (*"She is pretty"*, không nói *"She pretty"*).
> 2. Với động từ thường, đặt câu hỏi và phủ định **bắt buộc mượn trợ động từ** 'do/does' (*"Do you like...?"*, không nói *"You like... or not?"*).

## Negative with 'am'
- **ID**: gram-a1-1
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: I am a student.
- **Transformation**: I am not a student.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add 'not' after am, is or are.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với 'be': thêm 'not' sau am/is/are. Tiếng Việt dùng 'không phải là', còn tiếng Anh vẫn giữ động từ 'be'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + not + noun`
- **Analysis**: To make a negative sentence with 'be', we put 'not' after the verb. The verb 'be' stays in the sentence.
- **Exemplar**: She is not a doctor.

## Question with 'is'
- **ID**: gram-a1-2
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: She is a teacher.
- **Transformation**: Is she a teacher?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put 'is' before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với 'be': đưa am/is/are lên trước chủ ngữ. Tiếng Việt thêm 'không?' ở cuối câu, tiếng Anh đổi trật tự từ.
- **Formula**: `Am/Is/Are + subject + noun?`
- **Analysis**: In a question with 'be', the verb comes first. The subject comes after the verb.
- **Exemplar**: Are you a student?

## Negative with 'have'
- **ID**: gram-a1-3
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: He has a car.
- **Transformation**: He does not have a car.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'does not' and 'have' for he, she and it.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với 'have': dùng do not/does not + have. Với he/she/it dùng 'does not', và 'has' trở lại thành 'have'. Tiếng Việt chỉ cần thêm 'không'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + do/does + not + have + noun`
- **Analysis**: We use 'do' or 'does' to make a negative with 'have'. After 'does not', the verb is 'have', not 'has'.
- **Exemplar**: I do not have a phone.

## Question with 'have'
- **ID**: gram-a1-4
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: You have a sister.
- **Transformation**: Do you have a sister?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Start with 'Do' or 'Does' before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với 'have': thêm Do/Does ở đầu câu, động từ giữ nguyên là 'have'. Tiếng Việt dùng 'có ... không?'.
- **Formula**: `Do/Does + subject + have + noun?`
- **Analysis**: We use 'do' or 'does' to ask a question with 'have'. The verb 'have' stays in its base form.
- **Exemplar**: Does she have a brother?

## Question with 'there is'
- **ID**: gram-a1-5
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: There is a cat in the garden.
- **Transformation**: Is there a cat in the garden?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put 'is' before 'there'.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với 'there is': đưa 'is' lên trước 'there'. Tiếng Việt dùng 'có ... không?', tiếng Anh đảo 'is' lên đầu câu.
- **Formula**: `Is there + noun + place?`
- **Analysis**: In a question, 'is' comes before 'there'. We use 'there is' for one thing.
- **Exemplar**: Is there a bank near here?

## Negative with 'there are'
- **ID**: gram-a1-6
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: There are apples in the bag.
- **Transformation**: There are no apples in the bag.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'no' before the plural noun.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với 'there are': dùng 'no' trước danh từ số nhiều. Tiếng Việt nói 'không có'; tiếng Anh vẫn giữ 'there are'.
- **Formula**: `There are + no + plural noun`
- **Analysis**: We use 'there are' for more than one thing. We add 'no' before the noun to say there are none.
- **Exemplar**: There are no chairs in the room.

## Negative with 'can'
- **ID**: gram-a1-7
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: I can swim.
- **Transformation**: I cannot swim.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add 'not' after 'can'.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với 'can': thêm 'not' sau can (cannot hoặc can't). Động từ chính giữ nguyên, không thêm 'to'. Tiếng Việt dùng 'không thể' hoặc 'không biết'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + cannot + base verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Can' shows ability. We add 'not' after it and use the base verb.
- **Exemplar**: He cannot drive.

## Question with 'can'
- **ID**: gram-a1-8
- **Mode**: A1_BE_AND_HAVE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: She can cook.
- **Transformation**: Can she cook?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put 'can' before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với 'can': đưa 'can' lên trước chủ ngữ. Không cần 'do/does'. Tiếng Việt thêm 'được không?' hoặc 'có thể ... không?'.
- **Formula**: `Can + subject + base verb?`
- **Analysis**: In a question with 'can', the word 'can' comes first. The main verb stays in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Can you swim?

## He or she and the verb
- **ID**: gram-a1-9
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: I work in a shop.
- **Transformation**: She works in a shop.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Change 'I' to 'she' and add -s to the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Với he/she/it, thêm -s hoặc -es vào động từ. Tiếng Việt không đổi động từ theo chủ ngữ, nên người học hay quên -s.
- **Formula**: `He/She/It + verb + s`
- **Analysis**: In the present simple, the verb changes after he, she and it. We add -s to the base verb.
- **Exemplar**: He lives in a small town.

## Negative with 'do not'
- **ID**: gram-a1-10
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: They like fish.
- **Transformation**: They do not like fish.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'do not' before the base verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định thì hiện tại đơn: dùng do not + động từ nguyên mẫu. Tiếng Việt chỉ thêm 'không' trước động từ.
- **Formula**: `Subject + do not + base verb`
- **Analysis**: We use 'do not' to make a negative with I, you, we and they. The main verb stays in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: We do not eat meat.

## Negative with 'does not'
- **ID**: gram-a1-11
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: He likes tea.
- **Transformation**: He does not like tea.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'does not' and the base verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với he/she/it: dùng does not + động từ nguyên mẫu. Chữ -s chuyển sang 'does', nên động từ chính không còn -s.
- **Formula**: `He/She/It + does not + base verb`
- **Analysis**: With he, she and it, we use 'does not'. The main verb has no -s after it.
- **Exemplar**: She does not work here.

## Yes/no question with 'do'
- **ID**: gram-a1-12
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: You live here.
- **Transformation**: Do you live here?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put 'Do' at the start of the sentence.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi Yes/No: thêm Do ở đầu câu, động từ giữ nguyên mẫu. Tiếng Việt thêm 'không?' ở cuối câu.
- **Formula**: `Do + subject + base verb?`
- **Analysis**: We use 'do' to ask questions with I, you, we and they. The word order is Do, subject, verb.
- **Exemplar**: Do they play football?

## Yes/no question with 'does'
- **ID**: gram-a1-13
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: She plays tennis.
- **Transformation**: Does she play tennis?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'Does' and remove the -s from the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với he/she/it: dùng Does ở đầu câu và bỏ -s ở động từ chính. Chỉ có một chỗ thể hiện ngôi thứ ba, đó là 'does'.
- **Formula**: `Does + he/she/it + base verb?`
- **Analysis**: With he, she and it, we start the question with 'does'. The main verb goes back to the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Does he like coffee?

## Wh-question with 'do'
- **ID**: gram-a1-14
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: You eat lunch at noon.
- **Transformation**: When do you eat lunch?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Ask about the time ('at noon'): put 'When' first, then 'do'.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi Wh với thì hiện tại đơn: Từ để hỏi + do/does + chủ ngữ + động từ nguyên mẫu. Tiếng Việt để từ hỏi ở cuối câu, tiếng Anh đưa lên đầu.
- **Formula**: `Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb?`
- **Analysis**: The question word comes at the start. Then we use 'do' or 'does' and the subject.
- **Exemplar**: Where do you live?

## Adverb 'always'
- **ID**: gram-a1-15
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: I eat breakfast.
- **Transformation**: I always eat breakfast.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put 'always' before the main verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Trạng từ tần suất (always, usually, never) đứng trước động từ chính. Tiếng Việt thường để 'luôn luôn' ở nhiều vị trí khác nhau.
- **Formula**: `Subject + always + verb`
- **Analysis**: Adverbs of frequency tell us how often something happens. They go before the main verb.
- **Exemplar**: She usually walks to school.

## Adverb with 'be'
- **ID**: gram-a1-16
- **Mode**: A1_PRESENT_SIMPLE
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: He is late.
- **Transformation**: He is never late.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add 'never' and put it after 'is', 'am' or 'are'.
- **Vietnamese**: Với động từ 'be', trạng từ tần suất đứng sau am/is/are, khác với động từ thường (đứng trước). Tiếng Việt thường đặt 'không bao giờ' trước động từ.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + adverb`
- **Analysis**: With the verb 'be', the adverb comes after it. This is different from other verbs.
- **Exemplar**: I am always happy.

## Plural with -es
- **ID**: gram-a1-17
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: I have one box.
- **Transformation**: I have two boxes.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add -es to nouns that end in -x, -s, -ch or -sh.
- **Vietnamese**: Số nhiều: thêm -es vào danh từ tận cùng bằng -x, -s, -ch, -sh. Tiếng Việt không đổi danh từ, chỉ thêm số đếm như 'hai', 'nhiều'.
- **Formula**: `number + noun + es`
- **Analysis**: Most nouns take -s in the plural. Nouns that end in -x, -s, -ch or -sh take -es.
- **Exemplar**: She has three watches.

## 'an' before a vowel sound
- **ID**: gram-a1-18
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: She has one egg.
- **Transformation**: She has an egg.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'an' before a vowel sound.
- **Vietnamese**: Mạo từ 'an' dùng trước từ bắt đầu bằng âm nguyên âm (không phải chữ cái nguyên âm): 'an egg', 'an hour' (h câm), nhưng 'a university' (bắt đầu bằng âm /j/). Tiếng Việt dùng 'một' cho mọi danh từ, còn tiếng Anh phải chọn a hoặc an.
- **Formula**: `an + noun starting with a vowel sound`
- **Analysis**: We use 'a' before a consonant sound and 'an' before a vowel sound. 'Egg' starts with a vowel sound. The sound matters, not the spelling: 'hour' starts with a vowel sound (silent h), so we say 'an hour'; 'university' starts with the consonant sound /j/, so we say 'a university'.
- **Exemplar**: I eat an apple, then wait an hour for a university bus.

## 'a' and 'the'
- **ID**: gram-a1-19
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Give me a book.
- **Transformation**: Give me the book.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'the' for one clear thing.
- **Vietnamese**: 'A' dùng khi nói về một thứ chung chung, 'the' dùng khi cả hai người biết rõ thứ đó. Tiếng Việt không có mạo từ, thường dùng 'cái', 'quyển' để chỉ rõ.
- **Formula**: `the + noun (known thing)`
- **Analysis**: We use 'a' for something new. We use 'the' when the listener knows which thing we mean.
- **Exemplar**: Open the door, please.

## 'this' and 'that'
- **ID**: gram-a1-20
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: This is my bag.
- **Transformation**: That is my bag.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'that' for a thing far from you.
- **Vietnamese**: 'This' chỉ vật ở gần, 'that' chỉ vật ở xa. Tiếng Việt dùng 'này' và 'kia', 'đó' tương tự.
- **Formula**: `This/That + is + noun`
- **Analysis**: 'This' is for things near us. 'That' is for things far from us.
- **Exemplar**: That is my teacher.

## 'this' and 'these'
- **ID**: gram-a1-21
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: This is my friend.
- **Transformation**: These are my friends.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Change 'this is' to 'these are' for many things.
- **Vietnamese**: Số nhiều của 'this' là 'these', và 'is' đổi thành 'are'. Danh từ phải thêm -s. Tiếng Việt chỉ thêm 'những' hoặc 'các'.
- **Formula**: `These + are + plural noun`
- **Analysis**: 'These' is the plural of 'this'. We use 'are' and a plural noun with it.
- **Exemplar**: These are my shoes.

## Wh-question with 'what'
- **ID**: gram-a1-22
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: She likes pizza.
- **Transformation**: What does she like?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace the object with 'what' and put it first.
- **Vietnamese**: Hỏi về vật: thay tân ngữ bằng 'What' và đưa lên đầu câu, sau đó dùng do/does. Tiếng Việt giữ 'gì' ở cuối câu, tiếng Anh đưa 'what' lên đầu.
- **Formula**: `What + do/does + subject + verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'What' asks about a thing. It goes at the start of the question, before 'does'.
- **Exemplar**: What do you eat for lunch?

## Place with 'on' and 'where'
- **ID**: gram-a1-23
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: The book is on the table.
- **Transformation**: Where is the book?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use 'where' to ask about a place.
- **Vietnamese**: Hỏi vị trí: dùng 'Where' + is/are + chủ ngữ. Giới từ chỉ nơi chốn: in (trong), on (trên), under (dưới), next to (bên cạnh).
- **Formula**: `Where + is/are + subject?`
- **Analysis**: 'Where' asks about a place. The answer often has a preposition like 'on', 'in' or 'under'.
- **Exemplar**: The cat is under the chair.

## Time with 'at' and 'what time'
- **ID**: gram-a1-24
- **Mode**: A1_NOUNS_AND_QUESTIONS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: The class starts at nine.
- **Transformation**: What time does the class start?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Ask 'What time' and use 'does'.
- **Vietnamese**: Hỏi giờ: 'What time' + do/does + chủ ngữ + động từ. Giờ cụ thể dùng 'at', ngày dùng 'on', tháng dùng 'in'. Tiếng Việt dùng 'lúc' cho tất cả.
- **Formula**: `What time + do/does + subject + verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'What time' asks about the clock. We use 'at' with a time in the answer.
- **Exemplar**: We have lunch at twelve.

# Mode: A2 Essentials

> [!NOTE]
> **Mẹo phản xạ nhanh cho người Việt (Rule of Thumb)**: 
> 1. **Luôn chia thì cho động từ** kể cả khi trong câu đã có từ chỉ thời gian (*"Yesterday I went"*, không nói *"Yesterday I go"*).
> 2. Luôn phát âm rõ âm đuôi phụ âm và đuôi **-s / -ed** để tạo đà nối âm tự nhiên và tránh lỗi nuốt ngữ pháp khi nói.

## Past Simple: Regular Verb
- **ID**: gram-a2-1
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I walk to school every day.
- **Transformation**: I walked to school yesterday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add -ed to the verb to talk about the past.
- **Vietnamese**: Thì quá khứ đơn với động từ có quy tắc: thêm -ed vào động từ. Tiếng Việt không đổi động từ mà dùng từ như "đã" hoặc "hôm qua".
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb-ed + Object`
- **Analysis**: The verb 'walk' becomes 'walked'. We use the past simple for a finished action.
- **Exemplar**: She cleaned the kitchen last night.

## Past Simple: Spelling of Regular Verbs
- **ID**: gram-a2-2
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: We study English and we plan a trip.
- **Transformation**: We studied English and we planned a trip.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Change y to ied, and double the last letter in short verbs.
- **Vietnamese**: Quy tắc chính tả khi thêm -ed: study đổi thành studied, plan gấp đôi phụ âm thành planned. Tiếng Việt không có quy tắc này.
- **Formula**: `consonant + y → ied; short verb: double the last consonant + ed`
- **Analysis**: 'Study' ends in consonant plus y, so it becomes 'studied'. 'Plan' is a short verb, so we write 'planned'.
- **Exemplar**: He stopped the car and carried the bags.

## Past Simple: Irregular Verb
- **ID**: gram-a2-3
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I go to the market and buy some fruit.
- **Transformation**: I went to the market and bought some fruit.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the special past form of irregular verbs.
- **Vietnamese**: Động từ bất quy tắc có dạng quá khứ riêng (go - went, buy - bought) và phải học thuộc. Tiếng Việt chỉ thêm "đã" nên không có dạng riêng này.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Past Form (irregular) + Object`
- **Analysis**: 'Go' becomes 'went' and 'buy' becomes 'bought'. These verbs do not take -ed.
- **Exemplar**: They ate rice and drank tea at lunch.

## Past Simple: Negative
- **ID**: gram-a2-4
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: She visited her aunt on Sunday.
- **Transformation**: She did not visit her aunt on Sunday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use did not and the base form of the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu phủ định quá khứ: did not (didn't) + động từ nguyên mẫu. Động từ trở về dạng gốc, không còn -ed. Tiếng Việt dùng "không" hoặc "chưa".
- **Formula**: `Subject + did not + Base Verb + Object`
- **Analysis**: 'Did not' shows the past, so the main verb stays in the base form. We say 'did not visit', not 'did not visited'.
- **Exemplar**: I did not see my friends last weekend.

## Past Simple: Yes/No Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-5
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: You watched a film last night.
- **Transformation**: Did you watch a film last night?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put did at the start and use the base form of the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi Yes/No ở quá khứ: Did + chủ ngữ + động từ nguyên mẫu? Tiếng Việt thêm "không" hoặc "chưa" ở cuối câu, còn tiếng Anh đưa Did lên đầu.
- **Formula**: `Did + Subject + Base Verb + Object?`
- **Analysis**: 'Did' comes before the subject. The main verb goes back to the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Did he call you this morning?

## Past Simple: Wh- Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-6
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: He bought a new phone yesterday.
- **Transformation**: What did he buy yesterday?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put the question word first, then did, the subject and the base verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi có từ để hỏi ở quá khứ: Wh-word + did + chủ ngữ + động từ nguyên mẫu? Trong tiếng Việt từ để hỏi ("cái gì") thường đứng cuối câu, còn tiếng Anh đưa lên đầu.
- **Formula**: `Wh-word + did + Subject + Base Verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'What' replaces 'a new phone' and goes to the front. 'Did' stays before the subject, so 'buy' is in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Where did you go on holiday?

## Past Continuous
- **ID**: gram-a2-7
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I watch TV at eight o'clock.
- **Transformation**: I was watching TV at eight o'clock last night.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use was or were and the -ing form for an action in progress in the past.
- **Vietnamese**: Quá khứ tiếp diễn: was/were + V-ing, chỉ hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm trong quá khứ. Tiếng Việt dùng "đang" kèm "lúc đó".
- **Formula**: `Subject + was/were + Verb-ing`
- **Analysis**: We use 'was' with I, he, she and it. We use 'were' with you, we and they.
- **Exemplar**: They were playing football at four o'clock.

## Past Continuous with Past Simple
- **ID**: gram-a2-8
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I cooked dinner. The phone rang.
- **Transformation**: I was cooking dinner when the phone rang.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use past continuous for the longer action and past simple for the short action.
- **Vietnamese**: Hành động dài đang diễn ra (was cooking) thì bị hành động ngắn chen vào (rang), nối bằng when. Giống cấu trúc "đang... thì..." trong tiếng Việt.
- **Formula**: `Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + when + Subject + Past Simple`
- **Analysis**: The long action is 'was cooking'. The short action is 'rang'.
- **Exemplar**: She was walking home when it started to rain.

## Future with Going To: Plan
- **ID**: gram-a2-9
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I visit my grandmother on Saturday.
- **Transformation**: I am going to visit my grandmother on Saturday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use am, is or are + going to + base verb for a plan.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng be going to + động từ nguyên mẫu cho dự định đã có từ trước. Tương đương "định" hoặc "sẽ" trong tiếng Việt.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + going to + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: The plan was made before now. The verb after 'to' is in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: We are going to have a picnic tomorrow.

## Future with Going To: Negative
- **ID**: gram-a2-10
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: She is going to buy a new bag.
- **Transformation**: She is not going to buy a new bag.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put not after am, is or are.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định của be going to: thêm not sau am/is/are. Tiếng Việt dùng "không định" hoặc "sẽ không".
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: We keep 'going to' and add 'not' after 'is'. The main verb 'buy' does not change.
- **Exemplar**: They are not going to travel this summer.

## Future with Going To: Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-11
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: You are going to cook tonight.
- **Transformation**: Are you going to cook tonight?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Move am, is or are before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với be going to: đảo am/is/are lên trước chủ ngữ. Tiếng Việt thêm "không" ở cuối câu hoặc dùng "có... không".
- **Formula**: `Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to + Base Verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'Are' moves to the front of the sentence. The rest of the sentence stays the same.
- **Exemplar**: Is she going to come to the party?

## Future with Will: Decision or Offer
- **ID**: gram-a2-12
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I open the window.
- **Transformation**: I will open the window.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use will + base verb for a quick decision or an offer.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng will + động từ nguyên mẫu khi quyết định ngay lúc nói hoặc đưa ra lời đề nghị. Dịch là "sẽ" hoặc "để tôi".
- **Formula**: `Subject + will + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Will' does not change for any subject. The verb after 'will' is always in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: It is hot, so I will turn on the fan.

## Future with Will: Negative
- **ID**: gram-a2-13
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: He will come to the meeting.
- **Transformation**: He will not come to the meeting.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put not after will. The short form is won't.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với will: will not (won't) + động từ nguyên mẫu. Tiếng Việt dùng "sẽ không".
- **Formula**: `Subject + will not (won't) + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: We add 'not' after 'will'. The short form 'won't' is common in speaking.
- **Exemplar**: I won't be late for class.

## Future with Will: Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-14
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: It will rain tomorrow.
- **Transformation**: Will it rain tomorrow?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put will before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với will: đưa will lên trước chủ ngữ. Tiếng Việt thêm "không" ở cuối: "Ngày mai trời sẽ mưa không?"
- **Formula**: `Will + Subject + Base Verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'Will' moves to the front. The main verb stays in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Will you help me with my homework?

## Present Continuous for Future Plans
- **ID**: gram-a2-15
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I meet my friend at six.
- **Transformation**: I am meeting my friend at six tonight.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use am, is or are + verb-ing with a future time for a fixed plan.
- **Vietnamese**: Hiện tại tiếp diễn dùng cho kế hoạch đã sắp xếp trong tương lai gần, thường có từ chỉ thời gian như "tonight" hoặc "tomorrow". Tiếng Việt dùng "sẽ" hoặc "định".
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + future time`
- **Analysis**: The word 'tonight' shows the future. The plan is fixed, for example with a time or a place.
- **Exemplar**: We are flying to Da Nang next week.

## Present Continuous: Now vs Future Plan
- **ID**: gram-a2-16
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: He is cooking dinner now.
- **Transformation**: He is cooking dinner with his friends tomorrow.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Change the time word from now to a future time.
- **Vietnamese**: Cùng một cấu trúc is cooking, nhưng now chỉ việc đang xảy ra, còn tomorrow chỉ kế hoạch tương lai. Thời gian trong câu quyết định nghĩa.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + now (present) / tomorrow (future)`
- **Analysis**: With 'now', the action is happening at this moment. With 'tomorrow', it is a plan for the future.
- **Exemplar**: I am studying now, and I am working on Monday.

## Comparative: Short Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-17
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: My brother is tall. I am not very tall.
- **Transformation**: My brother is taller than me.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add -er to a short adjective and use than.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh hơn với tính từ ngắn: adj-er + than. Tiếng Việt dùng "hơn": "cao hơn tôi".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + Adjective-er + than + Noun`
- **Analysis**: 'Tall' becomes 'taller'. We use 'than' to compare two things.
- **Exemplar**: This bag is cheaper than that bag.

## Comparative: Long Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-18
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: The train is comfortable. The bus is not comfortable.
- **Transformation**: The train is more comfortable than the bus.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use more before a long adjective such as 'comfortable', with no -er.
- **Vietnamese**: Với tính từ dài (ba âm tiết trở lên) dùng more + adj + than, không thêm -er. Tính từ hai âm tiết thì đa số dùng more (more careful, more modern), trừ những từ tận cùng bằng -y (happy - happier) và một số từ như narrow, simple, clever thường dùng -er. Tiếng Việt vẫn chỉ dùng "hơn".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + more + Adjective + than + Noun`
- **Analysis**: 'Comfortable' has three syllables, so we use 'more'. We never say 'more comfortabler'. Most adjectives with three or more syllables take 'more'; two-syllable adjectives mostly take 'more' too, except those ending in -y ('happier') and a few such as 'narrow', 'simple' and 'clever'.
- **Exemplar**: English is more interesting than maths for me.

## Comparative: Irregular and Spelling
- **ID**: gram-a2-19
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Today is hot and good. Yesterday was not so hot and not so good.
- **Transformation**: Today is hotter and better than yesterday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Compare today with yesterday: good becomes better, and short adjectives like hot double the last letter.
- **Vietnamese**: Một số tính từ đặc biệt: good - better, bad - worse. Tính từ ngắn như hot, big gấp đôi phụ âm cuối: hotter, bigger. Tiếng Việt không đổi hình thức từ.
- **Formula**: `good → better; bad → worse; consonant + vowel + consonant → double + er`
- **Analysis**: 'Good' has a special form, 'better'. 'Hot' becomes 'hotter' with a double t.
- **Exemplar**: My new phone is bigger and better than my old phone.

## Superlative: Short Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-20
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Mai is taller than her friends.
- **Transformation**: Mai is the tallest girl in her class.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the + adjective-est for the number one in a group.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh nhất với tính từ ngắn: the + adj-est + in/of + nhóm. Tiếng Việt dùng "nhất": "cao nhất lớp".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + the + Adjective-est + in/of + group`
- **Analysis**: 'Tall' becomes 'the tallest'. We use 'in' with a place or a group, for example 'in her class'.
- **Exemplar**: This is the cheapest shop in town.

## Superlative: Long Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-21
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: This film is more exciting than the other films.
- **Transformation**: This is the most exciting film of all.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the most before a long adjective.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh nhất với tính từ dài: the most + adj. Không nói "the excitingest". Tiếng Việt vẫn dùng "nhất".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + the most + Adjective + Noun`
- **Analysis**: 'Exciting' is a long adjective, so we use 'the most'. We use 'of all' to mean 'compared with all the others'.
- **Exemplar**: She is the most helpful person in our team.

## Modal Can: Ability
- **ID**: gram-a2-22
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I swim.
- **Transformation**: I can swim, but I cannot dance.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use can or cannot + base verb for ability.
- **Vietnamese**: Can + động từ nguyên mẫu chỉ khả năng ("có thể", "biết"). Phủ định là cannot (can't). Can không thêm -s và không cần trợ động từ.
- **Formula**: `Subject + can/cannot + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Can' stays the same for every subject, so we say 'she can swim'. We make a question with 'Can you swim?'.
- **Exemplar**: He can cook rice, but he cannot cook fish.

## Modal Should: Advice
- **ID**: gram-a2-23
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: You go to bed early.
- **Transformation**: You should go to bed early.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use should + base verb to give advice.
- **Vietnamese**: Should + động từ nguyên mẫu dùng để khuyên: "nên". Phủ định là should not (shouldn't) nghĩa là "không nên".
- **Formula**: `Subject + should/shouldn't + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Should' gives a friendly suggestion. It is not as strong as 'must'.
- **Exemplar**: You should drink more water when it is hot.

## Modal Must and Have To: Rules
- **ID**: gram-a2-24
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Students wear a uniform at school.
- **Transformation**: Students must wear a uniform at school.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the modal 'must' (not 'have to') for a school rule.
- **Vietnamese**: Must và have to đều có nghĩa "phải". Must là quy định mạnh, have to là điều cần thiết từ bên ngoài. Phủ định khác nhau: mustn't là "cấm", don't have to là "không cần".
- **Formula**: `Subject + must/have to + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Must' is strong and does not change. With 'he' or 'she', we say 'has to'. In 'Students must wear a uniform' the sentence 'Students have to wear a uniform' is also correct; this exercise practises 'must'.
- **Exemplar**: She has to get up early on Monday.

# Mode: B1 Core Grammar

> [!NOTE]
> **Phân biệt then chốt cho người Việt**: Tiếng Việt dùng chữ "đã" cho mọi hành động đã qua. Tiếng Anh phân biệt rõ:
> - **Quá khứ đơn (Past Simple)**: Kể lại một sự việc đã chấm dứt hoàn toàn tại một mốc thời gian xác định trong quá khứ.
> - **Hiện tại hoàn thành (Present Perfect)**: Hành động xảy ra trước đó nhưng **kết quả hoặc hậu quả của nó vẫn đang ảnh hưởng trực tiếp tới hiện tại** (*I have lost my key* = bây giờ tôi vẫn chưa vào được nhà).

## Present Perfect: Result Now
- **ID**: gram-b1-1
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I lost my keys, so I can't open the door.
- **Transformation**: I have lost my keys, so I can't open the door.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Past action with a result that matters now uses have/has + past participle.
- **Vietnamese**: Hiện tại hoàn thành: hành động trong quá khứ nhưng kết quả còn ở hiện tại. Tiếng Việt dùng 'đã' cho mọi thì quá khứ, còn tiếng Anh phải chọn: 'have lost' (còn ảnh hưởng bây giờ) khác 'lost' (chỉ kể chuyện đã qua).
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: The keys are still missing now, so the speaker uses the present perfect. The result is connected to the present.
- **Exemplar**: My sister has broken her phone, so she is using mine.

## Present Perfect with 'for'
- **ID**: gram-b1-2
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I started living here five years ago.
- **Transformation**: I have lived here for five years.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'For' + a length of time shows how long a situation has continued until now.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng 'for' + khoảng thời gian (for five years = được năm năm). Tiếng Việt nói 'sống ở đây được năm năm' nên dễ dùng thì quá khứ; tiếng Anh dùng have + V3 vì việc vẫn tiếp diễn.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + Past Participle + for + period of time`
- **Analysis**: The situation started in the past and continues now. 'For five years' tells us the length of time.
- **Exemplar**: They have known each other for ten years.

## Present Perfect with 'since'
- **ID**: gram-b1-3
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: She began working at the bank in 2019.
- **Transformation**: She has worked at the bank since 2019.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Since' + a starting point in time shows when a situation began.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng 'since' + mốc thời gian (since 2019 = từ năm 2019). Khác với 'for' (khoảng thời gian), 'since' chỉ điểm bắt đầu. Tiếng Việt đều nói 'từ/được', nhưng tiếng Anh phải phân biệt hai từ này.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + Past Participle + since + starting point`
- **Analysis**: The job began in 2019 and she still works there. 'Since' points to the start, not the length.
- **Exemplar**: We have lived in this town since last summer.

## Present Perfect with 'just'
- **ID**: gram-b1-4
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: He arrived a minute ago.
- **Transformation**: He has just arrived.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Just' between have/has and the participle means a very recent action.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Just' nghĩa là 'vừa mới'. Đặt giữa have/has và V3: 'has just arrived' (vừa mới đến). Tiếng Việt đặt 'vừa mới' trước động từ, tiếng Anh cũng đặt trước động từ chính nhưng sau trợ động từ.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + just + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: The action finished a very short time ago. 'Just' goes after has and before the participle.
- **Exemplar**: I have just finished my lunch, so I am not hungry.

## Present Perfect with 'already'
- **ID**: gram-b1-5
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I don't need to cook because my brother cooked dinner earlier.
- **Transformation**: I don't need to cook because my brother has already cooked dinner.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Already' in a positive sentence shows something happened sooner than expected.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Already' nghĩa là 'đã... rồi' trong câu khẳng định, nhấn mạnh việc xong sớm hơn dự kiến. Đặt giữa have/has và V3. Tiếng Việt có 'đã... rồi' bao quanh động từ, tiếng Anh chỉ cần một từ 'already'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + already + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: The dinner is ready now, so no cooking is needed. 'Already' stresses that it is done.
- **Exemplar**: We have already booked the hotel for our trip.

## Present Perfect with 'yet'
- **ID**: gram-b1-6
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Did you finish the report?
- **Transformation**: Have you finished the report yet?
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Yet' at the end of a question or negative sentence asks about something expected.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Yet' dùng trong câu hỏi và câu phủ định, nghĩa là 'chưa/đã... chưa', đặt ở cuối câu. Ví dụ: 'Have you finished yet?' (Bạn xong chưa?), 'I haven't finished yet' (Tôi chưa xong).
- **Formula**: `Have/Has + Subject + Past Participle + yet?`
- **Analysis**: The speaker expects the report to be done soon. 'Yet' goes at the end of the question.
- **Exemplar**: I haven't seen that film yet.

## Present Perfect for Life Experience
- **ID**: gram-b1-7
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: This is my first time eating sushi.
- **Transformation**: I have never eaten sushi before.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Never' with the present perfect talks about life experience up to now.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng have/has + V3 với 'never/ever' để nói về kinh nghiệm sống ('chưa từng', 'đã từng'). Tiếng Việt dùng 'chưa từng/đã từng', tiếng Anh không nêu thời điểm cụ thể.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + never/ever + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: The sentence talks about all of the speaker's life until now. No exact time is given.
- **Exemplar**: Have you ever tried Vietnamese coffee with egg?

## Present Perfect Continuous
- **ID**: gram-b1-8
- **Mode**: B1_PRESENT_PERFECT
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I started waiting for the bus at six, and I am still waiting.
- **Transformation**: I have been waiting for the bus since six.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Have/has been + verb-ing shows an action that began in the past and is still happening.
- **Vietnamese**: Hiện tại hoàn thành tiếp diễn nhấn mạnh hành động kéo dài liên tục đến bây giờ, như 'đang... mãi từ...'. Cấu trúc: have/has been + V-ing, thường đi với for/since.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + been + Verb-ing + for/since...`
- **Analysis**: The waiting began at six and has not stopped. The continuous form shows the action is still in progress.
- **Exemplar**: It has been raining for two hours.

## Zero Conditional: Facts
- **ID**: gram-b1-9
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Water boils when you heat it to 100 degrees.
- **Transformation**: If you heat water to 100 degrees, it boils.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Zero conditional uses present simple in both clauses for facts that are always true.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu điều kiện loại 0 nói về sự thật hiển nhiên: If + hiện tại đơn, hiện tại đơn. Tiếng Việt dùng 'nếu... thì...' và không đổi thì; tiếng Anh cũng giữ hiện tại đơn ở cả hai vế.
- **Formula**: `If + Present Simple, Present Simple`
- **Analysis**: This is always true, so both verbs are in the present simple. 'If' can be replaced by 'when'.
- **Exemplar**: If you mix red and blue, you get purple.

## Zero Conditional: Habits and Rules
- **ID**: gram-b1-10
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Plants die when they don't get water.
- **Transformation**: If plants don't get water, they die.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Zero conditional can also describe a general rule or a result that always follows; both clauses are usually present simple.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu điều kiện loại 0 cũng dùng cho quy luật chung. Cả hai vế thường dùng hiện tại đơn. Lưu ý: 'If plants don't get water, they will die' vẫn đúng ngữ pháp (điều kiện loại 1, dự đoán), nhưng khi nói quy luật chung ta thường dùng hiện tại đơn ở cả hai vế.
- **Formula**: `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + Present Simple`
- **Analysis**: The rule is general, so both clauses are usually in the present simple. 'If plants don't get water, they will die' is also grammatical, but it reads as a prediction (first conditional) rather than a general rule. The if-clause can also come second without a comma.
- **Exemplar**: If I drink coffee late at night, I don't sleep well.

## First Conditional: Warning
- **ID**: gram-b1-11
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Study hard or you will fail the test.
- **Transformation**: If you don't study hard, you will fail the test.
- **Grammatical Cue**: First conditional uses if + present simple, then will + base verb, for a real future result.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu điều kiện loại 1 nói về khả năng có thật ở tương lai: If + hiện tại đơn, will + động từ nguyên mẫu. Tiếng Việt dùng thì hiện tại cho cả hai vế, nhưng tiếng Anh không dùng 'will' sau 'if'.
- **Formula**: `If + Present Simple, will + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: The if-clause uses the present simple, not 'will'. The main clause uses 'will' for the future result.
- **Exemplar**: If you leave now, you will arrive on time.

## First Conditional: Plans
- **ID**: gram-b1-12
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: It might rain tomorrow, so we may stay at home.
- **Transformation**: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use if + present simple for a possible future event and will for the plan.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng 'if + hiện tại đơn' cho sự kiện có thể xảy ra ngày mai, và 'will' cho kết quả. 'If it rains' (không phải 'if it will rain') dịch là 'nếu ngày mai trời mưa'.
- **Formula**: `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + will + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: Rain tomorrow is possible, not certain. The if-clause stays in the present simple even though it is about the future.
- **Exemplar**: If I have time on Saturday, I will visit my grandparents.

## Second Conditional
- **ID**: gram-b1-13
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I don't have a lot of money, so I can't buy a new car.
- **Transformation**: If I had a lot of money, I would buy a new car.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Second conditional uses if + past simple and would + base verb for unreal or unlikely situations.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu điều kiện loại 2 nói về điều không có thật ở hiện tại: If + quá khứ đơn, would + động từ nguyên mẫu. Quá khứ đơn ở đây không chỉ quá khứ, mà chỉ sự giả định. Tiếng Việt dùng 'nếu... thì...' không đổi thì.
- **Formula**: `If + Past Simple, would + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: The speaker does not have much money now, so the situation is imaginary. The past form 'had' shows it is not real.
- **Exemplar**: If I lived closer to the city, I would go there more often.

## Wish + Past Simple
- **ID**: gram-b1-14
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I don't live near the sea, and I'm sad about it.
- **Transformation**: I wish I lived near the sea.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Wish' + past simple expresses a wish about a present situation that is not true.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Wish' + quá khứ đơn diễn tả điều ước trái với thực tế hiện tại ('ước gì'). Tiếng Việt chỉ nói 'ước gì tôi sống gần biển', còn tiếng Anh phải lùi một thì: 'lived', không phải 'live'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + wish + Subject + Past Simple`
- **Analysis**: The speaker does not live near the sea now. The past simple shows the wish is not real. The same structure appears in a formal register in gram-subj-3.
- **Exemplar**: I wish I had more free time.

## 'Unless' in Conditionals
- **ID**: gram-b1-15
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: If you don't hurry, you will miss the train.
- **Transformation**: Unless you hurry, you will miss the train.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Unless' means 'if not' and is followed by a positive verb.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Unless' nghĩa là 'trừ khi / nếu không', đã mang nghĩa phủ định nên động từ phía sau ở dạng khẳng định. Tiếng Việt dùng 'nếu không', nên người học hay viết sai 'unless you don't hurry'.
- **Formula**: `Unless + Subject + Positive Verb, Subject + will + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Unless you hurry' has the same meaning as 'if you do not hurry'. We do not add 'not' after 'unless'.
- **Exemplar**: Unless it rains, we will have the picnic in the park.

## Time Clauses with Future Meaning
- **ID**: gram-b1-16
- **Mode**: B1_CONDITIONALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: First I will finish my homework. Then I will watch TV.
- **Transformation**: I will watch TV after I finish my homework.
- **Grammatical Cue**: After a time word like 'after', 'when' or 'before', we use the present simple for the future.
- **Vietnamese**: Sau 'when, after, before, as soon as' dùng hiện tại đơn dù nghĩa là tương lai. Tiếng Việt nói 'sau khi tôi làm xong bài tập' không đổi thì, nhưng tiếng Anh không dùng 'will' trong mệnh đề thời gian.
- **Formula**: `Main clause (will + Base Verb) + after/when/before + Present Simple`
- **Analysis**: The homework comes first, then the TV. The time clause uses the present simple, not 'will finish'.
- **Exemplar**: I will call you when I get home.

## Present Passive
- **ID**: gram-b1-17
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: People speak English in many countries.
- **Transformation**: English is spoken in many countries.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Present passive uses am/is/are + past participle when the action is more important than the doer.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu bị động hiện tại: am/is/are + V3. Dùng khi người làm không quan trọng. Tiếng Việt dùng 'được/bị' ('Tiếng Anh được nói ở nhiều nước'); tiếng Anh dùng to be + V3.
- **Formula**: `Object + am/is/are + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: The object of the active sentence becomes the subject. We do not need to say who speaks English.
- **Exemplar**: Rice is grown in many parts of Vietnam.

## Past Passive
- **ID**: gram-b1-18
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Someone stole my bike last night.
- **Transformation**: My bike was stolen last night.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Past passive uses was/were + past participle.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu bị động quá khứ: was/were + V3. Tiếng Việt thường thêm 'bị' cho việc không hay ('xe bị lấy cắp') hoặc 'được' cho việc tốt; tiếng Anh dùng cấu trúc giống nhau cho cả hai.
- **Formula**: `Object + was/were + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: We do not know who took the bike, so the passive is natural. The bike is the subject now.
- **Exemplar**: This house was built in 1990.

## Present Perfect Passive
- **ID**: gram-b1-19
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: They have built a new bridge in our town.
- **Transformation**: A new bridge has been built in our town.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Present perfect passive uses have/has + been + past participle.
- **Vietnamese**: Bị động hiện tại hoàn thành: have/has + been + V3. Tiếng Việt nói 'Một cây cầu mới đã được xây'; tiếng Anh phải có 'been' giữa 'has' và V3.
- **Formula**: `Object + have/has + been + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: The bridge now exists, so we use the present perfect. 'Been' is needed in the passive form.
- **Exemplar**: Many new shops have been opened in the city centre.

## Reported Statements
- **ID**: gram-b1-20
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: He said, "I am tired."
- **Transformation**: He said (that) he was tired.
- **Grammatical Cue**: In reported speech, the verb usually moves one tense back and the pronouns change.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu tường thuật: lùi một thì (am -> was) và đổi đại từ (I -> he). Tiếng Việt chỉ thêm 'rằng' ('anh ấy nói rằng anh ấy mệt') mà không đổi thì, nhưng tiếng Anh phải lùi thì.
- **Formula**: `Subject + said (that) + Subject + Past Form`
- **Analysis**: The present 'am' changes to the past 'was'. 'I' changes to 'he' because we are talking about him.
- **Exemplar**: Anna said that she liked my new haircut.

## Reported Wh-Questions
- **ID**: gram-b1-21
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: He asked, "Where do you live?"
- **Transformation**: He asked me where I lived.
- **Grammatical Cue**: In reported questions, the word order is like a statement and there is no 'do/does'.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi tường thuật với từ để hỏi: dùng trật tự câu khẳng định, bỏ do/does và lùi thì ('do you live' -> 'I lived'). Không dùng dấu chấm hỏi. Tiếng Việt giữ nguyên trật tự nên người học hay viết 'where did I live'.
- **Formula**: `asked + (object) + wh-word + Subject + Past Verb`
- **Analysis**: The question word stays at the front. The subject comes before the verb, as in a statement.
- **Exemplar**: She asked me what time the film started.

## Reported Yes/No Questions
- **ID**: gram-b1-22
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: She asked, "Do you like coffee?"
- **Transformation**: She asked me if I liked coffee.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Reported yes/no questions use 'if' or 'whether' and statement word order.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi Yes/No tường thuật: dùng 'if' hoặc 'whether' (liệu/có... không), trật tự khẳng định, lùi thì. Tiếng Việt dùng 'có... không' ('cô ấy hỏi tôi có thích cà phê không'), còn tiếng Anh cần 'if'.
- **Formula**: `asked + (object) + if/whether + Subject + Past Verb`
- **Analysis**: The direct question 'Do you like...' becomes 'if I liked...'. We do not use 'do' in the reported question.
- **Exemplar**: He asked me if I had finished my homework.

## Relative Clause with 'who'
- **ID**: gram-b1-23
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I met a girl. She plays the violin.
- **Transformation**: I met a girl who plays the violin.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Who' joins two sentences and refers to a person.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Who' là đại từ quan hệ chỉ người, thay cho 'she/he/they'. Tiếng Việt dùng 'mà/người mà' ('cô gái mà chơi violin'); tiếng Anh dùng 'who' ngay sau danh từ chỉ người.
- **Formula**: `Noun (person) + who + Verb...`
- **Analysis**: 'Who' replaces 'she' and joins the two sentences. It comes straight after 'a girl'.
- **Exemplar**: The man who lives next door is a doctor.

## Relative Clause with 'which' and 'that'
- **ID**: gram-b1-24
- **Mode**: B1_PASSIVE_AND_REPORTED
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: This is the book. I bought it yesterday.
- **Transformation**: This is the book that I bought yesterday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Which' or 'that' joins two sentences and refers to a thing.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Which/that' là đại từ quan hệ chỉ vật. Tiếng Việt dùng 'mà' ('quyển sách mà tôi mua hôm qua'). Khi 'that' là tân ngữ, có thể bỏ: 'the book I bought yesterday'.
- **Formula**: `Noun (thing) + which/that + Subject + Verb...`
- **Analysis**: 'That' replaces 'it' and joins the two sentences. We do not repeat 'it' after the verb.
- **Exemplar**: The phone which I use every day is very old.

# Mode: Vietnamese L1 Contrastive Repair (24 Rules)

## Final Consonant Release with Plosive Codas
- **ID**: gram-l1-01
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: FINAL_CONSONANTS
- **Trap**: Monosyllabic (C)(w)V(C) coda dropping: /faɪv/ and /faɪt/ collapse into /faɪ/.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: I have fi book on my table.
- **Transformation**: I have five books on my table.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Release the final voiced labiodental fricative /v/ and pluralize the noun.
- **Vietnamese**: Lỗi nuốt âm đuôi phụ âm: Người Việt có xu hướng bỏ âm /v/ trong 'five' và âm đuôi /s/ trong danh từ số nhiều. Hãy nối âm: five‿apples, five‿books.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have + Number (with audible coda) + Plural Noun (-s/-es)`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese syllable phonotactics prohibit post-vocalic fricatives in codas, causing learners to truncate /faɪv/ to /faɪ/. English requires audible realization of the final consonant to avoid semantic collapse with 'fine', 'fight', or 'fly'.
- **Exemplar**: She bought five apples at the supermarket yesterday.

## Third-Person Singular Verb Catenation
- **ID**: gram-l1-02
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: FINAL_CONSONANTS
- **Trap**: Zero-inflection transfer: Verb stays invariant regardless of third-person subject.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: My brother live in an apartment near the university.
- **Transformation**: My brother lives in an apartment near the university.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add inflectional -s (/z/) to third-person singular present verbs and link to following vowels.
- **Vietnamese**: Quên chia đuôi -s/es ngôi thứ ba số ít do tiếng Việt không biến đổi hình thái động từ theo ngôi. Đọc nối âm /z/ sang 'in': live-zin.
- **Formula**: `Third-Person Singular Subject + Verb-s/es + Prepositional Phrase`
- **Analysis**: In Vietnamese, verbs never inflect for person or number (anh ấy sống, tôi sống). Learners must actively bridge the phonological ending /z/ to following vowels to automate grammatical agreement in real-time speech.
- **Exemplar**: The manager lives in a quiet neighborhood outside the city center.

## Regular Past Simple -ed Tri-Phonemic Realization
- **ID**: gram-l1-03
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: FINAL_CONSONANTS
- **Trap**: Past ending deletion: Omitting regular past -ed because time adverbial already indicates past.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Yesterday the engineering team watch the presentation.
- **Transformation**: Yesterday the engineering team watched the presentation.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add -ed (/t/ after voiceless /tʃ/) to mark past tense on the finite verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Bỏ đuôi -ed trong quá khứ đơn vì nghĩ rằng từ 'yesterday' đã đủ biểu thị quá khứ. Đuôi -ed sau âm vô thanh /tʃ/ phát âm là /t/: watch-t.
- **Formula**: `Past Time Expression + Subject + Verb-ed (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) + Object`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese marks tense through optional free lexical items (hôm qua, đã), leaving the verb unmarked. English syntax strictly demands morphological past tense inflection on the finite verb regardless of temporal adjuncts.
- **Exemplar**: Last night the research team watched the documentary on renewable energy.

## Final Consonant Cluster Retention with Fricative-Stop Sequences
- **ID**: gram-l1-04
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: FINAL_CONSONANTS
- **Trap**: Cluster simplification: Deleting the stop in -skt or -sks codas (asked -> as).
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The journalist as the minister several direct questions.
- **Transformation**: The journalist asked the minister several direct questions.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Articulate the complete /skt/ triple coda cluster without epenthetic vowel insertion.
- **Vietnamese**: Đơn giản hóa cụm phụ âm đuôi: Cụm /skt/ trong 'asked' hay /sks/ trong 'desks' (/desks/) dễ bị rút gọn, ví dụ 'asked' chỉ còn /æs/ và 'desks' chỉ còn /des/. Hãy luyện giữ nguyên /s/ rồi bật /k/ và /t/ (hoặc /s/ cuối).
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb[Complex Cluster Coda] + Indirect Object + Direct Object`
- **Analysis**: Complex English consonant clusters (/skt/, /ksts/, /mpts/) have no equivalent in Vietnamese phonology. Learners tend to elide internal stops or insert an intrusive schwa. Articulating the full coda maintains lexical precision.
- **Exemplar**: The auditor asked the financial officer for detailed balance sheet records.

## Voicing Contrast /s/ vs /z/ in Inflected Morphemic Codas
- **ID**: gram-l1-05
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: FINAL_CONSONANTS
- **Trap**: Devoicing of final /z/ to /s/, causing morphological and lexical confusion between 'raises' and 'races'.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The central bank races interest rates to curb inflation.
- **Transformation**: The central bank raises interest rates to curb inflation.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the voiced alveolar fricative /z/ with vocal fold vibration for the verb 'raise'.
- **Vietnamese**: Mất độ rung thanh quản (devoicing): Nhầm lẫn giữa /s/ và /z/ làm biến đổi nghĩa từ vựng (races - chạy đua vs raises - tăng lên). Cần rung dây thanh khi phát âm /z/.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb[Voiced Suffix /z/ or /ɪz/] + Object`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese possesses initial /z/ in northern dialects but never coda /z/. In final positions, voiced fricatives are universally devoiced or dropped by L1 speakers. Maintaining vocal fold vibration prevents semantic distortion in critical executive contexts.
- **Exemplar**: The executive committee raises concerns regarding supply chain vulnerabilities.

## Plural Concord in Four-Consonant Codas
- **ID**: gram-l1-06
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: FINAL_CONSONANTS
- **Trap**: Losing the plural /s/ at the end of a four-consonant coda (/ksts/) in academic nouns.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The study compared two distinct context of wage bargaining across developing economies.
- **Transformation**: The study compared two distinct contexts of wage bargaining across developing economies.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Pronounce the final /s/ of 'contexts' (/ˈkɒntɛksts/) so the full coda /ksts/ is heard.
- **Vietnamese**: Mất âm /s/ cuối trong cụm phụ âm dày: 'contexts' /ˈkɒntɛksts/ có chuỗi /ksts/. Khi nói nhanh, người Việt thường bỏ âm /s/ cuối, làm mất dấu hiệu số nhiều. Phải phát âm trọn cụm /ksts/.
- **Formula**: `Determiner + Quantifier + Plural Noun[-kts/-ksts] + of + Noun Phrase`
- **Analysis**: 'Contexts' has a four-consonant coda, so the plural marker is the last sound and is the first to disappear in fast academic speech. Keeping /s/ preserves the plural and the agreement with 'two'.
- **Exemplar**: The study examined several contexts of wage bargaining across developing economies.

## Adjective Predication Missing Copula
- **ID**: gram-l1-07
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: ZERO_COPULA
- **Trap**: Adjectives functioning as statutory predicates without 'be': Cô ấy rất đẹp -> She very beautiful.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: My sister very tired after school.
- **Transformation**: My sister is very tired after school.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Insert the copular verb 'is' before the predicate adjective.
- **Vietnamese**: Bỏ sót động từ 'to be' trước tính từ: Trong tiếng Việt, tính từ bản thân nó là vị ngữ (Cô ấy [rất mệt]), nên người học thường nói 'My sister very tired'. Tiếng Anh bắt buộc phải có liên động từ 'be'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + (Adverb) + Predicate Adjective`
- **Analysis**: In Austroasiatic syntax, adjectives are stative verbs capable of heading a predicate without verbal support. In Indo-European English, adjectives require a copular head ('be') to receive tense and agreement features.
- **Exemplar**: Our new classroom is very clean and bright.

## Negative Adjectival Predication Copula Omission
- **ID**: gram-l1-08
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: ZERO_COPULA
- **Trap**: Negating predicate adjectives with 'not' directly: Phòng không sạch -> Room not clean.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: This hotel room not comfortable for our family.
- **Transformation**: This hotel room is not comfortable for our family.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Place 'is not' (or 'isn't') between the subject noun phrase and the adjective.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định tính từ trực tiếp bằng 'not' mà thiếu 'is': Tiếng Việt chỉ cần 'không + tính từ' (không thoải mái), dẫn đến lỗi 'room not comfortable'. Phải dùng 'is not'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + not + Predicate Adjective`
- **Analysis**: Direct transfer of Vietnamese negation particle 'không' placed directly before stative predicates produces ungrammatical bare negative strings ('Subject not Adjective'). English demands the auxiliary/copular negator anchor.
- **Exemplar**: This public transport option is not convenient during rush hour.

## Hyper-Correction Overuse of 'Be' with Lexical Action Verbs
- **ID**: gram-l1-09
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: ZERO_COPULA
- **Trap**: Overgeneralizing copula insertion to lexical verbs: 'I am agree', 'She is work'.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I am agree with your assessment of the market situation.
- **Transformation**: I agree with your assessment of the market situation.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Remove the intrusive copula 'am'; 'agree' is a finite lexical verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Lạm dụng 'to be' với động từ thường (Hyper-correction): Do bị sửa lỗi thiếu 'be' quá nhiều, người học sinh ra phản xạ đặt 'am/is/are' trước mọi động từ, tạo thành 'I am agree' hay 'I am work'. 'Agree' là động từ thường.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Finite Lexical Verb (No Copula) + Prepositional Complement`
- **Analysis**: Hyper-correction phenomenon where learners, having internalized that English clauses require 'be', misclassify stative lexical verbs ('agree', 'doubt', 'understand') as adjectives, producing ungrammatical copula-verb doublets.
- **Exemplar**: We completely agree with the proposed revisions to the contractual agreement.

## Misplacement of Adverb 'Very' Directly Modifying Full Verbs
- **ID**: gram-l1-10
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: ZERO_COPULA
- **Trap**: Transfer of Vietnamese 'rất' modifying verbs: Tôi rất thích -> I very like.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The senior board members very like this expansion strategy.
- **Transformation**: The senior board members really like this expansion strategy.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Replace 'very' with 'really' before the verb, or place 'very much' at the end.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng 'very' bổ nghĩa trực tiếp cho động từ: Tiếng Việt có cấu trúc 'rất + động từ' (rất thích, rất muốn). Trong tiếng Anh, 'very' chỉ bổ nghĩa cho tính từ/trạng từ; với động từ phải dùng 'really' hoặc 'very much' ở cuối câu.
- **Formula**: `Subject + really + Verb + Object OR Subject + Verb + Object + very much`
- **Analysis**: The Vietnamese intensifier 'rất' freely modifies adjectives, stative verbs, and mental-state predicates. In English, degree adverb 'very' is restricted to adjectival and adverbial heads; lexical verbs require 'really' or post-verbal 'very much'.
- **Exemplar**: The client really appreciates our prompt response to technical inquiries.

## Copula Deletion in Subordinate Concessive Structures
- **ID**: gram-l1-11
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: ZERO_COPULA
- **Trap**: Omitting copula in subordinate adverbial clauses following concessive conjunctions.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: Although the regulatory guidelines strict, the firm complied fully.
- **Transformation**: Although the regulatory guidelines were strict, the firm complied fully.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Insert the past copular verb 'were' in agreement with plural subject 'guidelines'.
- **Vietnamese**: Bỏ quên liên động từ trong mệnh đề phụ chỉ sự nhượng bộ: Do tập trung vào cấu trúc liên từ 'Although', người học thường dịch nguyên vẹn từ tiếng Việt 'Mặc dù quy định [nghiêm ngặt]' mà quên chia 'were'.
- **Formula**: `Although + Subordinate Subject + was/were + Adjective, Main Clause`
- **Analysis**: High cognitive load during subordinate clause construction causes L1 regression where Austroasiatic stative predication resurfaces, dropping tense-bearing copulas in dependent finite clauses.
- **Exemplar**: Although the fiscal constraints were severe, the department delivered on all objectives.

## Copula Retention After Finite Relative Clauses
- **ID**: gram-l1-12
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: ZERO_COPULA
- **Trap**: Dropping the copula before a predicate adjective inside a long finite relative clause.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The findings that the panel reviewed unexpected in several respects.
- **Transformation**: The findings that the panel reviewed were unexpected in several respects.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Keep the copula (was/were) before the predicate adjective, even when the relative clause is long.
- **Vietnamese**: Tiếng Việt không cần động từ "là" trước tính từ vị ngữ, nên người học hay bỏ "to be" khi mệnh đề quan hệ dài. Tiếng Anh bắt buộc có động từ liên kết (was/were) trước tính từ vị ngữ, kể cả khi mệnh đề quan hệ đã dài.
- **Formula**: `Head Noun + that/which + Subject + Verb + was/were + Predicate Adjective`
- **Analysis**: A finite relative clause cannot end in a bare adjective: the predicate needs a linking verb. Reduced forms that drop the copula are allowed only in specific structures, not here.
- **Exemplar**: The forecasts that the central bank published were considerably more pessimistic than the markets expected.

## Adverbial Presence Causing Finite Past Tense Deletion
- **ID**: gram-l1-13
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: TENSE_ASPECT
- **Trap**: Relying on external temporal adverb to establish past time while leaving verb uninflected.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Yesterday afternoon my family visit the national museum.
- **Transformation**: Yesterday afternoon my family visited the national museum.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Inflect the finite verb with past tense -ed even when temporal adverb 'yesterday' is present.
- **Vietnamese**: Quên chia thì quá khứ khi đã có trạng từ chỉ thời gian: Tiếng Việt dùng trạng từ 'hôm qua' để biểu thị quá khứ, động từ giữ nguyên. Tiếng Anh đòi hỏi bắt buộc cả hai: Trạng từ thời gian + Động từ chia quá khứ.
- **Formula**: `Past Adverbial + Subject + Past Tense Verb (-ed / Irregular) + Object`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese temporal reference operates on discourse-level aspectual pragmatics; once a temporal anchor is established, verbal morphemes remain invariant. English is a strict grammaticalized tense language requiring dual anchoring.
- **Exemplar**: Yesterday morning the maintenance crew inspected the backup generators.

## Aspectual Particle 'Rồi' Conflation with Monolithic 'Already'
- **ID**: gram-l1-14
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: TENSE_ASPECT
- **Trap**: Translating 'rồi' as clause-final 'already' with present simple verb: Tôi ăn cơm rồi -> I eat dinner already.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: I submit the scholarship application already.
- **Transformation**: I have already submitted the scholarship application.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the Present Perfect auxiliary 'have' + past participle with pre-verbal 'already'.
- **Vietnamese**: Lạm dụng 'already' ở cuối câu để dịch từ 'rồi': Cấu trúc 'V + already' là sao chép từ 'V + rồi'. Trong tiếng Anh chuẩn, hành động vừa hoàn thành có kết quả ở hiện tại phải dùng thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (have/has + V3).
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + already + Past Participle + Object`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese 'rồi' is a multifunctional completive aspect particle indicating status transition. Translating 'rồi' as clause-final lexical 'already' violates English aspectual syntax, which pairs completion with auxiliary 'have/has' + participle.
- **Exemplar**: She has already submitted the revised draft to the editorial board.

## Durative Past Continuing to Present (Present Perfect vs Simple Present)
- **ID**: gram-l1-15
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: TENSE_ASPECT
- **Trap**: Using Present Simple + 'already' to express duration extending from past to now: Sống ở đây 5 năm rồi.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Our team works on this software development project already six months.
- **Transformation**: Our team has worked on this software development project for six months.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use Present Perfect (has worked) with preposition 'for' to express ongoing duration.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng thì hiện tại đơn kèm 'already' để diễn đạt hành động kéo dài từ quá khứ đến nay: 'Làm việc 6 tháng rồi'. Tiếng Anh bắt buộc dùng Hiện tại hoàn thành với 'for + khoảng thời gian'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + have/has + Past Participle + for + Duration (No 'already')`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese uses bare verbs accompanied by durative complements plus completive particle 'rồi' (làm việc 6 tháng rồi). English reserves present tense exclusively for habitual or concurrent states; duration originating in past requires perfect aspect.
- **Exemplar**: The consulting firm has operated in Southeast Asia for more than a decade.

## Sequence of Tenses in Indirect Reported Speech Backshifting
- **ID**: gram-l1-16
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: TENSE_ASPECT
- **Trap**: Failing to backshift verb in reported speech because original statement was present tense.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: When I phoned yesterday morning, the finance director told me that he is in a budget meeting.
- **Transformation**: When I phoned yesterday morning, the finance director told me that he was in a budget meeting.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Backshift the subordinate verb 'is' to past 'was': the meeting belongs to yesterday morning, so the reported verb must be past.
- **Vietnamese**: Quên lùi thì trong câu gián tiếp: Tiếng Việt không có quy tắc lùi thì (Giám đốc nói rằng ông ấy [đang họp]). Trong tiếng Anh, khi động từ tường thuật ở quá khứ, mặc định là lùi thì ở mệnh đề sau. Nếu điều được nói vẫn còn đúng ở hiện tại thì có thể giữ thì hiện tại, nhưng ở đây cuộc họp chỉ diễn ra hôm qua nên bắt buộc dùng 'was'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Past Reporting Verb + that + Subordinate Subject + Backshifted Past Verb`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese lacks tense concordance; subordinate clauses maintain the temporal orientation of the reported utterance. In English, backshift is the default after a past reporting verb, but it is optional when the reported statement is still true at the time of reporting ('The director said the revenue is satisfactory' is acceptable if it still is). Here the state was true only at a past moment, so the past form is the only natural choice.
- **Exemplar**: The technical lead reported that the production server was operating normally.

## Hypothetical Conditional Clause Tense Flattening
- **ID**: gram-l1-17
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: TENSE_ASPECT
- **Trap**: Flattening conditional tenses into present/future forms due to lack of conditional morphology in L1.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The data leak happened last month. If the security team detected the breach earlier, they will stop the data leak.
- **Transformation**: If the security team had detected the breach earlier, they would have stopped the data leak.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use Past Perfect in the if-clause and 'would have' + past participle in the main clause for unfulfilled past hypothetical.
- **Vietnamese**: Không chia thì giả định trong câu điều kiện loại 3: Tiếng Việt chỉ dùng từ 'Nếu... thì...' mà không biến đổi động từ. Khi nói về điều trái với quá khứ, tiếng Anh bắt buộc dùng 'had + V3' và 'would have + V3'.
- **Formula**: `If + Subject + had + Past Participle, Subject + would have + Past Participle`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese conditional sentences rely on particles ('nếu', 'giá như') without temporal counterfactual morphology. English Type 3 counterfactuals demand systematic past-of-past marking to encode unreality.
- **Exemplar**: If the risk committee had acted on the warnings, they would have mitigated the exposure.

## No Sooner Inversion and Past Perfect
- **ID**: gram-l1-18
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: TENSE_ASPECT
- **Trap**: Using 'did' or the simple past in the inverted first clause of 'No sooner… than', where the perfect is required.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: No sooner did the data was released than analysts revised their models.
- **Transformation**: No sooner had the data been released than analysts revised their models.
- **Grammatical Cue**: After 'No sooner', invert with 'had' + past participle and no 'did'.
- **Vietnamese**: Trong cấu trúc đảo ngữ 'No sooner… than', tiếng Anh dùng 'had' + quá khứ phân từ cho hành động xảy ra trước. Người học hay dùng 'did' hoặc quá khứ đơn vì tiếng Việt không đánh dấu trình tự bằng trợ động từ.
- **Formula**: `No sooner + had + Subject + Past Participle + than + Subject + Simple Past`
- **Analysis**: This inverted perfect is a formal C2 pattern. 'Had' marks the first event and the simple past marks the second. Adding 'did' or using a simple past in the first clause makes the sequence unreadable.
- **Exemplar**: No sooner had the regulator published the draft than three banks filed formal objections.

## Bare Singular Countable Nouns (Determiner Omission)
- **ID**: gram-l1-19
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: CLASSIFIERS_COUNT
- **Trap**: Bare singular count nouns: Tôi có xe -> I have car; Cô ấy là giáo viên -> She is teacher.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: My older brother wants to buy new computer next week.
- **Transformation**: My older brother wants to buy a new computer next week.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Insert indefinite article 'a' before singular countable noun phrase 'new computer'.
- **Vietnamese**: Để danh từ đếm được số ít đứng trơ trọi không có từ hạn định (Bare Nouns): Tiếng Việt danh từ đứng độc lập không cần mạo từ (mua máy tính mới). Trong tiếng Anh, danh từ đếm được số ít BẮT BUỘC phải có mạo từ (a/an/the) hoặc từ hạn định.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb + Indefinite Article (a/an) + (Adjective) + Singular Count Noun`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese nouns denote generic concepts by default; individuation requires an overt numeral plus classifier. In English, singular countable nouns cannot appear as bare arguments; they must be grammatically determined.
- **Exemplar**: The junior developer requested a new monitor for the workstation.

## Uncountable Noun Pluralization with 'Homework' and 'Information'
- **ID**: gram-l1-20
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: CLASSIFIERS_COUNT
- **Trap**: Treating mass nouns as count nouns by adding plural -s: many homeworks, useful informations.
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: The instructor assigned us several difficult homeworks over the weekend.
- **Transformation**: The instructor assigned us a lot of difficult homework over the weekend.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Treat 'homework' as an uncountable mass noun; replace 'several difficult homeworks' with 'a lot of difficult homework'.
- **Vietnamese**: Thêm đuôi số nhiều -s vào danh từ không đếm được: Tiếng Việt đếm 'bài tập' (nhiều bài tập về nhà), 'thông tin' (nhiều thông tin) thông qua từ chỉ số lượng. Tiếng Anh coi 'homework', 'information' là danh từ trừu tượng không đếm được, tuyệt đối không thêm -s.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb + Quantifier (a lot of / much) + Uncountable Noun (No -s)`
- **Analysis**: Cross-linguistic semantic mismatch: Vietnamese treats instructional tasks as discrete countable units via classifiers ('bài'). English categorizes 'homework' and 'information' as continuous non-count mass substances.
- **Exemplar**: The students completed all their assigned homework before attending the workshop.

## Uncountable 'Advice' Partitive Carrier Construction
- **ID**: gram-l1-21
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: CLASSIFIERS_COUNT
- **Trap**: Using indefinite article or plural with 'advice': an advice, many advices.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: The senior mentor gave me a valuable advice regarding career progression.
- **Transformation**: The senior mentor gave me a valuable piece of advice regarding career progression.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Insert the partitive classifier noun 'piece of' before uncountable noun 'advice'.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng 'an advice' hoặc 'advices': 'Lời khuyên' trong tiếng Việt đếm được (một lời khuyên). Trong tiếng Anh, 'advice' là không đếm được; muốn đếm một lời khuyên phải dùng danh từ phân đoạn: 'a piece of advice'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb + a valuable piece of + Uncountable Noun (advice)`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese speakers intuitively transfer classifier grammar by assuming 'advice' accepts direct cardinal counting. English requires an explicit partitive counter noun ('a piece of', 'words of') to individuate the mass concept.
- **Exemplar**: The career counselor offered several useful pieces of advice on interview preparation.

## Mass Technical Nouns in Industrial & Technological Contexts
- **ID**: gram-l1-22
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: CLASSIFIERS_COUNT
- **Trap**: Affixing plural -s to technical mass nouns: equipments, machineries, softwares.
- **Level**: 2
- **CEFR**: B2
- **Prompt**: The new laboratory installed several cutting-edge equipments yesterday.
- **Transformation**: The new laboratory installed several pieces of cutting-edge equipment yesterday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Treat 'equipment' as non-count; use partitive 'pieces of equipment'.
- **Vietnamese**: Thêm -s vào danh từ kỹ thuật không đếm được như 'equipment', 'software'. Tiếng Việt coi 'thiết bị', 'phần mềm' là danh từ đếm được; tiếng Anh bắt buộc dùng cụm phân đoạn 'pieces of equipment' hoặc 'software applications'.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb + Quantifier + pieces of + Uncountable Technical Noun`
- **Analysis**: Vietnamese technical terminology translates concepts like 'thiết bị' (equipment) as countable physical items. In standard English, 'equipment', 'software', and 'machinery' are aggregate mass nouns that forbid inflectional -s.
- **Exemplar**: The manufacturing facility upgraded its high-precision diagnostic equipment.

## Generic Zero-Article vs Definite Article in Abstract Nouns
- **ID**: gram-l1-23
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: CLASSIFIERS_COUNT
- **Trap**: Over-inserting 'the' before abstract nouns used in a generic sense: The society, The life.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C1
- **Prompt**: The society must establish robust institutions to preserve the democracy.
- **Transformation**: Society must establish robust institutions to preserve democracy.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Remove the over-inserted definite article 'the' before generic abstract nouns 'society' and 'democracy'.
- **Vietnamese**: Lạm dụng 'the' trước danh từ trừu tượng nói chung: Người học thường nghĩ 'the' làm cho câu văn học thuật hơn, dẫn đến 'The society', 'The life'. Khi nói về xã hội hay dân chủ nói chung, tiếng Anh dùng danh từ trần (Zero Article).
- **Formula**: `Zero Article + Abstract Noun (Generic) + Verb + Zero Article + Abstract Noun`
- **Analysis**: In Vietnamese, demonstratives ('ấy', 'này') specify referents, but abstract generalizations require no markers. Learners overcompensate in formal English by adding 'the' to generic concepts. English uses the zero article for general abstract notions.
- **Exemplar**: Democratic society depends on transparent institutions to safeguard human rights.

## Latinate Plural Criteria and Concord
- **ID**: gram-l1-24
- **Mode**: L1_CONTRASTIVE_REPAIR
- **Vector**: CLASSIFIERS_COUNT
- **Trap**: Treating the Latinate plural 'criteria' as a singular noun and using 'is' with it.
- **Level**: 3
- **CEFR**: C2
- **Prompt**: The criteria for admission is more stringent than before.
- **Transformation**: The criteria for admission are more stringent than before.
- **Grammatical Cue**: 'Criteria' is plural (singular: 'criterion'), so the verb is 'are'. Use 'is' only with 'the criterion'.
- **Vietnamese**: 'Criteria' là số nhiều của 'criterion' (tiêu chí). Tiếng Việt không đổi hình thức danh từ nên người học thường dùng 'criteria is' hoặc coi 'criteria' là danh từ không đếm được. Trong văn bản học thuật C2, cần dùng 'criteria are' hoặc 'the criterion is'.
- **Formula**: `The criteria for + Noun Phrase + are + Comparative Adjective`
- **Analysis**: Latinate plurals such as 'criteria', 'phenomena' and 'data' take plural concord in formal register. The singular form 'criterion' takes 'is'. Mixing the two causes agreement errors that academic readers notice at once.
- **Exemplar**: The criteria for granting tenure are now applied more rigorously across faculties.

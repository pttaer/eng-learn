# Speaking Drills: B1 Opinion Talks

## A Memorable Experience
- **ID**: speaking-b1-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Talk about a memorable experience you have had. Say what happened, who was with you and why you still remember it.
- **Anchor**: Set the scene (when and where) → Tell what happened → Say why it is special to you
- **Collocations**: unforgettable experience, take place, to be honest, looking back
- **Phonetic**: Stress the key words in each sentence, for example 'It was the BEST day of my LIFE', and say small words like 'the' and 'of' quickly.

## A Problem and How You Solved It
- **ID**: speaking-b1-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Describe a problem you had and explain how you solved it. What did you learn from it?
- **Anchor**: Explain the problem → Describe what you did to fix it → Say what you learned
- **Collocations**: deal with a problem, come up with a solution, ask for help, in the end
- **Phonetic**: Link words that join consonant to vowel, for example 'ask_for_help' sounds like 'as-kfor-help'.

## Advantages and Disadvantages of Social Media
- **ID**: speaking-b1-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: What are the advantages and disadvantages of social media? Give your own opinion.
- **Anchor**: Main advantages (stay in touch, share news) → Main disadvantages (waste of time, privacy) → Your opinion and reason
- **Collocations**: keep in touch with, waste of time, on the other hand, in my opinion
- **Phonetic**: Use rising intonation on each item in a list and falling intonation on the last one: 'friends, news, photos and videos'.

## Your Plans for the Future
- **ID**: speaking-b1-4
- **Index**: 4
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your plans for the next five years. What do you hope to do, and what might stop you?
- **Anchor**: Your main plan (study, work or travel) → Steps you will take → Possible problems and how you will deal with them
- **Collocations**: be planning to, make progress, achieve a goal, if everything goes well
- **Phonetic**: Stress the important verb in plans, for example 'I'm going to STUDY abroad', and say 'going to' quickly as 'gonna' in relaxed speech.

## A Book or Film You Recommend
- **ID**: speaking-b1-5
- **Index**: 5
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Recommend a book or a film to a friend. Say what it is about and why you think they will enjoy it.
- **Anchor**: Name it and say what it is about → What you liked most → Who you recommend it to and why
- **Collocations**: be based on, the main character, well worth watching, I would recommend
- **Phonetic**: Make your voice go up and louder on the words that show feeling, for example 'It was REALLY exciting'.

## Life in a Big City or a Small Town
- **ID**: speaking-b1-6
- **Index**: 6
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Would you prefer to live in a big city or a small town? Give reasons for your answer.
- **Anchor**: Say which you prefer → Give two reasons with examples → Mention one good point about the other place
- **Collocations**: public transport, cost of living, peace and quiet, there are plenty of
- **Phonetic**: Stress the contrast words in a comparison, for example 'The city is NOISY but the town is QUIET'.

## A Person Who Has Helped You
- **ID**: speaking-b1-7
- **Index**: 7
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Talk about a person who has helped you in your life. Say who the person is, how they helped you and how you feel about them.
- **Anchor**: Who the person is → How they helped you → What you would like to say to them now
- **Collocations**: look up to, be grateful for, support someone, get on well with
- **Phonetic**: Say the 'ed' endings clearly, for example 'helped' ends with a /t/ sound and 'supported' ends with /id/.

## Should Students Wear a School Uniform?
- **ID**: speaking-b1-8
- **Index**: 8
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Do you think students should wear a school uniform? Explain your opinion with reasons.
- **Anchor**: Your opinion → Two reasons with examples → What people who disagree might say and your answer
- **Collocations**: feel equal, express yourself, I strongly believe that, on the whole
- **Phonetic**: Pause briefly after your opinion, then give your reasons: 'I think so, because...'.

## A Trip You Have Taken
- **ID**: speaking-b1-9
- **Index**: 9
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Describe a trip you have taken. Where did you go, what did you do and would you go there again?
- **Anchor**: Where you went and who with → The best and worst parts → Whether you would go again
- **Collocations**: go sightseeing, book a hotel, local food, have a great time
- **Phonetic**: Use past tense endings clearly in a story, for example 'visited', 'stayed', 'enjoyed'.

## Working from Home or in an Office
- **ID**: speaking-b1-10
- **Index**: 10
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Is it better to work from home or in an office? Say what you think and why.
- **Anchor**: Benefits of working from home → Benefits of working in an office → Your choice and reason
- **Collocations**: save time, work as a team, stay focused, it depends on
- **Phonetic**: Stress 'but' and the word after it to show a change of idea: 'It is flexible, but it can be LONELY'.

## Healthy Habits in Daily Life
- **ID**: speaking-b1-11
- **Index**: 11
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: What do you do to stay healthy? Talk about your habits and say what you could do better.
- **Anchor**: Your good habits (food, sport, sleep) → One habit you want to change → Advice for a friend
- **Collocations**: get enough sleep, take exercise, eat a balanced diet, give up
- **Phonetic**: Link 'a' and 'of' to the next word, for example 'a lot of' sounds like 'a-lo-tuv'.

## Learning English: Your Experience
- **ID**: speaking-b1-12
- **Index**: 12
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Talk about how you have been learning English. What helps you most and what is difficult for you?
- **Anchor**: Why and how you started → What helps you most → Your goal for the future
- **Collocations**: make mistakes, improve my speaking, practise every day, keep going
- **Phonetic**: Say long words with the stress in the right place, for example 'imPROVE' and 'PRACtise'.

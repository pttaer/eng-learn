# Writing Drills: B1 Paragraphs

## Social Media: Good or Bad?
- **ID**: writing-b1-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Do you think social media is good or bad for young people? Give your opinion with reasons.
- **Register**: Semi-formal / Opinion
- **Master Sentence**: In my opinion, social media is useful for young people, but they should not use it too much.
- **M**: In my opinion, social media is useful for young people, but they should not use it too much. I believe it has both good and bad sides.
- **E**: For example, it helps students keep in touch with friends and share ideas about their homework. However, some of them spend hours online every night.
- **A**: Because of this, they often feel tired and cannot concentrate at school. They also see too many perfect photos, which can make them feel unhappy.
- **L**: Therefore, I think young people should use social media for about one hour a day. If they do this, they will enjoy the good side without the problems.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'however' at the start of a sentence to show a contrast, and follow it with a comma.

## An Email of Complaint
- **ID**: writing-b1-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: You bought a pair of shoes online, but they arrived broken. Write an email to the shop to complain.
- **Register**: Semi-formal / Email
- **Master Sentence**: I am writing to complain about a pair of shoes that I bought from your website last week.
- **M**: I am writing to complain about a pair of shoes that I bought from your website last week. They arrived yesterday, but one of them was broken.
- **E**: For example, the heel is loose and the left shoe has a long mark on it. I have attached two photos so you can see the problem.
- **A**: Because I need the shoes for a wedding next month, I would like a quick answer. I would be happy to receive either a new pair or my money back.
- **L**: Please let me know what you can do as soon as possible. I look forward to hearing from you.
- **Stylistic**: Start with 'I am writing to...' to say your reason, and end with 'I look forward to hearing from you'.

## Describing a Memorable Day
- **ID**: writing-b1-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Describe a day that you remember well. Say what happened and why it was special.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: One of the best days of my life was my first trip to the beach with my family.
- **M**: One of the best days of my life was my first trip to the beach with my family. I was ten years old, and I still remember it clearly.
- **E**: For example, we got up very early and drove for three hours. When we arrived, I ran straight into the sea.
- **A**: Although the water was cold, I was so happy that I did not care. After that, we ate fresh fish and played games on the sand.
- **L**: At the end of the day, I was tired but very happy. That is why I will never forget it.
- **Stylistic**: Use time words like 'when', 'after that' and 'at the end of the day' to keep a story in order.

## The Advantages of Learning a Second Language
- **ID**: writing-b1-4
- **Index**: 4
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: What are the advantages of learning a foreign language? Write a paragraph giving your opinion.
- **Register**: Semi-formal / Opinion
- **Master Sentence**: I think learning a foreign language is one of the most useful things a person can do.
- **M**: I think learning a foreign language is one of the most useful things a person can do. It gives people many chances in life.
- **E**: For example, people who speak English can find better jobs and travel more easily. They can also make friends from different countries.
- **A**: Because language and culture are connected, learning a language helps you understand other people better. However, it takes time and you must practise often.
- **L**: In conclusion, the effort is worth it. Anyone who keeps learning will see great results.
- **Stylistic**: Finish with 'In conclusion' and repeat your main idea in new words.

## An Invitation to a Friend
- **ID**: writing-b1-5
- **Index**: 5
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Write a message to a friend inviting them to your birthday party. Give the details and ask for a reply.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: I am having a birthday party next Saturday, and I would love you to come.
- **M**: I am having a birthday party next Saturday, and I would love you to come. It will be at my house, starting at six o'clock.
- **E**: For example, my mum is going to cook a lot of food, and we will play music and games. I have also invited some people from our old class.
- **A**: Because the house is hard to find, I will send you a map. If you want, you can stay the night, as we will probably finish late.
- **L**: Please tell me by Wednesday if you can come. I hope to see you there.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'would love' and 'I hope' to sound warm and friendly in a personal message.

## Should Children Have Mobile Phones?
- **ID**: writing-b1-6
- **Index**: 6
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: At what age should children get a mobile phone? Give your opinion and reasons.
- **Register**: Semi-formal / Opinion
- **Master Sentence**: I believe that children should not have a mobile phone until they are about twelve years old.
- **M**: I believe that children should not have a mobile phone until they are about twelve years old. Before that age, they do not really need one.
- **E**: For example, younger children are always with their parents or teachers, so they can ask them for help. However, older children often travel alone and a phone can keep them safe.
- **A**: Because phones can also be dangerous if children use them without rules, parents must check how they are used. Games and videos can take too much of their free time.
- **L**: For these reasons, I think twelve is a good age to start. Parents should still set clear rules.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'should' and 'must' to give advice and show how strong your opinion is.

## A Letter to a New Neighbour
- **ID**: writing-b1-7
- **Index**: 7
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: A new family has moved in next door. Write a short note to welcome them and offer help.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: Welcome to the street, and I hope you are enjoying your new home.
- **M**: Welcome to the street, and I hope you are enjoying your new home. I live in the blue house next to yours.
- **E**: For example, if you need to know where the shops, the bus stop or the best cafe are, just ask me. I have lived here for ten years, so I know the area well.
- **A**: Because moving is always hard work, I would be glad to lend you tools or look after your pets. However, please do not feel you have to ask.
- **L**: I would also like to invite you for tea this weekend. Please knock on my door when you are free.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'I would be glad to' and 'please' to make offers polite and kind.

## Eating Out or Cooking at Home
- **ID**: writing-b1-8
- **Index**: 8
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Is it better to eat in restaurants or to cook at home? Write a paragraph with your opinion.
- **Register**: Semi-formal / Opinion
- **Master Sentence**: In my view, cooking at home is better than eating out most of the time.
- **M**: In my view, cooking at home is better than eating out most of the time. It is cheaper and usually healthier.
- **E**: For example, a meal for a family of four in a restaurant can cost more than a week of vegetables from the market. When you cook, you also know exactly what is in your food.
- **A**: However, restaurants are good for special days, because you can relax and enjoy the atmosphere. Cooking at home can also be tiring after a long day at work.
- **L**: So I think the best choice is to cook at home during the week. We can eat out once in a while as a treat.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'most of the time' and 'once in a while' to describe how often something happens.

## A Problem at Work or School
- **ID**: writing-b1-9
- **Index**: 9
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Write about a problem you had at work or school and say how you solved it.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: Last term I had a big problem with a group project, but I managed to solve it.
- **M**: Last term I had a big problem with a group project, but I managed to solve it. One member of our group never did his part of the work.
- **E**: For example, he missed three meetings and did not answer our messages. Because of this, we were worried that we would not finish on time.
- **A**: In the end, I talked to him alone and asked him what was wrong. He told me he was busy with a part-time job, so we gave him an easier task.
- **L**: As a result, we finished the project on time and got a good mark. I learned that talking calmly is better than getting angry.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'in the end' and 'as a result' to show how a story or problem finished.

## Public Transport in My City
- **ID**: writing-b1-10
- **Index**: 10
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Do you think public transport in your city is good enough? Give reasons and suggest one improvement.
- **Register**: Semi-formal / Opinion
- **Master Sentence**: I think public transport in my city is not good enough, and it needs to improve.
- **M**: I think public transport in my city is not good enough, and it needs to improve. Many people still prefer to drive or ride motorbikes.
- **E**: For example, buses are often late, and they are very crowded at rush hour. Some areas of the city do not have any bus service at all.
- **A**: Because of this, the roads are full of traffic and the air is dirty. However, if the buses were cheaper and more regular, more people would use them.
- **L**: Therefore, I would suggest adding more buses and building a metro line. This would make travel faster and the city cleaner.
- **Stylistic**: Use the second conditional ('if the buses were... more people would...') to talk about a possible change.

## Writing About a Hobby
- **ID**: writing-b1-11
- **Index**: 11
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: Write about a hobby you enjoy. Say when you started and why you like it.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: My favourite hobby is taking photos, and I have done it for about three years.
- **M**: My favourite hobby is taking photos, and I have done it for about three years. I started when my uncle gave me an old camera.
- **E**: For example, I now go out every weekend to take pictures of streets and markets. I have also learned how to use light to make a photo look better.
- **A**: Because photography makes me look carefully at the world, I notice small things that I never saw before. However, good cameras are expensive, so I still use my old one.
- **L**: One day I hope to have my own small exhibition. Until then, I will keep practising.
- **Stylistic**: Use the present perfect with 'for' and 'since' ('for three years') to link the past with now.

## A Healthy Lifestyle
- **ID**: writing-b1-12
- **Index**: 12
- **Mode**: B1 Eight-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Question**: What is the best way to stay healthy? Write a paragraph giving advice to a friend.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: If you want to stay healthy, you should eat well, exercise and get enough sleep.
- **M**: If you want to stay healthy, you should eat well, exercise and get enough sleep. These three things are easy to start but need to become habits.
- **E**: For example, you could walk to work instead of taking the bus, and eat more fruit and vegetables. I started doing this last year and I feel much better now.
- **A**: Because many people sit at a desk all day, they need to move more. However, you do not have to join a gym, as a short walk after dinner is enough.
- **L**: If you try these small changes, you will soon notice the difference. I am sure you can do it.
- **Stylistic**: Use the first conditional ('If you try..., you will...') to give advice with a result.

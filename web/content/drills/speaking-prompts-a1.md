# Speaking Drills: A1 Short Talks

## Introduce Yourself
- **ID**: speaking-a1-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Say hello and introduce yourself. Say your name, your age and where you are from.
- **Anchor**: My name and age → Where I am from → What I like
- **Collocations**: my name is, I am from, nice to meet you, I like
- **Phonetic**: Say the "th" in "this" and "thanks" with your tongue between your teeth.

## My Family
- **ID**: speaking-a1-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your family. Who is in your family? What are they like?
- **Anchor**: Who is in my family → Their names and ages → What I do with them
- **Collocations**: there are four people, my mother, my brother, we live together
- **Phonetic**: Say the final "s" in "parents" and "brothers" clearly.

## My Daily Routine
- **ID**: speaking-a1-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Describe your day. What do you do in the morning, afternoon and evening?
- **Anchor**: Morning (get up, eat breakfast) → Afternoon (work or school) → Evening (dinner, sleep)
- **Collocations**: get up, have breakfast, go to work, go to bed
- **Phonetic**: Say the "s" at the end of "gets up" and "goes" when you talk about he or she.

## My Home
- **ID**: speaking-a1-4
- **Index**: 4
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Describe your home. Say what rooms it has and what you like in it.
- **Anchor**: Where my home is → The rooms in my home → My favourite room
- **Collocations**: I live in, there is a, living room, my favourite room
- **Phonetic**: Say "there is" and "there are" slowly and clearly, with a soft "th" sound.

## My Favourite Food
- **ID**: speaking-a1-5
- **Index**: 5
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your favourite food. Say what it is and when you eat it.
- **Anchor**: My favourite food → When and where I eat it → Why I like it
- **Collocations**: my favourite food is, I like to eat, it is delicious, for lunch
- **Phonetic**: Say the final "s" in "noodles" and "vegetables" as a "z" sound.

## What I Can Do
- **ID**: speaking-a1-6
- **Index**: 6
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Say what you can do and what you cannot do. Talk about three things.
- **Anchor**: Two things I can do → One thing I cannot do → What I want to learn
- **Collocations**: I can, I cannot, I can swim, I want to learn
- **Phonetic**: Say "can" short and soft, but say "can't" long and clear.

## My Best Friend
- **ID**: speaking-a1-7
- **Index**: 7
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your best friend. Say who he or she is and what you do together.
- **Anchor**: Who my friend is → What my friend looks like → What we do together
- **Collocations**: my best friend, he is tall, she has long hair, we play together
- **Phonetic**: Say the "h" in "he", "his" and "home" with a little air from your mouth.

## My School or My Work
- **ID**: speaking-a1-8
- **Index**: 8
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your school or your work. Say where it is and what you do there.
- **Anchor**: Where it is → What I do there → Who I meet there
- **Collocations**: I go to school, I work in, every day, at nine o'clock
- **Phonetic**: Say the "s" at the start of "school" and "student" with a short, clear sound.

## My Weekend
- **ID**: speaking-a1-9
- **Index**: 9
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your weekend. What do you do on Saturday and Sunday?
- **Anchor**: What I do on Saturday → What I do on Sunday → Who I am with
- **Collocations**: on Saturday, go shopping, watch TV, visit my family
- **Phonetic**: Say the final "s" in "weekends" and "Sundays" clearly.

## My Town
- **ID**: speaking-a1-10
- **Index**: 10
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Describe your town or city. Say what places are in it and what you like.
- **Anchor**: Where my town is → Places in my town → What I like about it
- **Collocations**: there is a park, near my house, a big city, I like it
- **Phonetic**: Say the "r" in "park" and "market" with your tongue back, not touching the roof of your mouth.

## My Clothes and Colours
- **ID**: speaking-a1-11
- **Index**: 11
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Say what clothes you wear today. Say the colours.
- **Anchor**: What I wear today → The colours → My favourite clothes
- **Collocations**: I am wearing, a blue shirt, black shoes, my favourite colour
- **Phonetic**: Say the final "s" in "clothes" and "shoes" as a "z" sound.

## My Pet or My Favourite Animal
- **ID**: speaking-a1-12
- **Index**: 12
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about a pet or your favourite animal. Say what it looks like and what it does.
- **Anchor**: What animal it is → What it looks like → What it likes to do
- **Collocations**: I have a dog, it is small, it likes to eat, it is very cute
- **Phonetic**: Say the "d" at the end of "good" and "friend" with a quick touch of your tongue.

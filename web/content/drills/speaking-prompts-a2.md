# Speaking Drills: A2 Short Talks

## A Trip You Took
- **ID**: speaking-a2-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Talk about a trip you took. Where did you go, and what did you do?
- **Anchor**: Where and when you went → What you did there → Why you liked it
- **Collocations**: go on a trip, stay in a hotel, take photos, have a great time
- **Phonetic**: Past -ed endings: say 'visited' as 'VIZ-i-tid', but 'walked' as 'walkt' (one sound).

## My Best Friend
- **ID**: speaking-a2-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Talk about your best friend. What is he or she like?
- **Anchor**: Who the person is → What he or she looks like and is like → What you do together
- **Collocations**: have known someone for years, be good at, spend time with, get on well with
- **Phonetic**: Word stress: put the stress on the first part of 'FRIENDly' and 'FUNny'.

## Last Weekend
- **ID**: speaking-a2-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Tell me about what you did last weekend.
- **Anchor**: What you did on Saturday → What you did on Sunday → How you felt about it
- **Collocations**: stay at home, meet friends, go shopping, watch a film
- **Phonetic**: Past -ed endings: 'played' ends with a 'd' sound, 'cooked' ends with a 't' sound.

## A Shop You Like
- **ID**: speaking-a2-4
- **Index**: 4
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Talk about a shop you like. What do you buy there?
- **Anchor**: Where the shop is → What you buy there → Why you like it
- **Collocations**: be open until, a good price, try on clothes, pay by card
- **Phonetic**: Weak forms: say 'a cup of tea' as 'a CUP uv TEA'; 'of' is short and quiet.

## Plans for Next Week
- **ID**: speaking-a2-5
- **Index**: 5
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: What are you going to do next week? Talk about your plans.
- **Anchor**: Your plan for work or study → Your plan for the evenings → Your plan for the weekend
- **Collocations**: be going to, next week, have a meeting, visit my family
- **Phonetic**: Weak forms: 'going to' sounds like 'gonna' in fast speech, and 'to' is short.

## Advice for a Visitor
- **ID**: speaking-a2-6
- **Index**: 6
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: A friend wants to visit your town. Give some advice.
- **Anchor**: The best time to come → Places to see and food to try → Things to be careful about
- **Collocations**: you should, the best time to visit, try the local food, take an umbrella
- **Phonetic**: Word stress: say 'ADvice' with stress on the first part, and 'viSIT' on the second.

## My Daily Routine
- **ID**: speaking-a2-7
- **Index**: 7
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Describe a normal day in your life.
- **Anchor**: Morning routine → Work or study during the day → Evening and bedtime
- **Collocations**: get up early, have breakfast, go to work, go to bed
- **Phonetic**: Sentence stress: say the important words louder, like 'I GET UP at SIX'.

## My Home
- **ID**: speaking-a2-8
- **Index**: 8
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Describe your home. Which room do you like best?
- **Anchor**: Where you live and who lives with you → The rooms in your home → Your favourite room and why
- **Collocations**: live in a flat, share a room, next to, a big window
- **Phonetic**: Word stress: say 'BEDroom' and 'LIVing room' with stress on the first part.

## My Favourite Food
- **ID**: speaking-a2-9
- **Index**: 9
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Talk about your favourite food. How do you make it, or where do you eat it?
- **Anchor**: What the food is → What is in it or where you eat it → Why you love it
- **Collocations**: taste good, cook a meal, a lot of, too spicy
- **Phonetic**: Weak forms: say 'a lot of rice' as 'a LOT uv RICE'; the word 'of' is quiet.

## A Time You Were Ill
- **ID**: speaking-a2-11
- **Index**: 11
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Talk about a time you were ill. What happened?
- **Anchor**: What was wrong → What you did to feel better → How long it took
- **Collocations**: have a headache, see a doctor, take some medicine, feel better
- **Phonetic**: Past -ed endings: say 'needed' as 'NEE-did', but 'stayed' as 'stayd' (one sound).

## Weather and Seasons
- **ID**: speaking-a2-12
- **Index**: 12
- **Mode**: A2 Short Talk (60s)
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Talk about the weather in your country. What is your favourite season?
- **Anchor**: The weather in each season → Your favourite season → What you do in that season
- **Collocations**: be hot and sunny, rain a lot, in the summer, wear a coat
- **Phonetic**: Word stress: say 'WEAther' and 'SEAson' with stress on the first part.

# Writing Drills: A2 Short Paragraphs

## My Last Holiday
- **ID**: writing-a2-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: A2 Six-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Question**: Write about your last holiday. Where did you go and what did you do?
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: Last summer I went to the beach with my family.
- **M**: Last summer I went to the beach with my family.
- **E**: We stayed in a small hotel near the sea. Every day we swam and played games.
- **A**: The weather was hot, so we ate a lot of ice cream.
- **L**: I had a great time, and I want to go again.
- **Stylistic**: Use past simple verbs (went, stayed, ate) and the word 'so' to show a result.

## My Best Friend
- **ID**: writing-a2-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: A2 Six-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Question**: Write about your best friend. What is he or she like?
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: My best friend is called Minh, and he is very kind.
- **M**: My best friend is called Minh, and he is very kind.
- **E**: We met at school ten years ago. Now we work in the same city.
- **A**: He is funny, but he is sometimes late.
- **L**: I like him because he always listens to me.
- **Stylistic**: Use 'because' to give a reason and 'but' to show a small problem.

## An Email to a Friend
- **ID**: writing-a2-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: A2 Six-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Question**: Write an email to invite a friend to your birthday party.
- **Register**: Simple Formal / Email
- **Master Sentence**: I am writing to invite you to my birthday party.
- **M**: I am writing to invite you to my birthday party.
- **E**: It is on Saturday at six o'clock at my house.
- **A**: We are going to eat pizza and play music.

## A Normal Day
- **ID**: writing-a2-4
- **Index**: 4
- **Mode**: A2 Six-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Question**: Write about a normal day in your life.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: On weekdays I get up at six and go to work.
- **M**: On weekdays I get up at six and go to work.
- **E**: I have breakfast at home and take the bus. I start work at eight.
- **A**: After work I usually cook dinner and watch TV.
- **L**: I go to bed at ten because I am tired.
- **Stylistic**: Use time words (at six, after work) and 'usually' before the main verb.

## A Time I Felt Ill
- **ID**: writing-a2-10
- **Index**: 10
- **Mode**: A2 Six-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Question**: Write about a time you were ill.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: Last winter I was ill for three days.
- **M**: Last winter I was ill for three days.
- **E**: I had a headache and a bad cough. I stayed in bed and drank hot tea.

## A Short Note to a Neighbour
- **ID**: writing-a2-12
- **Index**: 12
- **Mode**: A2 Six-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Question**: Write a short note to your neighbour. Your music was too loud, and you want to say sorry.
- **Register**: Simple Formal / Email
- **Master Sentence**: I am sorry about the loud music last night.
- **M**: I am sorry about the loud music last night.
- **E**: I had a small party, and we forgot the time.

# CEFR Communicative Can-Do Action Scenarios (60 Scenarios)

## Hotel Front Desk Check-in & Key Card Activation
- **ID**: scen-a1-01
- **Index**: 1
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Mode**: SPOKEN_INTERACTION
- **Domain**: PUBLIC
- **Can-Do**: Can ask and answer simple questions about personal details and immediate transactional needs in a structured setting.
- **Interlocutor**: Hotel receptionist (polite, speaks slowly and clearly).
- **Context**: Arriving at an international hostel after a long flight; need to confirm booking and request Wi-Fi credentials.
- **Friction**: The booking name has a slight spelling typo in the hostel reservation database.
- **Outcome**: Spell surname aloud, confirm 3-night stay, receive room key card and written Wi-Fi password.
- **Time Condition**: 2 minutes interaction; 30s preparation.
- **Scaffold**: "Hello, I have a reservation under...", "My last name is spelled...", "Could I have the Wi-Fi code, please?"
- **Vietnamese**: Làm thủ tục nhận phòng khách sạn, đánh vần họ tên để giải quyết lỗi sai chính tả trong hệ thống và lấy mật khẩu Wi-Fi.

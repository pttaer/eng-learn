# Writing Drills: A1 Short Paragraphs

## About Me
- **ID**: writing-a1-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: A1 Four-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Question**: Write 4 sentences about yourself.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: My name is Lan and I am ten years old.
- **M**: My name is Lan and I am ten years old.
- **E**: I am from Vietnam and I live in Hanoi.
- **A**: I have a small dog and a big bag.
- **L**: I like my school and I like my friends.
- **Stylistic**: Start every sentence with a capital letter and end it with a full stop.

## My Family
- **ID**: writing-a1-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: A1 Four-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Question**: Write 4 sentences about your family.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: There are four people in my family.
- **M**: There are four people in my family.
- **E**: I have a mother, a father and a brother.
- **A**: My brother is twelve and he likes football.
- **L**: I love my family very much.
- **Stylistic**: Use "has" for he or she: "My father has a car."

## My Day
- **ID**: writing-a1-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: A1 Four-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Question**: Write 4 sentences about your day.
- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: I get up at six o'clock every day.
- **M**: I get up at six o'clock every day.
- **E**: I have breakfast and then I go to work.
- **A**: I eat lunch with my friends at twelve.
- **L**: In the evening, I cook dinner and go to bed.
- **Stylistic**: Use "then" and "in the evening" to show the order of your day.

## My Favourite Food

- **CEFR**: A1

## My Pet
- **ID**: writing-a1-12
- **Index**: 12
- **Mode**: A1 Four-Sentence Paragraph
- **CEFR**: A1

- **Register**: Informal / Personal
- **Master Sentence**: I have a small brown dog.

- **E**: His name is Bobo and he is two years old.

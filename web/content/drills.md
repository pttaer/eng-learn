# Speaking Drills: A1 Short Talks

## Introduce Yourself
- **ID**: speaking-a1-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Say hello and introduce yourself. Say your name, your age and where you are from.
- **Anchor**: My name and age → Where I am from → What I like
- **Collocations**: my name is, I am from, nice to meet you, I like
- **Phonetic**: Say the "th" in "this" and "thanks" with your tongue between your teeth.

## My Family
- **ID**: speaking-a1-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Talk about your family. Who is in your family? What are they like?
- **Anchor**: Who is in my family → Their names and ages → What I do with them
- **Collocations**: there are four people, my mother, my brother, we live together
- **Phonetic**: Say the final "s" in "parents" and "brothers" clearly.

## My Daily Routine
- **ID**: speaking-a1-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)
- **CEFR**: A1
- **Prompt**: Describe your day. What do you do in the morning, afternoon and evening?
- **Anchor**: Morning (get up, eat breakfast) → Afternoon (work or school) → Evening (dinner, sleep)
- **Collocations**: get up, have breakfast, go to work, go to bed
- **Phonetic**: Say the "s" at the end of "gets up" and "goes" when you talk about he or she.

## My Home
- **ID**: speaking-a1-4
- **Index**: 4
- **Mode**: A1 Short Talk (30s)

- **Collocations**: I live in, there is a, living room, my favourite room
- **Phonetic**: Say "there is" and "there are" slowly and clearly, with a soft "th" sound.

# Speaking Drills: B1 Opinion Talks

## A Memorable Experience
- **ID**: speaking-b1-1
- **Index**: 1
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Talk about a memorable experience you have had. Say what happened, who was with you and why you still remember it.
- **Anchor**: Set the scene (when and where) → Tell what happened → Say why it is special to you
- **Collocations**: unforgettable experience, take place, to be honest, looking back
- **Phonetic**: Stress the key words in each sentence, for example 'It was the BEST day of my LIFE', and say small words like 'the' and 'of' quickly.

## A Problem and How You Solved It
- **ID**: speaking-b1-2
- **Index**: 2
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: Describe a problem you had and explain how you solved it. What did you learn from it?
- **Anchor**: Explain the problem → Describe what you did to fix it → Say what you learned
- **Collocations**: deal with a problem, come up with a solution, ask for help, in the end
- **Phonetic**: Link words that join consonant to vowel, for example 'ask_for_help' sounds like 'as-kfor-help'.

## Advantages and Disadvantages of Social Media
- **ID**: speaking-b1-3
- **Index**: 3
- **Mode**: B1 Opinion Talk (90s)
- **CEFR**: B1
- **Prompt**: What are the advantages and disadvantages of social media? Give your own opinion.

- **Collocations**: keep in touch with, waste of time, on the other hand, in my opinion
- **Phonetic**: Use rising intonation on each item in a list and falling intonation on the last one: 'friends, news, photos and videos'.

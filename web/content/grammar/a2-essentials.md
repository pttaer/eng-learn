# Mode: A2 Essentials

## Past Simple: Regular Verb
- **ID**: gram-a2-1
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I walk to school every day.
- **Transformation**: I walked to school yesterday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add -ed to the verb to talk about the past.
- **Vietnamese**: Thì quá khứ đơn với động từ có quy tắc: thêm -ed vào động từ. Tiếng Việt không đổi động từ mà dùng từ như "đã" hoặc "hôm qua".
- **Formula**: `Subject + Verb-ed + Object`
- **Analysis**: The verb 'walk' becomes 'walked'. We use the past simple for a finished action.
- **Exemplar**: She cleaned the kitchen last night.

## Past Simple: Spelling of Regular Verbs
- **ID**: gram-a2-2
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: We study English and we plan a trip.
- **Transformation**: We studied English and we planned a trip.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Change y to ied, and double the last letter in short verbs.
- **Vietnamese**: Quy tắc chính tả khi thêm -ed: study đổi thành studied, plan gấp đôi phụ âm thành planned. Tiếng Việt không có quy tắc này.
- **Formula**: `consonant + y → ied; short verb: double the last consonant + ed`
- **Analysis**: 'Study' ends in consonant plus y, so it becomes 'studied'. 'Plan' is a short verb, so we write 'planned'.
- **Exemplar**: He stopped the car and carried the bags.

## Past Simple: Irregular Verb
- **ID**: gram-a2-3
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I go to the market and buy some fruit.
- **Transformation**: I went to the market and bought some fruit.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the special past form of irregular verbs.
- **Vietnamese**: Động từ bất quy tắc có dạng quá khứ riêng (go - went, buy - bought) và phải học thuộc. Tiếng Việt chỉ thêm "đã" nên không có dạng riêng này.
- **Formula**: `Subject + Past Form (irregular) + Object`
- **Analysis**: 'Go' becomes 'went' and 'buy' becomes 'bought'. These verbs do not take -ed.
- **Exemplar**: They ate rice and drank tea at lunch.

## Past Simple: Negative
- **ID**: gram-a2-4
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: She visited her aunt on Sunday.
- **Transformation**: She did not visit her aunt on Sunday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use did not and the base form of the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu phủ định quá khứ: did not (didn't) + động từ nguyên mẫu. Động từ trở về dạng gốc, không còn -ed. Tiếng Việt dùng "không" hoặc "chưa".
- **Formula**: `Subject + did not + Base Verb + Object`
- **Analysis**: 'Did not' shows the past, so the main verb stays in the base form. We say 'did not visit', not 'did not visited'.
- **Exemplar**: I did not see my friends last weekend.

## Past Simple: Yes/No Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-5
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: You watched a film last night.
- **Transformation**: Did you watch a film last night?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put did at the start and use the base form of the verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi Yes/No ở quá khứ: Did + chủ ngữ + động từ nguyên mẫu? Tiếng Việt thêm "không" hoặc "chưa" ở cuối câu, còn tiếng Anh đưa Did lên đầu.
- **Formula**: `Did + Subject + Base Verb + Object?`
- **Analysis**: 'Did' comes before the subject. The main verb goes back to the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Did he call you this morning?

## Past Simple: Wh- Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-6
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: He bought a new phone yesterday.
- **Transformation**: What did he buy yesterday?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put the question word first, then did, the subject and the base verb.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi có từ để hỏi ở quá khứ: Wh-word + did + chủ ngữ + động từ nguyên mẫu? Trong tiếng Việt từ để hỏi ("cái gì") thường đứng cuối câu, còn tiếng Anh đưa lên đầu.
- **Formula**: `Wh-word + did + Subject + Base Verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'What' replaces 'a new phone' and goes to the front. 'Did' stays before the subject, so 'buy' is in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Where did you go on holiday?

## Past Continuous
- **ID**: gram-a2-7
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I watch TV at eight o'clock.
- **Transformation**: I was watching TV at eight o'clock last night.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use was or were and the -ing form for an action in progress in the past.
- **Vietnamese**: Quá khứ tiếp diễn: was/were + V-ing, chỉ hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm trong quá khứ. Tiếng Việt dùng "đang" kèm "lúc đó".
- **Formula**: `Subject + was/were + Verb-ing`
- **Analysis**: We use 'was' with I, he, she and it. We use 'were' with you, we and they.
- **Exemplar**: They were playing football at four o'clock.

## Past Continuous with Past Simple
- **ID**: gram-a2-8
- **Mode**: A2_PAST_TENSES
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I cooked dinner. The phone rang.
- **Transformation**: I was cooking dinner when the phone rang.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use past continuous for the longer action and past simple for the short action.
- **Vietnamese**: Hành động dài đang diễn ra (was cooking) thì bị hành động ngắn chen vào (rang), nối bằng when. Giống cấu trúc "đang... thì..." trong tiếng Việt.
- **Formula**: `Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + when + Subject + Past Simple`
- **Analysis**: The long action is 'was cooking'. The short action is 'rang'.
- **Exemplar**: She was walking home when it started to rain.

## Future with Going To: Plan
- **ID**: gram-a2-9
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I visit my grandmother on Saturday.
- **Transformation**: I am going to visit my grandmother on Saturday.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use am, is or are + going to + base verb for a plan.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng be going to + động từ nguyên mẫu cho dự định đã có từ trước. Tương đương "định" hoặc "sẽ" trong tiếng Việt.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + going to + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: The plan was made before now. The verb after 'to' is in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: We are going to have a picnic tomorrow.

## Future with Going To: Negative
- **ID**: gram-a2-10
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: She is going to buy a new bag.
- **Transformation**: She is not going to buy a new bag.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put not after am, is or are.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định của be going to: thêm not sau am/is/are. Tiếng Việt dùng "không định" hoặc "sẽ không".
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: We keep 'going to' and add 'not' after 'is'. The main verb 'buy' does not change.
- **Exemplar**: They are not going to travel this summer.

## Future with Going To: Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-11
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: You are going to cook tonight.
- **Transformation**: Are you going to cook tonight?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Move am, is or are before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với be going to: đảo am/is/are lên trước chủ ngữ. Tiếng Việt thêm "không" ở cuối câu hoặc dùng "có... không".
- **Formula**: `Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to + Base Verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'Are' moves to the front of the sentence. The rest of the sentence stays the same.
- **Exemplar**: Is she going to come to the party?

## Future with Will: Decision or Offer
- **ID**: gram-a2-12
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I open the window.
- **Transformation**: I will open the window.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use will + base verb for a quick decision or an offer.
- **Vietnamese**: Dùng will + động từ nguyên mẫu khi quyết định ngay lúc nói hoặc đưa ra lời đề nghị. Dịch là "sẽ" hoặc "để tôi".
- **Formula**: `Subject + will + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Will' does not change for any subject. The verb after 'will' is always in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: It is hot, so I will turn on the fan.

## Future with Will: Negative
- **ID**: gram-a2-13
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: He will come to the meeting.
- **Transformation**: He will not come to the meeting.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put not after will. The short form is won't.
- **Vietnamese**: Phủ định với will: will not (won't) + động từ nguyên mẫu. Tiếng Việt dùng "sẽ không".
- **Formula**: `Subject + will not (won't) + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: We add 'not' after 'will'. The short form 'won't' is common in speaking.
- **Exemplar**: I won't be late for class.

## Future with Will: Question
- **ID**: gram-a2-14
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: It will rain tomorrow.
- **Transformation**: Will it rain tomorrow?
- **Grammatical Cue**: Put will before the subject.
- **Vietnamese**: Câu hỏi với will: đưa will lên trước chủ ngữ. Tiếng Việt thêm "không" ở cuối: "Ngày mai trời sẽ mưa không?"
- **Formula**: `Will + Subject + Base Verb?`
- **Analysis**: 'Will' moves to the front. The main verb stays in the base form.
- **Exemplar**: Will you help me with my homework?

## Present Continuous for Future Plans
- **ID**: gram-a2-15
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I meet my friend at six.
- **Transformation**: I am meeting my friend at six tonight.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use am, is or are + verb-ing with a future time for a fixed plan.
- **Vietnamese**: Hiện tại tiếp diễn dùng cho kế hoạch đã sắp xếp trong tương lai gần, thường có từ chỉ thời gian như "tonight" hoặc "tomorrow". Tiếng Việt dùng "sẽ" hoặc "định".
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + future time`
- **Analysis**: The word 'tonight' shows the future. The plan is fixed, for example with a time or a place.
- **Exemplar**: We are flying to Da Nang next week.

## Present Continuous: Now vs Future Plan
- **ID**: gram-a2-16
- **Mode**: A2_FUTURE_AND_CONTINUOUS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: He is cooking dinner now.
- **Transformation**: He is cooking dinner with his friends tomorrow.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Change the time word from now to a future time.
- **Vietnamese**: Cùng một cấu trúc is cooking, nhưng now chỉ việc đang xảy ra, còn tomorrow chỉ kế hoạch tương lai. Thời gian trong câu quyết định nghĩa.
- **Formula**: `Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + now (present) / tomorrow (future)`
- **Analysis**: With 'now', the action is happening at this moment. With 'tomorrow', it is a plan for the future.
- **Exemplar**: I am studying now, and I am working on Monday.

## Comparative: Short Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-17
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: My brother is tall. I am not very tall.
- **Transformation**: My brother is taller than me.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Add -er to a short adjective and use than.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh hơn với tính từ ngắn: adj-er + than. Tiếng Việt dùng "hơn": "cao hơn tôi".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + Adjective-er + than + Noun`
- **Analysis**: 'Tall' becomes 'taller'. We use 'than' to compare two things.
- **Exemplar**: This bag is cheaper than that bag.

## Comparative: Long Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-18
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: The train is fast. The bus is not fast.
- **Transformation**: The train is more comfortable than the bus.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use more before a long adjective, with no -er.
- **Vietnamese**: Với tính từ dài (từ hai âm tiết trở lên) dùng more + adj + than, không thêm -er. Tiếng Việt vẫn chỉ dùng "hơn".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + more + Adjective + than + Noun`
- **Analysis**: 'Comfortable' is a long adjective, so we use 'more'. We never say 'more comfortabler'.
- **Exemplar**: English is more interesting than maths for me.

## Comparative: Irregular and Spelling
- **ID**: gram-a2-19
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Today is good and hot.
- **Transformation**: Today is better, but yesterday was hotter.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Good becomes better, and short adjectives like hot double the last letter.
- **Vietnamese**: Một số tính từ đặc biệt: good - better, bad - worse. Tính từ ngắn như hot, big gấp đôi phụ âm cuối: hotter, bigger. Tiếng Việt không đổi hình thức từ.
- **Formula**: `good → better; bad → worse; consonant + vowel + consonant → double + er`
- **Analysis**: 'Good' has a special form, 'better'. 'Hot' becomes 'hotter' with a double t.
- **Exemplar**: My new phone is bigger and better than my old phone.

## Superlative: Short Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-20
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Mai is taller than her friends.
- **Transformation**: Mai is the tallest girl in her class.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the + adjective-est for the number one in a group.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh nhất với tính từ ngắn: the + adj-est + in/of + nhóm. Tiếng Việt dùng "nhất": "cao nhất lớp".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + the + Adjective-est + in/of + group`
- **Analysis**: 'Tall' becomes 'the tallest'. We use 'in' with a place or a group, for example 'in her class'.
- **Exemplar**: This is the cheapest shop in town.

## Superlative: Long Adjective
- **ID**: gram-a2-21
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: This film is more exciting than the other films.
- **Transformation**: This is the most exciting film of all.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use the most before a long adjective.
- **Vietnamese**: So sánh nhất với tính từ dài: the most + adj. Không nói "the excitingest". Tiếng Việt vẫn dùng "nhất".
- **Formula**: `Subject + be + the most + Adjective + Noun`
- **Analysis**: 'Exciting' is a long adjective, so we use 'the most'. We use 'of all' to mean 'compared with all the others'.
- **Exemplar**: She is the most helpful person in our team.

## Modal Can: Ability
- **ID**: gram-a2-22
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: I swim.
- **Transformation**: I can swim, but I cannot dance.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use can or cannot + base verb for ability.
- **Vietnamese**: Can + động từ nguyên mẫu chỉ khả năng ("có thể", "biết"). Phủ định là cannot (can't). Can không thêm -s và không cần trợ động từ.
- **Formula**: `Subject + can/cannot + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Can' stays the same for every subject, so we say 'she can swim'. We make a question with 'Can you swim?'.
- **Exemplar**: He can cook rice, but he cannot cook fish.

## Modal Should: Advice
- **ID**: gram-a2-23
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: You go to bed early.
- **Transformation**: You should go to bed early.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use should + base verb to give advice.
- **Vietnamese**: Should + động từ nguyên mẫu dùng để khuyên: "nên". Phủ định là should not (shouldn't) nghĩa là "không nên".
- **Formula**: `Subject + should/shouldn't + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Should' gives a friendly suggestion. It is not as strong as 'must'.
- **Exemplar**: You should drink more water when it is hot.

## Modal Must and Have To: Rules
- **ID**: gram-a2-24
- **Mode**: A2_COMPARISON_AND_MODALS
- **Level**: 1
- **CEFR**: A2
- **Prompt**: Students wear a uniform at school.
- **Transformation**: Students must wear a uniform at school.
- **Grammatical Cue**: Use must or have to for a rule or something necessary.
- **Vietnamese**: Must và have to đều có nghĩa "phải". Must là quy định mạnh, have to là điều cần thiết từ bên ngoài. Phủ định khác nhau: mustn't là "cấm", don't have to là "không cần".
- **Formula**: `Subject + must/have to + Base Verb`
- **Analysis**: 'Must' is strong and does not change. With 'he' or 'she', we say 'has to'.
- **Exemplar**: She has to get up early on Monday.

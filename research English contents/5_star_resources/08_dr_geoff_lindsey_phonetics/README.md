### TRAP vowel (Trad IPA: /æ/ -> Modern: /a/)
Modern British speakers pronounce TRAP as a fully open front vowel [a], not the tense, raised [æ] of 1950s BBC or General American.

### FOOT vs STRUT split
Crucial distinction for non-native speakers: /ʊ/ in 'put', 'book' is rounded and near-back; /ʌ/ in 'cut', 'strut' is open-mid central [ɐ].

### FLEECE vowel (Trad IPA: /iː/ -> Modern: /ɪj/)
The FLEECE vowel is not a static monophthong; it is phonetically a closing diphthong starting near [ɪ] and ending in high glide [j].

## 1. Core Philosophy: 'Simple is Best'
Simon's approach demystifies IELTS writing by discarding the myth of 'complex vocabulary'. High-scoring writing is characterized by precise collocation, natural phrasing, and bulletproof coherence.

## 2. Writing Task 2 Standard 4-Paragraph Formula

- **Paragraph 1: Introduction (2 sentences)**
  - Sentence 1: Paraphrase the question prompt.
  - Sentence 2: Clear thesis statement answering the prompt.
- **Paragraph 2: Main Body 1 (4–5 sentences)**
  - Sentence 1: Topic sentence introducing Idea 1.
  - Sentence 2: Explanation / Why.
  - Sentence 3: Concrete example.
  - Sentence 4: Concluding impact / result.
- **Paragraph 3: Main Body 2 (4–5 sentences)**
  - Sentence 1: Topic sentence introducing Idea 2.
  - Sentence 2: Explanation / Why.
  - Sentence 3: Concrete example.
  - Sentence 4: Concluding impact / result.
- **Paragraph 4: Conclusion (1–2 sentences)**
  - Paraphrase thesis and summarize the two main ideas.

## 3. Writing Task 1 4-Paragraph Blueprint

- **Para 1: Paraphrase** (Never copy question prompt verbatim).
- **Para 2: Overview** (Identify 2 major trends or contrasting extremes. **No numbers allowed!**).
- **Para 3 & 4: Specific Details** (Group data logically, compare figures, include units and time periods).

## 1. What makes SKELL Indispensable?
Unlike traditional dictionaries that list abstract definitions, SKELL extracts real-world collocations directly from a cleaned multi-billion-word English corpus. For any word, it reveals:
1. **Word Sketch:** What verbs take this noun as an object? What adverbs modify this verb?
2. **Similar Words:** Semantic neighbors that share syntactic distributions.
3. **Examples:** Full, natural sentences from academic and published prose.

## 2. Core Academic Word Sketches

### `analyze` (verb)
- **Typical Objects:** data, sample, result, trend, impact, relationship
- **Typical Subjects:** study, researcher, report, paper, author
- **Key Adverbs:** carefully, thoroughly, critically, statistically
- **Corpus Examples:**
  - *The study analyzed data collected from over 5,000 participants.*
  - *Researchers thoroughly analyzed the environmental impact of industrial waste.*

### `evidence` (noun)
- **Key Modifiers:** compelling, empirical, anecdotal, substantial, growing
- **Prepositional Patterns:** evidence of, evidence for, evidence against
- **Corpus Examples:**
  - *There is substantial empirical evidence to support the implementation of renewable energy policies.*
  - *Recent clinical trials provide compelling evidence for the efficacy of the treatment.*

### `demonstrate` (verb)
- **Typical Objects:** feasibility, efficacy, importance, need, correlation
- **Typical Subjects:** finding, experiment, evidence, figure, investigation
- **Key Adverbs:** clearly, convincingly, empirically, consistently
- **Corpus Examples:**
  - *The figures clearly demonstrate a sharp decline in manufacturing output.*
  - *Extensive research demonstrates the direct correlation between sleep deprivation and cognitive impairment.*

### `establish` (verb)
- **Typical Objects:** link, relationship, framework, criterion, consensus
- **Key Adverbs:** firmly, definitively, formally
- **Corpus Examples:**
  - *The commission was formed to establish clear regulatory guidelines.*
  - *Scientists have firmly established the link between carbon emissions and global temperature rise.*

### `contribute` (verb)
- **Typical Subjects:** factor, advancement, pollution, urbanization
- **Key Adverbs:** significantly, substantially, directly, greatly
- **Corpus Examples:**
  - *Rapid urbanization has contributed significantly to habitat fragmentation.*
  - *Technological advancements have contributed greatly to remote working capabilities.*

### `hypothesis` (noun)
- **Key Modifiers:** plausible, initial, underlying, compelling, testable
- **Corpus Examples:**
  - *Subsequent experiments failed to support the original hypothesis.*
  - *Researchers formulated a testable hypothesis based on preliminary observations.*

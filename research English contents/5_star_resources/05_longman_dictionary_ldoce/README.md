## 1. Why LDOCE Leads Academic English Preparation
LDOCE is renowned for its systematic coverage of complementation syntax. Where native speakers intuitively know whether a verb requires an infinitive or a gerund, English learners rely on LDOCE's explicit pattern codes.

## 2. Essential Grammar Patterns

### Pattern: `Verb + Object + from + -ing`
- **Verbs:** prevent, discourage, deter, prohibit, stop, dissuade
- **Example:** *Stricter laws help prevent companies from dumping untreated effluent into rivers.*
- **⚠️ Common Learner Error:** Saying *prevent companies to dump* (incorrect infinitival complement).

### Pattern: `Verb + -ing (Gerund only)`
- **Verbs:** consider, avoid, postpone, admit, suggest, delay
- **Example:** *The government is considering introducing carbon taxes on high-emission vehicles.*
- **⚠️ Common Learner Error:** Saying *considering to introduce*.

## 3. High-Band Collocation Boxes by IELTS Domain

### Domain: Education
- **Verbs with curriculum:** broaden the curriculum, standardize the curriculum, revise the curriculum
- **Nouns with academic:** academic performance, academic rigor, academic credentials, academic probation
- **Collocations with skills:** acquire skills, hone skills, practical skills, interpersonal skills

### Domain: Environment
- **Adjectives with pollution:** severe pollution, industrial pollution, untreated pollution, airborne pollutants
- **Verbs with resources:** deplete natural resources, conserve natural resources, exploit natural resources
- **Collocations with emissions:** curb emissions, slash emissions, greenhouse gas emissions, carbon footprint

### Domain: Technology
- **Verbs with technology:** embrace technology, harness technology, adopt cutting-edge technology
- **Collocations with artificial intelligence:** rapid development of AI, ethical implications of AI, automate routine tasks
- **Adjectives with advancement:** unprecedented advancement, groundbreaking discovery, exponential growth

## Overview
The Academic Word List (AWL) contains 570 headwords and their complete word families, divided into 10 sublists based on frequency across a 3.5-million-word corpus of academic texts. Sublist 1 contains the most frequent words; Sublist 10 contains the least frequent of the top 570.

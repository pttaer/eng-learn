## 1. Impromptu Speaking Frameworks (Table Topics)

### The P.R.E.P. Method
*Best for:* Opinion questions, policy evaluations, direct IELTS Speaking Part 3 prompts

- **Point:** State your core stance clearly and decisively in 1 sentence.
- **Reason:** Explain *why* you hold this stance using logical justification.
- **Example:** Illustrate with a vivid personal anecdote, historical precedent, or statistic.
- **Point:** Restate your main point with elevated vocabulary to seal the argument.

### The O.R.E.O. Method
*Best for:* Value judgements and moral / philosophical questions

- **Opinion:** Express your fundamental value judgement.
- **Reason:** Provide the ethical or pragmatic basis.
- **Explanation / Evidence:** Demonstrate the real-world consequences if this value is ignored.
- **Opinion:** Reaffirm your verdict.

### The Past - Present - Future Timeline
*Best for:* Questions about technology, social trends, work habits, urban living

- **Past:** A generation ago, how did society handle this issue?
- **Present:** What is the status quo today? What friction exists?
- **Future:** What will likely unfold over the next 20 years?

## 2. Eradicating Crutch Words: The Ah-Counter Protocol
Replace vocalized hesitations ('uhhh...') with intentional 1.5-second silence. Silence projects gravitas and gives the brain time to formulate the next grammatical clause.

## 3. High-Leverage Impromptu Questions (Mapped to IELTS Speaking Part 3)

### Artificial Intelligence & Ethics
> *"If artificial intelligence can outperform human doctors, should algorithms have the legal authority to diagnose patients without human oversight?"*

### Urban Living & Solitude
> *"Despite living in densely populated modern cities, why are more individuals experiencing chronic isolation than previous generations?"*

### Higher Education vs Vocational Training
> *"Does the traditional 4-year university degree still provide adequate return on investment in today's rapidly evolving global economy?"*

### Environmental Stewardship
> *"Should individual consumer choices or governmental industrial mandates bear the primary responsibility for reversing climate change?"*

### Preservation of Cultural Heritage
> *"In an increasingly homogenised global culture, is it essential for nation-states to spend tax revenue preserving obsolete dialects and traditions?"*

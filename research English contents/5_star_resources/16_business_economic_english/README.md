# Business & Economic English (The Economist & Harvard Business Review)

## 1. The Economist Style Commandments (George Orwell's Rules Adapted)

### 1. Never use a metaphor, simile, or other figure of speech which you are used to seeing in print.
*Clichés dull the mind and conceal lack of original thought.*

### 2. Never use a long word where a short one will do.
*Clarity is power. Prefer 'use' over 'utilize', 'end' over 'terminate'.*

### 3. If it is possible to cut a word out, always cut it out.
*Economic prose must be muscular and lean.*

### 4. Never use the passive where you can use the active.
*Active verbs identify accountability and establish causality.*

### 5. Never use a foreign phrase, scientific word, or jargon if you can think of an everyday English equivalent.
*Jargon alienates intelligent generalist decision-makers.*

## 2. High-Yield Macroeconomic & Strategic Terminology

| Term | Typical Collocation | Context Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **fiscal austerity** | *enact / impose fiscal austerity* | Government policies to reduce budget deficits through spending cuts or tax hikes. |
| **supply chain bottlenecks** | *alleviate supply chain bottlenecks* | Congestion points that impede the smooth flow of goods from manufacture to retail. |
| **inflationary pressures** | *curb / exacerbate inflationary pressures* | Factors causing rising price levels across a national economy. |
| **market penetration** | *achieve rapid market penetration* | The degree to which a product or service is adopted by target consumers. |
| **regulatory headwinds** | *encounter severe regulatory headwinds* | Legal and governmental compliance constraints that hinder corporate expansion. |
| **disruptive innovation** | *pioneer disruptive innovation* | Innovations that fundamentally alter how an entire industry operates. |

## 3. Harvard Business Review Case Study Analytical Blueprint

- **Section 1 Executive Summary:** Concise 3-sentence synthesis stating the core strategic dilemma, the root cause, and the recommended intervention.
- **Section 2 Strategic Diagnosis:** Evaluating competitive positioning, macroeconomic constraints, and internal operational bottlenecks.
- **Section 3 Alternative Options:** Contrasting two mutually exclusive courses of action with explicit risk-benefit trade-offs.
- **Section 4 Implementation Roadmap:** Milestone scheduling, resource allocation, and key performance indicators (KPIs).

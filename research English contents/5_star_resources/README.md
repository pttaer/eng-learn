## How to Utilize this Local Knowledge Base

1. **For App Developers & Engine Integration:**
   - All structured data is preserved in clean `.json` files inside each directory, ready for immediate parsing by TypeScript/Python engines.
2. **For Anki Flashcard Learners:**
   - Flashcards in `15_anki_srs_ecosystem/anki_import_ready_awl_cloze.tsv` can be imported directly into Anki with 1 click.
3. **For Essay Writers & IELTS Candidates:**
   - Consult `03_manchester_academic_phrasebank/` for discourse stems, `02_ielts_simon/` for essay planning, and `07_purdue_owl/` for thesis formulation.
4. **For Pronunciation & Speaking Trainees:**
   - Compare Modern British RP in `08_dr_geoff_lindsey_phonetics/` with General American in `09_rachels_english/`, and practice impromptu speaking with `14_toastmasters_international/`.

| Module | Timing | Format | Key Skills Tested |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listening** | 30 mins (+10 mins transfer on paper) | 4 sections, 40 questions | Monologue, dialogue, academic lecture comprehension |
| **Reading** | 60 mins | 3 passages, 40 questions | Skimming, scanning, detailed argument analysis |
| **Writing** | 60 mins | Task 1 (150 words) + Task 2 (250 words) | Data reporting & Academic essay argument synthesis |
| **Speaking** | 11 – 14 mins | 3 parts | Fluency, lexical resource, grammatical range, phonology |

## 3. Question Types Taxonomy & Defense Against Distractors

### Matching Headings
- **Target Skill:** Grasping central paragraph theme (Skimming & main idea identification)
- **Rules:** Headings outnumber paragraphs; headings express the essence, not supporting details
- **Common Traps:** Keywords from distractor headings appear as incidental mentions inside the paragraph
- **Recommended Strategy:** Read paragraph first, formulate personal 1-sentence summary, then match with heading options.

### True / False / Not Given
- **Target Skill:** Factual verification against passage claims
- **Common Traps:** Confusing 'False' with 'Not Given'. If statement mentions an unmentioned superlative ('the most important factor'), it is Not Given, not False.

### Yes / No / Not Given
- **Target Skill:** Identifying writer's views, claims, or attitude

### Summary Completion with Word Bank
- **Target Skill:** Synthesizing paraphrase and identifying parts of speech
- **Recommended Strategy:** Identify required part of speech (noun, verb, adjective) and grammar agreement before scanning word box.

### Matching Information to Paragraphs
- **Target Skill:** Locating specific details across the whole passage (Scanning)
- **Rules:** Paragraph letters may be used more than once if 'NB: You may use any letter more than once' appears.

### Multiple Choice (Single & Double Answer)
- **Target Skill:** Differentiating main assertions from distractor details
- **Rules:** Distractors often quote verbatim phrases from the text but reverse polarity or add extreme qualifiers.

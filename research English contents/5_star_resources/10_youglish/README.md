## 1. Power Query Syntax Cheat Sheet

| Operator | Description | Practical Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `:us, :uk, :aus` | Filter results exclusively by accent region | `schedule :uk vs schedule :us` |
| `:m, :f` | Filter by male or female speaker | `entrepreneurship :f` |
| `"phrase in quotes"` | Search exact multi-word chunks and collocations | `"in terms of" :us` |
| `#topic` | Filter by context/genre hashtag (e.g. #business, #science, #medical) | `hypothesis #science` |

## 2. The 4-Stage Shadowing Protocol
### Stage 1: Comprehension & Audio Calibration (Speed 0.75x)
Listen to the YouGlish clip without speaking. Read the subtitles to ensure 100% semantic comprehension and note where the speaker pauses and breathes.

### Stage 2: Delayed Shadowing with Subtitles (Speed 1.0x)
Play the clip at normal speed. Speak out loud approximately 1–2 words behind the speaker, mimicking the exact pitch contour and word reductions.

### Stage 3: Simultaneous Shadowing without Subtitles
Close your eyes or look away from the text. Speak at the exact same millisecond as the native speaker, locking into rhythm and chest resonance.

### Stage 4: Recording & Acoustic Comparison
Record your own voice speaking the sentence. Play your recording alongside the YouGlish native clip to pinpoint dropped consonant clusters or pitch errors.

## 3. Computer-Delivered IELTS Mock Simulator Breakdown

### 3.1 Listening Module (40 Items / 30 Minutes + 2 Min Review)
- **Format**: CD-IELTS interface with high-contrast audio waveforms, synchronized timestamp tracking, and single-play constraint.
- **Section 1 (Social Dialogue)**: Everyday transactional context (e.g., booking car rental, gym membership). Focus: numbers, names, addresses, price, dates.
- **Section 2 (Social Monologue)**: Public service / community context (e.g., museum audio tour, volunteer orientation). Focus: maps, flowchart labeling, multiple choice.
- **Section 3 (Academic Dialogue)**: 2–4 speakers in higher education (e.g., tutor and students discussing research methodology). Focus: matching speaker opinions, table completion.
- **Section 4 (Academic Lecture)**: Uninterrupted monologic university lecture (e.g., marine biomimicry, macroeconomic inflation history). Focus: dense note completion, signpost recognition.

### 3.2 Reading Module (40 Items / 60 Minutes)
- **Academic Stream (3 Long Passages, 2,150–2,750 words total)**:
  * *Passage 1*: Descriptive/Factual (Natural sciences / anthropology). Focus: True/False/Not Given, Flowchart.
  * *Passage 2*: Discursive/Analytical (Behavioral psychology / industrial design). Focus: Heading matching, Summary completion.
  * *Passage 3*: Complex Argumentative/Philosophical (Ethics / epistemological history). Focus: Yes/No/Not Given, Multiple choice.
- **General Training Stream (3 Sections, workplace & survival focus)**:
  * *Section 1*: Social Survival (Timetables, notices, housing agreements).
  * *Section 2*: Workplace Training (Employee manuals, contract terms, health & safety).
  * *Section 3*: General Reading (Extended descriptive narrative).
- **Interface Features**: Split-screen resizable panes, in-text word counter, text highlighter, note-taking popup, and instant question navigation strip (1–40).

### 3.3 Writing Module (2 Tasks / 60 Minutes)
- **Task 1 (Academic, 150 words, 20 min)**:
  * Graphical data deconstruction: Bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, tables, process diagrams, and dual geographic maps.
  * Required architecture: Overview statement (mandatory for Band 7+), 2 key feature grouping paragraphs, exact quantitative comparisons without opinion.
- **Task 1 (General Training, 150 words, 20 min)**:
  * Functional correspondence: Formal complaint, semi-formal colleague request, informal host thank-you.
  * Focus: Register consistency, bullet-point fulfillment, opening/closing conventions.
- **Task 2 (Both Streams, 250 words, 40 min)**:
  * 5 Core Essay Archetypes:
    1. *Opinion (Agree/Disagree)*: Clear thesis statement sustained throughout.
    2. *Discussion (Discuss both views and give your opinion)*: Balanced objective evaluation before personal verdict.
    3. *Advantages vs Disadvantages (Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?)*: Proportional comparative weighting.
    4. *Problem & Solution / Cause & Effect*: Feasible, non-superficial root cause remediation.
    5. *Two-Part Question*: Dual-prompt direct address without omitted criteria.
- **Automated 4-Criterion Heuristic Evaluation Strip**:
  * **TR/TA**: Word count verification, prompt coverage scanner, thesis detection.
  * **CC**: Paragraph balance check, referential pronoun tracker, transition density auditor.
  * **LR**: AWL lexical density %, collocation frequency score, repetition penalty.
  * **GRA**: Sentence complexity index, clause coordination/subordination ratio, tense accuracy check.

### 3.4 Speaking Module (3 Parts / 11–14 Minutes)
- **Part 1 (Introduction & Interview, 4–5 min)**:
  * 3 Topic sets (Home/Work/Study + 2 familiar topics like Leisure, Transport, Daily routines).
  * Automated audio recorder with cadence timer (20–35s per turn target).
- **Part 2 (Individual Long Turn, 3–4 min)**:
  * Candidate task card with 4 sub-prompts.
  * 60s silent preparation timer with scratchpad notepad.
  * 2-minute uninterrupted speech recorder with real-time acoustic pitch and tempo telemetry.
- **Part 3 (Two-Way Abstract Discussion, 4–5 min)**:
  * Thematic extension of Part 2 into societal, philosophical, and policy abstractions.
  * Conversational partner pushes follow-up counter-questions: *"Some argue the opposite; how would you respond?"*

- **Epistemic Modality (25%)**: Precise deployment of hedges (*would appear to indicate, tentatively suggests, cannot be discounted*).
- **Stress & Pause Mastery (25%)**: Prosodic nuclear accent placement to signal contrastive focus without aggressive pitch spikes.

## 5. Vietnamese L1 Interference Remediation in IELTS

| IELTS Criterion | Vietnamese Transfer Error | Root Cause in L1 | Remediation Drill / Sieve |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task Response (TR)** | Circular argumentation; delayed thesis statement | Topic-comment discourse structure (*dẫn dắt lòng vòng* before main point) | **Linear SVO Thesis Engine**: First sentence of Intro must take unequivocal stance. |
| **Coherence & Cohesion (CC)** | Starting every sentence with *Moreover, Furthermore, In addition* | Lack of referential cohesion in L1; treating connectors as glue | **Lexical Chaining Drill**: Replace mechanical linkers with demonstratives (*This approach, Such disparities*). |
| **Lexical Resource (LR)** | Thesaurus stuffing: *"a double-edged sword"*, *"every coin has two sides"* | Rote memorization of clichéd idioms from outdated prep books | **AWL & Academic Collocation Sieve**: Ban clichés; reward high-utility academic collocations. |
| **Grammatical Range (GRA)** | Run-on sentences; comma splices; dropped `-ed` / `-s` endings | Paratactic sentence linking; absence of inflectional codas in L1 | **Subordination Matrix & Catenation Bridge**: Train semicolon + however, and audible coda linking. |
